## R4 requirements preserved

- **Resolution is mandatory for real branches.** A provisional effect
  cannot be left indefinitely owned with no declared settlement,
  supersession, timeout, rejection, retirement or recovery continuation.
  The application need not implement impossible branches, but must
  justify why a reachable branch is excluded.
- **Local retirement differs from historical command disposition.**
  Ending E1, hiding an overlay or missing a response cannot establish
  that C1 was rejected. A safe presentation can recover from an
  authorized reread while original command attribution is still unknown.
- **Authoritative installation outranks obsolete provisional ownership.**
  A late owner callback cannot restore a captured older DOM, erase
  adopted content, or make a provisional remnant authoritative.
  Actual owner and target incarnation matter.
- **Multiple provisional views do not fork the authoritative domain.**
  Alice and Bob may present incompatible pending helpers for Request R;
  only the protected model determines final Claim/assignment outcomes.
- **Optimistic resources remain owner-relative.** Physical cleanup is
  distinct from immediate semantic retirement. The later command
  resolver may run under newly admitted rights without reviving old
  timers, transport correlations or DOM rights.
- **No silent downgrade to naked HTMX.** Missing required managed
  semantics fail closed; explicitly authored raw HTMX and complete
  non-optimistic modes remain valid positive choices.
- **Model/Choreo/view separation.** Business outcomes belong to the
  model; choreography controls provisional and resolution semantics;
  views choose visual treatment. A visual helper cannot invent grants
  or authoritative outcomes.

## Specific R5.1 improvements

1. **Resolution ledger:** every reachable terminal/interruption path
   accounts for independent command attribution, acquisition/adoption,
   physical effects and resource handoffs. Semantic retirement does not
   prove physical cleanup or canonical replacement of provisional DOM.
2. **Dependency-safe overlap:** conflicts are detected at declared
   read/write, field, query-membership, installation and local-draft
   scopes, not only DOM element IDs. The admitted strategy owns
   arbitration and successor generations.
3. **C1/C2 race handling:** distinguishes an unsent dependent draft
   from an already-submitted C2. Removing/rebasing an overlay never
   cancels an in-flight authoritative command or changes its arguments.
   Rejection/unknown of C1 cannot fabricate C2's disposition.
4. **Application-defined strategy:** stays ordinary typed
   Clojure/ClojureScript; explicit declared result includes
   pending command, resource and recovery obligations. Opaque
   behavior remains a premise, not a trusted authority or proof.
5. **Per-target physical installation:** only qualified actual target
   completion advances its own covered adopted scope. A multi-target
   atomicity claim needs a genuine all-or-nothing mechanism or
   declared compensation and exposed intermediate states. Newer
   local card/membership and Refresh debt cannot be erased incidentally.
6. **No-downgrade physical gate:** incomplete managed requests reject
   before irreversible send. A current HTMX bridge's synchronous
   projection limit is implementation evidence, not a permanent
   prohibition on supported asynchronous transports or un-managed
   HTMX.
7. **Five positive/adverse controls:** retirement/reacquisition,
   dependent unsent versus submitted C2, overlapping scopes,
   partial multi-target installation, and legitimate
   non-optimistic/raw HTMX versus forbidden managed fallback.
