## Independent editorial conclusion

Retain R5's single source-owned choreography and portable
executable semantic core. R4's broad Choreo and browser safety
requirements remain, but they must be expressed as *distinct
checkable joins*: original domain operation, global action,
role-local knowledge, projected machine, canonical plan,
adapter transition, issued effect, actual HTMX/DOM installation,
model authority and optionally a proof/physical witness.
One observed edge cannot simply impersonate the next.

For choreography, a global abstract value is not participant
knowledge. A source-compatible send does not itself prove
receive, authoritative causality, current trusted basis,
permission to disclose private fields or global termination.
Behavior-sensitive branches, owner-local knowledge and
required/optional payload semantics are retained explicitly.
R4's portability target is preserved without claiming that
equal normalized event streams establish network timing,
browser effects or actual XTDB/host refinement.

For the browser, the pure adapter remains the policy owner and
the narrow shell realizes abstract effects. A callback may be
semantically invalid *after* it was scheduled and therefore
must recheck permission at the final protected mutation.
Retirement revokes old effect rights before physical cleanup,
while pending historical commands and separately owned
subscription state may survive under named contracts. Real
HTMX request/swap/OOB/settle is a physical dependency, not an
invented lifecycle function exposed as a fictional framework API.

HumanHelp's product choice is decisive: remote changes are
advisory until the explicitly granted one-use Refresh (or
one of the separately declared, narrowly authorized local
exceptions). Even a successful browser swap does not make
an unauthorized remote collection adoption permissible.
Likewise current Request truth is not proof C42 incorporated.
Partial installation, missing notices, old clients and
incorrect sidecar evidence must retain their respective
unresolved obligations.

## Bounded Gesso source correspondence

These are real source files in `application-assurance` v1622,
inspected through GitHub at the pinned branch; the claims
are intentionally limited to what their namespaces state.

- `src/gesso/choreo/semantics.cljc` specifies the supported
  global `:local`, `:authoritative`, `:branch`,
  `:communicate`, `:await` and `:return` operations,
  with global value flow, closed semantic outputs and
  a defined distributed-observation projection. Its
  global value store explicitly is *not* actor knowledge.
- `src/gesso/choreo/verify.cljc` checks source-level
  shape, reachability, definite value and role knowledge,
  as well as definite authoritative classification,
  but expressly disclaims authentication, arbitrary
  liveness, physical realization or general refinement.
- `src/gesso/choreo/knowledge.cljc` records role-local
  provenance (input, communicated, authoritative,
  derived or asserted), separating assertion from
  granted authority. Authoritative basis changes
  require a qualified external progression witness.
- `src/gesso/choreo/project.cljc` projects into one
  canonical ExecutablePlan, rejects some foreign
  branch/causality and receive ambiguity cases, and
  records source/proof mapping separately from
  runtime locators. It does not claim its existence
  proves all refinement or browser effects.
- `src/gesso/live/browser/adapter.cljc` defines a
  pure `step` that returns next state plus abstract
  effects, with execution, fragment, continuity,
  HTMX and optimistic generation rules. Its abstract
  basis ordering is not provider authority.
- `src/gesso/live/browser/shell.cljs` translates
  abstract effects and normalized completion events
  while owning opaque physical timers, transport,
  continuity and optimistic resources; it does not
  own a second semantic machine or HTMX lifecycle.
- `src/gesso/live/browser/dom.cljs` restricts itself
  to physical DOM primitives and leaves knowledge,
  canonical adoption and rollback policy outside
  its implementation authority.
- `src/gesso/live/browser/optimistic.cljs` realizes
  physical optimism subordinate to the adapter,
  avoiding resurrection of old provisional DOM.
- `src/gesso/live/browser/refinement.cljc` maps
  role-local adapter observations conservatively.
  Its explicitly named outstanding sender,
  delivery, global-schedule and terminal witnesses
  prevent a local receive from impersonating an
  authenticated global Choreo trace.
- `src/gesso/live/browser/continuity.cljs` owns
  local capture/restore and deliberately no
  global target lock or source of business truth.

The source shows meaningful typed/protocol machinery,
not an independently proven complete Gesso system.
Neither a published namespace nor an earlier test
fixture proves the source-to-actual-browser adoption
chain, general refinement soundness, all async
interleavings or full product behavior. No fresh
Chromium/Node/JVM/HTMX/XTDB tests were executed for
this editorial revision.
