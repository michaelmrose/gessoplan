*** Gate I -- Representation does not confer admission

A represented value is not an admitted semantic subject or privileged
capability merely because it has the expected tag, nominal identifier, map
keys, opaque-looking bytes, source digest, certificate-shaped fields or
serialized form. Every elevation of status belongs to the responsible
*actual* boundary: original-source type/semantic checking, trusted
model/principal acquisition, canonical command-history admission,
issuer-controlled proof checking, current resource/effect admission or
qualified physical persistence and installation.

Distinguish at least the following from each other:

- original model rule or operation declaration, checked semantic term,
  generated plan and actually issued executable callback;
- proof candidate/report, source-rechecked in-process proof handle,
  independent checked theorem artifact and transferred production proof;
- browser claim/role/correlation token, currently authenticated actor
  and authoritatively verified permission to invoke or disclose;
- command ID and canonical immutable binding, physical attempt,
  retained original-command result and current authorized resolution;
- proposed basis/progression witness, trusted provider-qualified
  observation and authorized physically adopted fragment;
- local function/plan object, installed host binding and actual
  protected backend or DOM effect.

A versioned decoder may reject malformed input without thereby proving
that all well-shaped data is trusted. A JVM-local checked handle
prevents ordinary copy-and-paste substitution inside its admitted
scope; it is not a hostile-JVM sandbox, transferable capability,
signature or runtime authorization. Importing a durable report or
proof package requires fresh checked source/issuer and applicable
premise admission; one printable field cannot confer a stronger
evidence class. An adversary supplying their own compatible-looking
graph does not establish the required original-source dependency
closure of the intended application.

*Positive/adverse oracle:* an actually issued Claim executor and a
current original-source proof handle are accepted for the exact
supported subjects; a copied EDN certificate/report, forged role,
replayed stale resource generation, mismatched C42 binding or
browser-supplied provider witness cannot cross the corresponding
trusted admission boundary. The eligible positive must remain
usable after rejecting its counterfeit.

*** Gate J -- Capability completeness is independent of closure

The required product and original assurance inventories are
*independent inputs*, not whatever the implementation currently
registers, compiles, projects, tests or proves. A closed Choreo
graph, source-derived report, finite operation catalog or
internally consistent proof DAG can certify its explicitly
smaller declared subject without satisfying the original
stronger application or HumanHelp completion claim.
The generator may not define its own denominator by
discarding difficult capabilities, positive cases,
external writers, invariants or dynamic recovery paths.

For HumanHelp, the independent positive product set
includes Create; Claim, Unclaim, Mark on the way,
Complete, Cancel and manager-target Reassign;
search/order/options/details/continuity on the
authorized adopted cohort; current authentication/
Membership/Organization policy; multi-client
advisory-only remote lifecycle; one-use explicit
Refresh; eligible unknown-command recovery; and
the separately selected Response interaction.
The fourteen independent Part VIII gates also
include real production-model authority,
installed Biff/XTDB/HTMX paths, physical
assets, true browser acceptance and failures.
The Response decision remains product-owned
and *unselected* until its authorized
requirements are chosen and implemented.
A placeholder, deliberately disabled control
or missing registry entry does not count
as a completed positive.

A genuinely narrower selected Claim pilot,
external S0/S1 theorem or bounded prototype
may pass its *named* partial profile; it
must not relabel that profile as complete
HumanHelp, all-writer closure, external
formal transfer or multi-node production.
The original requested release profile
and requirements are fixed before
evaluation. An unsupported or missing
mandatory gate cannot be silently
made inapplicable after it fails.

*Positive/adverse oracle:* all required
original product actions have genuine
eligible paths and their separate negative
cases before a full operational claim
passes; removing Reassign from the
exposed registry and the checker
catalog, or omitting Response and
its positive oracle, does not make
the full requirement set smaller.
A legitimate partial Claim release
must be reported as partial rather
than refused as if no useful
scoped work were possible.

*** Gate K -- Incorporation, delivery, and knowledge stay distinct

The authoritative database's actual
incorporation, a command's durable
historical attribution, an HTTP attempt's
delivery/return, a subscriber's advisory
knowledge, the user's permitted
authoritative read and the browser's
adopted presentation are separate
facts and evidence subjects. A
connection timeout, abort, host loss,
retired execution or absent immediate
response does *not* prove a possibly
submitted command was rejected.
A known committed operation stays
committed after postcommit render,
publication or transport failure.

One logical command C42 retains
its immutable principal/operation/
target/arguments binding across
compatible retries and multiple
physical attempts. The trusted
resolver checks its exact retained
historical result and *current*
permission to disclose that result
before using a fresh operation's
eligibility conditions. A Claim may
be historically incorporated even
when Request R is no longer open
for new Claim. Reusing C42 for
different actors/arguments is a
typed incompatibility; creating
C43 is a different intent, not
automatic safe replay of C42.
Failure of A1 does not exclude
incorporation by concurrent A2.

An authorized read of current R can
repair a stale optimistic card
without proving which previous
logical command produced that state.
A missing, unreadable, expired
or insufficiently progressed
command-result record must not
be replaced by a guess from the
card, request status, basis token
or a timeout. The selected
resolve/retry/fence protocol
preserves =:commit-unknown=
until valid command-specific
evidence establishes a compatible
original outcome or its qualified
exclusion. A local result receipt
cannot authenticate all other hosts'
physical attempts.

*Positive/adverse oracle:* a real
C42 Claim incorporates, loses its
HTTP response and later permits
historical =:already-incorporated=
resolution under current disclosure,
without a duplicate primary
RequestAssignment. A newer
post-Reassign card or A1 abort
does not falsely prove that
C42 succeeded or failed.
A fresh eligible command still
has a valid permitted success.

*** Gate L -- Recovery preserves scope and adoption permission

An advisory is a scoped statement
that a relevant authoritative
observation may need refreshing;
it is not an authority token,
permission to issue a protected
read, permission to replace a
collection or guarantee of
physical installation. Source,
principal, Organization/Location,
actual applied query, fragment,
target/resource owner, provider
basis and required coverage
remain bound through retry,
reconnect, failover and release
retirement. Unrelated changes
and unresolved obligations must
not disappear because one newer
or locally successful result
was installed.

Under the selected HumanHelp remote policy, Bob's other-user Create and
lifecycle changes, including changes to cards he already sees, remain
advisory until he explicitly grants a scoped one-use Refresh. Its default
grant permits **one logical authorized acquisition with one physical read
budget and no automatic successor**. A separate selected recovery policy
may authorize a bounded retry under its actual remaining physical budget,
or a new explicit user Refresh/Retry may issue a new grant; an SSE notice,
failed attempt, queued intent or reconnect cannot authorize another
physical read by itself.

A new notice during the granted acquisition may be covered by the
already authorized observation only when the trusted provider establishes
its progression, scope and coverage and the browser finishes every
required actual HTMX/OOB installation. An insufficient or incomparable
basis, missing target or partial swap retains the uncovered debt; it
cannot create a successor grant or silently widen the original query.

A permitted manual Refresh
and initial acquisition remain
available even when no notice
arrived or no interest was
registered in time. A
subscription reconnect,
routing filter hit, local
Create exception, unrelated
own-command settlement or
new plan generation cannot
implicitly grant Bob remote
collection adoption.
Creator-local accepted
Create and a narrowly
correlated authorized
own-command reread are
separate selected scopes.
Revocation blocks future
protected disclosures and
effects at the current
boundary; already disclosed
offline data cannot be
retroactively erased.

An authoritative absence
requires a qualified *negative*
observation under the correct
query, principal, disclosure
and basis, not simply an
absent DOM node, local filter
or failed network request.

*Positive/adverse oracle:* Bob
can choose one authorized Refresh
without a preceding SSE event,
and the complete installed
result discharges only covered
debt. An O2 advisory arriving
during A1 cannot issue an
ungranted A2; a lost notice,
unmatched OOB target or
recycled DOM resource cannot
authorize remote content or
declare false freshness.

*** Gate M -- Evidence retains its scope through composition

Each assurance conclusion keeps
its original proposition, normalized
semantic subject, actor/operation/
state scope, premises used,
evidence and checker class,
quantification domain, relevant
version/implementation/host
generation and actual observation
window. Source completeness is
checked against the *independently
required* original obligation
manifest, not inferred from the
elaborator's candidate graph.
Copied identity fields, a
replayed successful report or
matching source hash do not
establish that the current
issuer, installed host, physical
writer or browser was admitted.

Maintain distinct strength for:
a propositional derivation,
guarded abstract transition,
conditional induction,
exact finite exhaustion within
its declared state/input domain,
bounded test, externally checked
theorem, source/assembly
preflight, scoped actual
physical observation and
implementation refinement.
A genuine S0/S1 soundness
theorem need not be dismissed
because S2 production checker,
S3 typed atoms or S4
issuer-to-physical transfer
are open; it equally may
not impersonate those
missing claims. Tests that
exercise one physical
schedule do not prove
unbounded scheduling,
transaction-internal
prestate or all-writer
closure. A proven abstract
theorem may survive an
unrelated source change
while old installed
physical evidence becomes
stale.

The four Part XI release
decisions—candidate,
installation, activation,
completion—keep separate
subjects. The four
independent operational,
compositional, external
formal and multi-node
milestones keep separate
denominators. The selected
profile's required results
are recorded using the
complete declared gate
vocabulary: passed, failed,
incomplete, unavailable,
unsupported, skipped,
stale and missing. A
reported =:passed= without
authenticity/applicability
checking is still not
accepted evidence.

*Positive/adverse oracle:* a
qualified unchanged abstract
Claim theorem is reused under
an actual valid dependency
relation, while the changed
XTDB adapter and emitted
browser asset receive new
physical checks. A copied
certificate map, finite
truth-table result,
unexecuted browser test,
narrow proof graph or
receipt from an older host
cannot make a stronger
current release green.

*** Gate N -- Policy visibility has an honest inspection boundary

Correctness-relevant application
semantics are inspectable as
original source-owned public
rules, operations, identities,
actor/relationship/disclosure
constraints, guarded effects,
recovery, lifecycle invariants,
required capability inventory,
dependency graph and explicitly
named opaque or external
conformance premises. The
audit and explanatory report
are *read-only derived views*
of the same construction
and supplied evidence, not
a second business policy
registry, separate runtime
interpreter or new admission
issuer. Real domain
complexity is not a defect;
no arbitrary ratio of
policy lines to total
implementation is an
acceptance criterion.

Inspection states precisely
which Clojure rule bodies,
callbacks, model predicates,
FX, writer paths,
administrative operations,
startup/replacement handlers
and protected acquisition
points were actually
examined. A condition
influencing authorization,
eligibility, disclosure,
command acceptance,
relationship uniqueness
or observable lifecycle
cannot appear mechanically
verified merely because
some other part of the
application graph is
closed. Where Gesso cannot
inspect the relevant host
code, require an original
source-owned typed/effect/
dependency contract and a
named implementation-
conformance premise, or
refuse a stronger selected
profile that requires
its discharge.

A known opaque branch
must be visible at the
first boundary capable
of identifying its influence;
unexamined code is
reported as not inspected,
not as absence of policy.
The original operation
inventory and physical
writer universe remain
independent: no list
of observed Gesso
executors proves that
outside XTDB clients
cannot mutate the same
business state.
A privacy-scoped view
never discloses protected
actor or organization
facts to a reader
unauthorized to see them.

*Positive/adverse oracle:* a
real public model-owned
Membership guard is
listed with its source,
dependencies and qualified
coverage; an arbitrary
Clojure callback with
uninspected extra
authorization is not
reported as fully proved
because the semantic-audit
operation list is empty
or contains no detected
escapes. A valid
complex domain policy
must remain expressible
with honest assumptions.

*** Gate O -- Formal assumptions create production obligations

A formal proof is about its
precise source-derived subject,
definitions, axioms and
premises. Every correctness-
relevant assumption needed
to apply its result creates
an *owned production obligation*,
not merely a diagnostic note.
For a model that treats
Claim as an atomic guarded
action, the selected real
Request/RequestAssignment
writer must implement the
same protected transition
or the correspondence
must retain a named,
unproved physical premise.
Passing model exploration
does not establish that
arbitrary Clojure or XTDB
executes the checked
transition.

The transfer ledger binds
the exact original
initializer/invariants
and complete operation
catalog, declared actor
and current Membership/
Organization policy,
read/guard footprint,
relationship absence or
one-primary cardinality,
multi-document write
footprint and protected
frame (including new
field presence), command
binding/retained outcome,
publication intent,
provider consistency/
progression, model-to-
concrete state relation,
installed handler/executor,
and every relevant
physical writer.
A pinned external read
is not a transaction-
internal prestate witness;
an ASSERT-before-PUT
shape, local submission
receipt, ledger key or
matching later card is
not independently a
universal backend
refinement theorem.
Full-value endpoint
comparison does not
exclude ABA without
a history-sensitive
mechanism when required.

If the formal subject
assumes authentic
communication, fair
delivery, resource
currentness, authorized
observation or eventual
browser convergence,
the actual selected
transport, router,
read permission,
HTMX effect and
user-action policy
carry separate physical/
environmental duties.
HumanHelp's advisory
remote policy cannot
prove unconditional
automatic visible
convergence when Bob
does not grant Refresh.

*Positive/adverse oracle:* a
qualifying actual guarded
multi-record Claim and
relevant external writer
inventory support the
stated concrete-to-
abstract safety relation;
concurrent duplicate
primary assignment,
unprotected newly added
field, missing trusted
Membership, incorrect
XTDB prestate claim or
lost router interest
leaves the exact
assumption open or
fails its requested
physical admission.
An unaffected abstract
theorem remains a valid
theorem under its
original premises,
not proof of the
broken production step.

*** Gate P -- Intent review and repair preserve the evidence boundary

Human, model-assisted or
LLM-assisted intent
review may identify an
internally well-typed
policy as surprising,
asymmetric, under-
constrained, inequitable,
incomplete or inconsistent
with independently
stated product needs.
Such a finding is
*advisory*. It cannot
be promoted into a
proof result, new
runtime authorization,
automatic source
mutation, denied
valid program,
discharged formal
premise or modified
release gate solely
because a reviewer
expressed confidence.

Only the legitimate
product/policy owner
may decide whether a
new requirement changes
business intent. A
confirmed decision
becomes an original
public model rule,
declared invariant,
operation/authorization/
disclosure contract or
explicit requirement.
Gesso then checks
the supported meaning
and regenerates
affected Choreo,
proof obligations,
application plans,
build assets and
current physical
qualification. A
changed assumption,
weakened invariant
or removed capability
is a *new claim* whose
original/revised
denominators and
evidence stay
distinguishable.
A generator or
review report cannot
edit the required
product inventory
until it appears
complete.

A mechanistically invalid
rule, unavailable
knowledge, missing
dynamic recovery, or
unsafe deployed effect
belongs to its first
appropriate typed
semantic, preflight,
trusted model,
issuer or physical
owner. An unprovable
opaque Clojure policy
remains a declared
premise or unsupported
assurance request
until independently
qualified, rather
than silently being
rewritten into a
convenient rule that
can be proved.
Ordinary Clojure
remains the developer
authoring surface;
Gesso owns generation
of formal models,
not a requirement
that HumanHelp
developers handwrite
a second TLA+/Quint/
Recife business model.

*Positive/adverse oracle:* a
product owner approves
a revised current
Membership requirement
at the original model
source, and the affected
proof/plan/physical
evidence is rechecked
without discarding
unaffected sound
results. A reviewer
who merely dislikes a
valid policy cannot
modify it through a
diagnostic report,
weaken the invariant
in generated formal
code, alter a green
status or conceal
the unselected
Response decision.

Together Gates A–P
remain *independently
scoped recurring
checks*, not one
undifferentiated
completion badge.
They reject an
invalid candidate
at its first real
owner, preserve
valid usable
positives and
leave genuinely
unknown, conditional,
uninspected and
unsupported duties
visible. A final
accepted release
must meet its
original selected
profile without
changing its
requirements after
seeing failures.
