** Final unified invariants

The unified design is one coherent account of what an application *means*,
which participants may learn and do, how authoritative facts change, which
physical effects implement that meaning, and exactly what the available
evidence establishes. Its purpose is not to replace ordinary Clojure web
development with a proof-only application language. It makes the supported,
correctness-relevant web domain narrower and more explicit than arbitrary
computation, so construction, source-derived checking and real enforcement can
work together without giving either a fictional authority.

These are the closing invariants of Parts I–XII, not a second model language,
an additional runtime policy registry, or new completion criteria. Each
applies only to its admitted subject and selected assurance/release profile; a
stronger conclusion requires the independently named supporting source,
theorem, observation and physical correspondence. The final sixteen Gates A–P
and four independent Part XI completion milestones retain their existing
owners and cannot be replaced by this summary.

*** Application meaning and executable construction

A Gesso application has *one original public semantic construction*. Models
own business policy, actor/relationship eligibility, disclosure, authoritative
state and permitted effects. Choreo describes typed interactions and the
knowledge participants must possess; its global machine admits no operation
whose projected local participant cannot justify its relevant inputs, causal
order or branch choice. Projection constructs local machines from that same
meaning. Views attach those operations to actual affordances and presentation;
application construction joins models, plans, routes, effects, transports,
browser artifacts, acquisition and corresponding checking into one
source-derived object, not independently hand-maintained copies.

Knowledge is not the same thing as data existing somewhere in a globally
described application. A participant knows only what its declared local input,
valid communication, authorized observation, deterministic derivation or
explicitly admitted trusted assertion establishes. Communication is one way to
produce knowledge, not the definition of distributed knowledge. Role,
participant, principal, resource, transport attempt and server host have
distinct identities; none may be inferred or authorized from another merely by
giving them matching names.

The portable **executable semantic core** is the permanent source of the
supported machine behavior. It is not identical to the small propositional
**proof kernel**: the former runs defined Gesso semantics, while the latter
checks specific derivations under an explicit logic. Finite checking, theorem
elaboration, independent proof assistants, generated model checking and test
instrumentation have distinct jobs; they neither replace the executable core
nor become new owners of HumanHelp's business rules. A pure browser transition
boundary and the relevant Clojure/JVM implementation can be compared only
through declared semantics and qualified correspondence, not through parallel
human-written versions of the same policy.

Ordinary Clojure remains the application author's medium, including complex
domain behavior and existing supported libraries. Gesso generates accepted
internal rules, obligations, plans and formal representations where its
precise language permits; authors need not maintain handwritten parallel TLA+,
Quint, Recife or proof-assistant specifications of their app. Host-language
policies outside the closed checked language require honest public typed,
effect, dependency and authority contracts with explicit opaque or trusted
implementation-conformance premises. Neither an unknown Clojure predicate nor
an unsupported program is made verified by calling it a rule. The checked
subset must not define away independently required application operations.

The source semantic owner remains single when the original business policy
changes: change the model/requirement and regenerate the affected Choreo,
projections, proof subjects, application closure, artifacts and actual
installation qualifications. A browser, router, auditor, report or formal
generator may not silently repair itself by inventing a second policy owner.
Source-derived audit and readable first-blocker diagnosis are projections of
that same original source, with their actual inspection coverage and
unexamined code made explicit.

*** Authority, commands, and observations

The browser is adversarial relative to authoritative model truth. It cannot
grant server rights by posting a principal, role, tenant, Request identifier,
capability-shaped map, proof report, claimed basis, plan label, provenance or
saved client state. Trusted server boundaries authenticate the principal,
select the admitted operation/target and reauthorize *current* read,
subscription, command-result disclosure and mutation. A presented affordance,
nominal value or checked theorem does not substitute for the guarded
authoritative action.

The authoritative transition enforces the current original model policy and
all required source-owned writes in its declared atomic frame. For HumanHelp
Claim that includes the actual Request, independent RequestAssignment
one-primary relationship, current Membership/Organization eligibility, command
history and any selected durable publication intent. A stale external read,
typed transaction plan, locally accepted write receipt, matching later card or
checked ASSERT/PUT syntax does not independently attest transaction-internal
prestate, all relevant writers, preserved unrelated fields, ABA history or
physically committed outcome. The selected backend's exact enforcement and any
open provider premises remain visible. XTDB may be deliberately chosen as the
implementation substrate when it simplifies real correctness; semantic
concepts should not be confused with incidental XTDB representation or
burdened by speculative portability requirements.

A static operation, one logical command, a particular attempt, physical
submission, transport request, browser execution and later observation are
*not* interchangeable identities. A command preserves its immutable original
binding across admitted retries. Incorporation, durable historical
attribution, transaction/provider result, HTTP delivery, participant
knowledge, current state and physically adopted presentation remain distinct.
A lost response after possible submission leaves a genuine unknown until
qualified command-specific evidence establishes the original result or valid
exclusion. A later Request state cannot establish that C42 incorporated, even
when it looks compatible; another attempt's abort does not exclude a
concurrent incorporation. Historical compatible replay may be permitted even
after a new-command Claim guard becomes false, but current result disclosure
remains authorized independently.

An authoritative read identifies its principal, scope, original
query/relationship meaning, projection, trusted provider position and
coverage. An authoritative *absence* is a qualified negative observation under
the appropriate disclosure semantics, not a missing DOM row, local filter,
silent subscriber or failed network request. Domain revision, transaction
basis, valid time, provider progression, observation, command, request,
resource instance and assembly generation remain separate coordinates. Generic
Live cannot invent total ordering from opaque basis strings, timestamps,
invalidation arrival or source names. Only the chosen trusted authority can
establish the progression and coverage relation needed by a read or
command-resolution claim.

*** Presentation, resources, and recovery

Optimism is typed, nonauthoritative, provisional knowledge of one command or
user intent under its admitted source and available basis. It may display
expected consequences but cannot claim incorporation, authorize new actions or
suppress unknown command outcomes. A creator's Create intent does not require
a fictional earlier observation of an entity that does not yet exist.
Provisional settlement, rejection, unknown status, supersession and postcommit
UI failure have different meanings and recovery owners.

The browser realizes its local machine through a small portable pure
state/event-to-abstract-effect transition boundary and a deliberately narrow
host-effect shell. HTMX retains the ordinary request/response, out-of-band
target, swap, settlement and lifecycle work it already owns. Gesso gates or
observes the documented boundaries needed for semantic safety; a bespoke HTMX
extension or replacement client network stack is not an architectural
prerequisite. Gesso-owned timers, DOM effects, deferred callbacks, continuity,
optimistic presentation and related browser resource effects have explicit
ownership and observable completion.

Semantic retirement revokes the relevant execution/ resource owner's effect
rights *before* best-effort physical cleanup. Shared resources independently
owned by other live executions retain their own legitimate rights. A callback,
HTTP response or timer must recheck its actual current effect/ target
ownership at the last physical mutation, not only when scheduled. A reused
logical DOM ID, failed abort, replaced plan or newer browser generation cannot
resurrect retired rights. Installation is recorded after the selected actual
physical effect completes, not merely on HTTP 200, response parsing or an
intended swap. Selection, scroll, focus, compatible drafts and details
continuity preserve local presentation only; they do not freeze model truth or
grant obsolete continuation authority.

Gesso Live transports scope-bound **advisory** information that relevant
authority may have changed. It is not a second database, automatic read
permission, authentic command result, subscription receipt or DOM
installation. A selected durable profile records the required original
publication intent in a suitable authoritative commit boundary and separately
owns dispatch, retry, deduplication and failure accounting. Neither an outbox
nor a locally complete router filter proves that a late subscriber cannot miss
an invalidation. Initial observation, registration fencing, qualified
replay/gap detection, failover and independently permitted reacquisition have
explicit owners and reliability premises. Duplicated advisory redelivery is
safe under its actual disclosure, resource and idempotency contract; it never
duplicates business incorporation.

Acquisition and *visible adoption* require their own current authorization,
source/query scope, trusted observation coverage, selected user grant when
applicable, and actual completed browser installation. For HumanHelp, remote
Create and remote lifecycle changes, including changes to already- visible
cards, remain advisory until Bob explicitly grants one scoped logical Refresh
under the selected ordinary policy. A notice, reconnect, router failover or
later advisory arriving during that cycle cannot mint an unrequested successor
read. A provider- qualified already covered newer observation may discharge
the corresponding debt; incomparable basis, incomplete OOB install or
uncovered query retains it. Creator- local accepted Create and narrowly
correlated authorized own-command recovery are separately declared exceptions,
not general remote auto-adoption.

Lost command outcome, lost notice, expired authority, invalidated browser
resources, provider failure and changed release need separately owned
recovery. Convergence and liveness are conditional on the named fairness,
retention, availability, scope, authenticated user action and environmental
premises. In particular a policy requiring Bob to press Refresh cannot promise
automatic visible convergence if he never does so.

*** Evidence, release, and topology

Every accepted conclusion keeps its exact source, proposition, scope, original
required obligation inventory, selected profile, premise set, checker or test
mechanism, observation/evidence provenance, interpretation version and actual
implementation and installation applicability. Original product/
operation/invariant requirements are independent of the currently discovered
proof, route, browser or writer graph. A closed smaller graph proves or checks
only its named smaller subject. A generator cannot silently remove Reassign,
Response, another relevant writer, a required dynamic recovery or a positive
operation and thereby complete the requested larger claim.

Mechanically derived proof status is reserved for what an accepted formal
semantics and actually checked derivation establish under named assumptions.
Conditional induction, finite state exhaustion, production test,
original-source type/preflight, physical writer observation, independent
mathematical theorem and formal-to-production refinement are distinct evidence
classes. Exact finite checking establishes its finite bounded subject;
unbounded transfer requires sound abstraction or another qualified theorem.
Tests remain essential for trusted host interfaces, libraries, actual
Chromium/HTMX, Biff/XTDB, deployment, security, faults and scale; they are not
a synonym for proof. Likewise a certificate-shaped map, hash, copyable report
or tool exit code is not proof issuance, runtime authorization or an installed
execution capability.

The closed seven-rule kernel's possible external theorem has explicit S0
rule-level soundness and S1 derivation- system soundness subjects. S2 actual
Clojure checker correspondence, S3 typed source-atom interpretation and S4
issuer/application to physical transfer are separately qualified. A sound
abstract theorem can remain true when a backend, handler, artifact or external
writer changes; its *physical applicability* must then be requalified. A
source- derived proof hierarchy cannot invent its own complete application
catalog or authenticate untested physical observations.

A released application is not defined by one green map. The original selected
requirements and permitted evidence mechanisms precede evaluation; the
candidate, actual installed host, current activation and named completion
claim require four subject- specific decisions. Application safety is enforced
from the first served managed effect, not postponed until full formal or
distributed qualification. Directional old/new client/server compatibility,
retired effect rights, real served/loaded artifact identities, protected
writers, command history, source generation and failure recovery are
independently checked where relevant. Rollback of code neither reverses a
durable transaction nor erases a pending historical command.

Operational HumanHelp completion, compositional certification, external formal
verification and production multi-node realization are four distinct milestone
claims with their own original requirements and evidence. The full HumanHelp
product includes six lifecycle actions, Create, manager-target Reassign,
current model authorization, rich adopted- board behavior, selected Response
interaction, explicit Refresh and fourteen independent positive/adverse
acceptance gates. A narrow Claim proof does not complete the product; an
operational application need not claim all external metatheory, and a two-
host prototype does not qualify every production topology. A reject-
everything candidate or a never- executed mandatory test cannot satisfy a
usable completion gate.

Multiple physical hosts realize the same logical authority, command, Choreo
and browser contract. They do not make a new business semantics. Durable model
facts remain the ordinary cross-host rendezvous; selected authoritative
command history, publication recovery, subscriber interest, authenticated
acquisition, router failover and browser adoption carry their real failure
windows. A node's interchangeability does not prove exactly- once effects,
complete interest knowledge, all-message receipt or participant liveness. A
Clojure router and a later Rust optimization may implement the same semantic
contract; neither a particular router topology nor universal durable
participant messaging is mandatory for ordinary single-node Gesso.

*** Source-owned audit, intent, and repair

An application's correctness-relevant policy is visible as named original
rules, operation inputs and effects, authority and disclosure, lifecycle
transitions, dependencies, admitted assumptions and required capabilities. The
audit identifies the code and physical paths it actually inspected, and which
Clojure predicates, callbacks, FX, external services and writers remain opaque
or uninspected. “No detected escape in the inspected subset” does not mean
“all source policy was inspected.” The report is a read-only explanation under
the same disclosure policy, not a second source of authority or an issuer.

A diagnostic finds the earliest legitimately knowable blocker and retains the
dependent failures as consequences. A model counterexample refers to its
actual formal subject, trace, abstraction, bounds and premises; a fixture
cannot be passed off as a generated external checker run or a real XTDB
incident. Mutants need a predeclared first enforcing gate, a usable valid
positive, and independent observation of the prohibited effect within the
actual scope. A late detection is not before-effect prevention, and no
accepted status may be manufactured by silently relaxing the selected profile.

Human or AI-assisted review may identify surprising intent, missing business
restrictions or reasonable alternative policies. It does not prove a
well-typed policy wrong, issue admission, grant new rights or change source.
Only the authorized product owner can select a new business requirement and
promote it into the original public model/semantic contract. Repair then
regenerates and rechecks only the affected source, proof, plan, adapter, host,
asset and physical evidence cone. Weakening an invariant, adding an axiom or
removing a required feature is a new claim, not an invisible way to make the
old claim pass.

*** Final design principle

#+begin_quote
A Gesso choreography is a typed, source-owned description of how participants
acting for principals interact with evolving authoritative facts, communicate
justified knowledge, and execute permitted local behavior.

One executable semantic machine joins public model truth, global and local
Choreo, declarative HTMX-facing views, typed provisional browser behavior,
trustworthy guarded transactions and scoped advisory recovery. The framework
generates the proof and assembly machinery around that meaning rather than
asking application authors to write separate business rules for every runtime
or theorem prover.

The same design is valid on one node or across multiple hosts. Its logical
meaning is stable while physical registration, publication, command history,
installation and recovery carry explicit real-world premises.

Gesso may prove the supported claims it has actually checked, and may accept
explicitly qualified trusted assumptions, but it may not transform a typed
label, finite test, diagnostic, report, compilation, browser success or
source-consistent graph into a stronger certificate or production fact without
the corresponding evidence and actual enforcing boundary.

Success is usable, authorized product behavior with its true source, physical
effects, proof scope and remaining obligations visible. No apparently green
subset, missing required capability, unchecked writer, silent downgrade,
fabricated command outcome, unauthorized adoption or unexamined assumption can
stand in for that result.
#+end_quote
