# R5.1 Part III — 03.15 Projection and Knowledge

**R4 baseline:** `main` Part III, “Projection and branch knowledge,”
“Projection is a semantic compilation boundary,” and
“Projection cannot create knowledge.”
**R5.1 candidate:** section 03.15.
**Status:** focused preservation review, not whole-Part-III acceptance
or certification of a physical deployment.

## R4 normative obligations preserved

- Global abstract values are not automatically role-local knowledge.
- Communication, justified entry knowledge, authorized authoritative
  observations, derivation and explicitly classified assertions are possible
  knowledge paths, each subject to its own applicable premises.
- Roles need discriminators before behavior-sensitive continuations diverge;
  equivalent local continuations do not demand gratuitous communication.
- A current Request state is not automatically a witness of an earlier
  command's outcome or the selection of a particular historical branch.
- Closed communication contracts admit only declared semantic fields;
  sender knowledge, trusted receipt, and adversarial-boundary admission
  remain separate from payload shape.
- Projection retains the necessary local message, correlation, resource,
  authority, failure and executable-guard semantics while omitting irrelevant
  or unavailable global data.
- Projection never manufactures knowledge or launders asserted,
  communicated or client-supplied values into stronger categories.

## R5 design retained and strengthened

- R5's succinct authority-approval/helper example and its separate
  R4-derived warning about command-specific history are retained.
- Distinguish the proposition required at the local use from an equal
  representation, unrelated subject, or similar current state.
- An assertion may be a legitimate *assertion* premise in an admitted
  contract; it cannot turn into a proved business proposition without
  another justification.
- A valid alternative knowledge source is not permission to edit the
  choreography's observable communication or control structure.
  Substitution needs a declared alternative or justified refinement,
  including causal and progress obligations.
- Message reception requires sender, correlation, scope and owner-relative
  admission; optional fields sent are not exempt from knowledge and
  disclosure rules.
- Global private state must not leak into a projected role merely to
  make selection convenient.
- Nine negative cases test scope, history, trust, communication
  substitution, private projections and branch effects.

## Current Gesso correspondence (audit only)

- `gesso.choreo.verify` implements separate forward must-analyses for
  definite global values, role-local knowledge and definitely authoritative
  provenance, including edge-sensitive `:await` alternatives. Its
  documentation does not claim authority authentication or full optional
  sender-field checking.
- `gesso.choreo.project` emits role-local plans, preserves declared
  communication contracts, rejects certain uncommunicated remote branch
  divergences, and tracks authoritative causal barriers. Those
  implementation choices are evidence of feasibility, not a universal
  refinement theorem or completeness proof for every knowledge path.
- `gesso.choreo.knowledge` records input, communicated, authoritative,
  derived and asserted provenance separately; its assertions do not
  establish independent truth or authorization.
- `gesso.choreo.correspondence` checks concrete schedules against
  independently projected machines and global semantic traces; those
  witnesses do not prove all-schedules correspondence or liveness.
- `gesso.choreo.semantics` owns the canonical distributed observation
  projection and distinguishes global semantic values from actor knowledge.
