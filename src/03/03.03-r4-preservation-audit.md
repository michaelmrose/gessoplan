# R5.1 Part III — 03.03 A Worked Expense Review

**Baseline:** R4 `main` Part III expense-approval example and role-local
projections (within “What a choreography is”).
**Candidate:** `r5.1` source 03.03.
**Status:** focused R4 preservation audit, not full Part III acceptance,
application acceptance, or executable model-policy certification.

## R4 semantics preserved

- Expense and ApprovalPolicy are application-owned domain facts; the manager
  must acquire relevant current Expense and Policy observations rather than
  infer them from receipt of a selected identifier.
- The Finance-versus-manager decision is owned by the role that has the
  necessary knowledge, and the Finance role need not learn unrelated
  manager-only details merely to participate.
- The authoritative model guards current status, caller permission, policy
  and the actual recorded outcome; a pre-read or branch decision is not a
  committed transition.
- Messages and projected continuations follow supported causal/knowledge
  contracts rather than independently authored local workflows.
- Finance approval is an independently guarded authoritative operation.
  R4's single conceptual employee/manager/Finance choreography remains a
  permitted form when its actual message, branching and liveness contracts
  justify it; this R5 example chooses shorter protocols joined by durable
  authoritative state rather than requiring unused roles to wait.
- R4's policy schema named `:policy/manager-limit` but its illustrative
  Finance-threshold rule did not use it. If a ceiling is a real policy
  constraint it belongs to the checked public model guard and any route
  derivation, not an inert schema attribute.

## Stronger R5 design retained

- Money values use the explicit currency-compatible Part I Money profile
  rather than host decimal comparisons with undefined units.
- R5 separates employee submission, manager review and later Finance
  authorization into independently admitted short executions with correct
  current role/principal knowledge; no imaginary persistent execution.
- The review acquisition binds Policy to the selected Expense's department
  under a qualified coherent read context, not by coincidental map shape.
- Model incorporation has guarded commit plus command identity/outcome
  record; compatible replay and indeterminate incorporation have separate
  continuations and current disclosure rules.
- Accepted notification requires exact command-related recorded evidence,
  a scoped recipient, explicit information-release policy, and delivery
  durability or permitted loss; the no-notification disposition cannot
  fabricate a negative fact about a hidden Request/Expense.
- The notifier receives only needed outcome information, not private
  policy thresholds, and local completion is not universal termination.

## Changes in this revision

1. State that one returned map does not itself prove Expense/Policy basis
   coherence, and address R4's unused manager-limit field without imposing
   an unconditional manager ceiling on every application.
2. Qualify the displayed `:variants` envelope as *higher-level schematic
   inspection data*. Current `gesso.choreo.core/communicate` exposes
   `:required`, `:optional`, `:correlation` and `:open-payload?`, not
   an executable `:variants` schema. Require checked elaboration into
   distinguishable closed communications and receive alternatives.
3. Add seven concrete negative cases for stale policy, missing ceiling
   guards, replayed notifications, forged results, ambiguous variants,
   postcommit delivery failure and independent Finance entry.
4. Preserve the R4 multi-role choreography as an available pattern under
   real delivery/knowledge contracts; do not require it in a workflow
   spanning different actors and days.

## Current Gesso correspondence and limits

- `gesso.choreo.semantics` implements six global nodes and enforces
  declared communication payload structure. Shape does not authenticate
  the manager or prove model incorporation.
- `gesso.choreo.core` currently implements required, optional,
  correlation and open-payload options for `:communicate`; there is no
  intrinsic tagged-variants authoring option in that constructor.
- `gesso.choreo.project` preserves communication boundaries and rejects
  statically overlapping receive alternatives sharing a
  sender/event/channel. It is not evidence that this illustrative Expense
  contract has been compiled into such alternatives.
- `gesso.choreo.verify` checks definite role-local knowledge and
  producer/consumer flow but does not authenticate the policy read,
  prove the Money rule, guard a physical transaction, or ensure actual
  notification delivery.
- The Expense schemas, public operations, notifier and exact Money
  contract in this example are normative fixtures, not proof that these
  exact APIs or a deployed Expense app exist.

preserved. This audit is not an implementation completion claim.
