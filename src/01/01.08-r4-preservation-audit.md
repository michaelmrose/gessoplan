# R5.1 Part I — 01.08 construction and ownership preservation audit

Baseline: canonical R4 `main`. Reviewed source: R5.1 01.08.
This is a document review, not deployed application or theorem evidence.

## R4 preservation

- The original "state semantic intent once" and "derive rather than
  synchronize" rules are preserved, including the illustrative Claim
  button, projection, checked route/server realization, and negative
  manually synchronized HTMX example.
- R5 improves on R4 by separating candidate computation, candidate
  admission, lowering, physical submission, incorporation, outcomes,
  command lifetime, and effect ownership. None of that is removed.
- R5 retains usable full-language Clojure behind explicit contracts,
  admits bounded concrete checks, and keeps proof limitations visible.
- Proof induction still requires initialization, invariant-preserving
  transitions, relevant writer coverage, and abstract-to-physical
  correspondence. The managed capability is not a command-deduplication
  mechanism or a hostile-code JVM sandbox.

## Changes made in 01.08

1. Direct read/write footprints from the supported semantic grammar are
   distinguished from the complete protected *semantic* dependencies.
   Relationship/absence/uniqueness predicates and opaque cross-model
   policies require independently source-owned dependency contracts and
   checked coverage, or explicit unresolved premises. An author-supplied
   footprint cannot override derived evidence.
2. The physical Claim example is aligned with Parts IV, VIII and XII:
   RequestAssignment primary responsibility, relevant Membership and
   Organization policy, absence of competing assignment where required,
   command-result incorporation, and durable publication intent are
   represented without inventing a Request helper field or physical schema.
3. "One semantic source" is one authoritative owner *per fact*, not a
   single giant registry. Model, Choreo, views, ingress, acquisition, and
   physical adapters own their genuine choices; the assembler derives or
   checks correspondences without granting one owner another's authority.
4. The Claim card's =status :open= visibility example is explicitly
   presentation-only. The actual permitted user affordance follows the
   model-owned policy and independent product acceptance; static argument
   validation or a browser command identifier is not authentication or
   commit-time authorization.
