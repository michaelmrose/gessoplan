## Governing reconciliation

Keep R5's clean developer path and intentionally small proof
language. Restore R4's concrete proof/user error examples,
exact public-admitted product category discipline,
application-author policy ownership and complete
multi-layer final invariant. Strengthen the definition
of “completion” by connecting sectional contracts to
independently testable semantics, genuine installed
realization, positive HumanHelp usability, and an
honest claim-scoped root report. This is a timeless
specification of finished Gesso capabilities, not
a summary of what v1622 currently demonstrates.

## R4 preservation and design decisions

1. **Declaration-first ergonomics:** Source authoring
   stays in normal Clojure/Gesso production model,
   rule, operation, Choreo and application
   construction. The framework carries internal
   proof extraction, transitive closure and
   certified-handle bookkeeping.

2. **No second proof language requirement:** Application
   developers need not maintain independent Recife,
   Quint, TLA+, Lean proof terms or manually
   copy semantic hashes. External formal tools
   may be used by Gesso's generated assurance
   machinery without authoring a second system.

3. **Usable full-language integration:** Opaque
   Clojure code has named scoped conformance
   premises, not dishonest proof of all branches.
   Typing wrappers appear only where raw
   category substitution violates actual
   semantic meaning or API admission.

4. **R4 valid and invalid examples survive:** A
   RequestStatus equality and properly sourced
   helper predicate can be well typed; numeric
   ordering of RequestStatus fails intrinsic
   typing; an uncommunicated private browser
   predicate fails distributed use-site
   knowledge rather than type checking.

5. **Stale dependency distinction:** A new
   coherent Request schema S18 can invalidate
   old Claim rules built from S17 without
   making S18 intrinsically malformed.
   Checked bridges or source re-elaboration
   are legitimate; copied signatures are not.

6. **Forged certificate and proof steps fail:**
   Same-shaped maps, `:valid? true` and
   missing-premise derivations cannot mint
   admitted categories. Real checker-issued
   evidence carries original subject and
   rule semantics.

7. **Repair guidance is honest:** Fixing
   malformed source, providing actual
   proof/observation, explicitly requesting
   a weaker profile and authorizing a
   product-policy change are different
   actions. Error handling cannot simply
   relabel failed source verification as
   trusted application truth.

8. **Framework and application policy ownership:**
   Gesso validates declared rules and
   distributed admissibility; HumanHelp
   owns which actors/Requests and
   relationships should be eligible.
   R4's explicit policy assertions
   remain visible in root reports
   without being called framework
   theorems.

9. **Twelve milestone acceptance gates:** Executable
   typed/checker semantics, source completeness,
   real issuance, premise propagation/discharge,
   opaque code, checked evolution/reuse,
   distributed knowledge, actual production
   correspondence, real Claim pilot,
   independent HumanHelp product acceptance,
   adversarial mutation corpus and usable
   developer/root reports are separate.

10. **R4 root proof chain preserved:** Public
    User/Membership/Organization/Location/
    Request/RequestAssignment semantics
    support rules, operation, Choreo,
    helper/authority projections,
    ExecutablePlan, ApplicationAssembly,
    installed route/assets/model
    execution and trustworthy outcome/
    permitted acquisition.

11. **Positive control is not optional:** A
    reject-all construction might have
    no forbidden transitions while
    failing essential eligible
    Create/Claim/Unclaim/Reassign
    and independent HumanHelp
    product gates.

12. **Part VIII remains independent:** Its
    fourteen completion gates include
    current protected model behavior,
    two-client selective adoption,
    browser continuity and still
    unselected response-flow contract.
    Completion of the Claim proof
    pilot is not full product acceptance.

13. **Conditional claims remain exact:** A
    checked abstract theorem may
    depend on opaque policy callable,
    XTDB atomicity, trusted
    installed source or fair
    environment. Those premises
    propagate into the exact root
    claim and are not silently
    discharged by a passing
    browser test.

14. **Coverage includes physical unknowns:** A
    complete declared induction
    catalog does not enumerate
    every external, admin or raw
    XTDB writer. The root report
    distinguishes checked declared
    closure from independently
    qualified installed
    realization/writer coverage.

15. **Currentness and temporal authority:** A
    stable abstract theorem can
    survive an unrelated source
    change while a swapped
    handler/asset, retired
    browser resource or changed
    Membership invalidates
    the corresponding physical/
    instance guarantee.

16. **Historical identity remains separate:**
    A current Request card cannot
    establish C42's historical
    incorporation, a trusted
    readback cannot by itself
    prove transaction causality,
    and HTTP success cannot
    prove physical adoption.

17. **Assurance classes are not fungible:** Source
    proof, finite checking,
    implementation conformance,
    controlled physical
    observation, actual production
    route/transaction, loaded
    browser effect and
    independent product
    acceptance remain
    different evidence
    families and quantifiers.

18. **Proof and runtime graph cycles differ:** Proof
    justification is acyclic
    except explicitly justified
    internal recursion; legal
    runtime retry/subscription
    loops do not provide
    reciprocal premise
    discharge.

19. **First-blocker diagnostics retained:** Original
    R4 typing, dependency, use-site
    knowledge, unsupported
    theorem and malformed
    certificate examples
    have stable explanation,
    actual enforcement boundary
    and downstream not-issued
    effects, without private
    actor data leakage.

20. **Release meaning and final invariant:** A
    strong selected profile
    cannot silently upgrade
    an unresolved premise
    or incomplete product
    requirement, and no
    copied status gives
    effect authority.
    The Part IX final
    invariant exports only
    the justified claim
    with its exact source
    and applicability.

## Current Gesso v1622 source evidence and scope

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/application_assurance_report.clj`
  exposes a source-rechecked, copyable
  conditional application assurance
  description for supported abstract
  induction, operation composition,

  observed writer accounting.
  Its own documentation lists
  unchecked kernel meta-soundness,
  installed production adoption,

  universal XTDB refinement and
  browser/HTMX/SSE correspondence.
  It is not a global proof or
  a runtime authorization receipt.

  reconstructs a deterministic
  first-blocker diagnostic from
  controlled source proof categories.
  Reports are descriptive and
  copyable; the namespace refuses
  to promote an arbitrary report

  issues restricted source-checked
  propositional, guarded-transition
  and conditional-induction products,

  dependency/premise checking,
  private issuer-registered
  process-local handles
  and nonauthoritative descriptions.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/application_dependency.clj`
  checks original declared
  induction catalog closure,
  while explicitly retaining
  real physical writer
  inventory and checked
  model-implementation
  correspondence as open.

- `src/gesso/live/application_preflight.clj`,
  `application_preflight/assurance.clj`

## Positive/adversarial end-section acceptance pairs

1. **Ordinary authoring:** A
   real eligible model/
   Choreo/assembly source
   generates the needed
   checked artifacts.
   Negative: manual
   copied semantic IDs
   or proof maps become
   a hidden requirement.

2. **Valid Claim rule:** Source
   admits an appropriate
   typed RequestStatus
   predicate. Negative:
   enum numeric ordering
   passes or a missing
   helper input is silently
   manufactured.

3. **Distributed use:** A
   permitted private
   decision is disclosed
   through an authorized
   server projection.
   Negative: browser
   branches on an
   uncommunicated
   private fact.

4. **Stale evolution:** New
   S18 and repaired
   dependent Claim
   can compose;
   negative: old S17
   certificate is accepted
   for S18 solely via
   same-shaped records.

5. **Proof issuance:** Original
   valid derivation
   earns a restricted
   certificate. Negative:
   forged map/omitted
   proof premise
   issues the same
   admitted category.

6. **Conditional honesty:** A
   permitted opaque
   Organization model
   premise remains
   visible. Negative:
   failed checking
   is relabeled
   :discharged
   without evidence.

7. **Complete source:** All
   required invariant/
   operation cases
   and source dependencies
   are present.
   Negative: pruned
   Unclaim in an
   otherwise valid
   DAG establishes
   original coverage.

8. **Current realization:** Actual
   installed route,
   model writer and
   loaded plan belong
   to one admitted
   generation. Negative:
   old certificate,
   new handler and
   mismatched browser
   asset are composed.

9. **Physical Claim:** Two
   eligible helpers
   actually contend for
   R17 under the guarded
   writer and preserve
   one active primary.
   Negative: separate
   pre-read or fabricated
   transaction report
   is treated as
   actual causality.

10. **History and recovery:** C42's
    valid original command
    can be reconciled
    under retention.
    Negative: new
    write after lost
    response or current
    card attributed
    as historical
    incorporation.

11. **Selective browser adoption:** One
    creator-local S
    and remote advisory
    R remain distinct;
    user-granted one
    Refresh installs
    its actual scoped
    acquisition.
    Negative: advisory
    causes unauthorized
    remote board read
    or stale owner
    commits late DOM
    update.

12. **Positive product coverage:** Real
    Claim, Unclaim,
    Mark-on-the-way,
    Complete, Cancel,
    Reassign and declared
    response interaction
    each have eligible
    positive/negative
    controls when selected.
    Negative: reject-all
    proof or missing
    product capability
    is scored as complete.

13. **Explainable failure:** A
    genuinely malformed
    rule or impossible
    proof yields stable
    first-blocker
    diagnostics. Negative:
    copied report, raw
    map or unsupported
    tool success
    can fake evidence.

14. **Profile truth:** A
    supported weaker
    conditional claim
    may be issued with
    its premises. Negative:
    an unavailable strong
    physical/theorem
    claim automatically
    downgrades under
    the same label.

15. **Scope preservation:** Exact
    finite/controlled
    physical evidence
    stays bounded.
    Negative: a
    test fixture's
    bounded positive
    trace becomes
    universal
    deployed theorem.

16. **Complete root report:** The
    source-derived
    generated report
    shows proof,
    dependency,
    deployment,
    runtime and
    independent
    product status.
    Negative: a
    generic :accepted
    field hides
    missing response
    decision, effect
    authorization,
    or physical writer
    evidence.

The test oracles
must predeclare the
