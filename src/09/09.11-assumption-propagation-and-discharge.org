** Assumption propagation and genuine discharge
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: req-certificate-assumption-propagation
:END:

An assumption is a scoped proposition whose truth is not established by the
current proof chain. It is not an arbitrary text label or an implicit escape
hatch. Its identity includes the exact subject, proposition, parameters,
nominal types, model/observation context, source and dependency generation,
issuer/origin, principal and authority domain where relevant, time/basis,
implementation boundary, and permitted use. Which of these are applicable
is determined by the proposition's semantic category, not by a universal
requirement to attach an unrelated browser session to every abstract lemma.

The proof system distinguishes (a) premises *used* to derive a specific
exported claim, (b) declared but unused or unrelated premises, (c) evidence
that establishes a particular premise, and (d) still-open premises admitted
conditionally by the selected profile. A syntactically matching assertion,
success report, nominal type, checked graph or provider name does not make
an open premise true. A certified conditional theorem is valid *with its
conditions*, not a license to drop those conditions downstream.

*** Assumption identity, origin and provenance

Each premise records its exact proposition and its original source in the
declared semantic interface or imported dependency. It also identifies any
authority/knowledge preconditions, checking-rule interpretation, opaque
Clojure binding, external provider/configuration envelope, application
policy owner or physical observation obligation on which it relies.

Two phrases such as “Membership is current” can denote different
subjects: Alice versus Bob, Request R17 versus R18, Organization O1
versus O2, transaction prestate versus an earlier candidate read, and
a particular command attempt versus all future commands. Equal names,
hashes, maps or equivalent-looking field values do not authorize
premise unification across those boundaries. A qualified provider
basis is ordered only by that provider's admitted scope/order contract;
an arbitrary timestamp or later-looking token does not strengthen
an observation.

The source catalog must distinguish at least domain assertions, opaque
implementation conformance, external-service behavior, authenticated
runtime acquisition/authorization, resource/instance rights, distributed
knowledge and progress/fairness premises. These are not one linear
ranking of “trust.” An assumption may be a legitimate explicit
contract for one profile while a stronger claim requires independent
evidence or refuses construction. In all cases an application-asserted
proposition remains an assertion until a checking boundary establishes
its own exact proposition.

*** Per-claim propagation through admitted dependencies

For each exported guarantee G, its accepted justification determines
a reconstructible dependency and premise footprint, not merely a flat
union of every premise in its containing namespace or certificate.
If an imported interface offers G1 under P1 and G2 under P2, a
consumer relying only on G1 inherits P1, not automatically P2.
Conversely, consuming both guarantees cannot lose either used
premise because a root report coalesced their labels.

Alternative accepted derivations may exist. The issuer records the
specific proof path used, its premises and every attempted or checked
substitution; it may report other viable paths without claiming that
the union, intersection, or unrecorded “best” path is a single proof.
If a different path is selected, its full premise footprint must
be checked again for the requested profile and current dependencies.

Composition preserves source, dependency, premise and evidence
identities. Transitive assumptions remain linked to the original
producer, and the final report can answer which conditional claims,
operations, role-local uses and installation boundaries depend on
each premise. A dependency's checked guarantee cannot be promoted
past an inherited open premise by renaming it or by flattening a
root certificate's summary.

The proof-dependency graph is acyclic in its justification projection.
Mutual claims that each “discharge” the other's premise have no
well-founded justification. Runtime retries and recursive
choreographies are different graphs and do not confer proof
permission. Scope-sensitive sharing is permitted only for reusable
facts with compatible subject, issuer and applicability; a one-use
authorization, browser Refresh grant or command-specific outcome
cannot be copied or spent as though it were a reusable lemma.

*** Genuine discharge as a checked substitution judgment

For an original accepted derivation of G under open premise P,
replacing P with evidence E requires the composition checker to
establish all the following:

1. *Evidence category:* E is itself admitted at the correct trusted
   checking, authenticated observation or enforcement boundary for
   the kind of proposition P. A copied report or structurally matched
   premise declaration is insufficient.
2. *Logical entailment:* E establishes precisely P, or an accepted
   inference/refinement bridge proves that E's proposition implies P.
   The checker performs type-correct, capture-avoiding substitution
   under the appropriate rule/context rather than treating copied
   identifiers as proof.
3. *Scope and applicability:* The checked relation binds original
   subject, parameters, principal/tenant, authority and disclosure
   scopes, source/semantic versions, instance/generation, and
   relevant temporal/basis conditions. No old observation is silently
   promoted into current transaction-state authority.
4. *Inherited conditions:* All assumptions, trust boundaries,
   dependencies and implementation qualifications of E are carried
   into the new derivation. An evidence chain conditional on Q
   replaces P with Q where applicable; it does not produce an
   unconditional G unless all relevant remaining premises are
   genuinely discharged.
5. *Complete checked construction:* The resulting proof/justification
   remains well founded, source- and claim-complete, and admitted
   under the consumer's selected profile. A changed premise set
   cannot bypass mandatory local rules, application coverage or
   positive non-vacuity obligations.

One useful schematic judgment is:

#+begin_example
Gamma, P |- G            Delta |- P
-----------------------------------  checked composition under same context
Gamma, Delta |- G
#+end_example

This is a *desired composition rule*, not a declaration that the
current Gesso propositional kernel implements hypothetical-context
discharge. A real checker must specify which entailment rules,
substitutions, contexts, assumption multiplicities and evidence
types it supports. It must reject unsupported generalization or
silently preserved free variables. The original premise and the
evidence that replaces it remain in the audit ledger even when P
no longer belongs to the *open* set of the resulting claim.

A source checker must not accept a caller's =:discharged?=,
=:verified?= or =:assumption-discharge= field as a verdict.
Structural equality of two assumptions only establishes that
their declarations have the same identity. Likewise, exact
finite entailment over uninterpreted Boolean atoms is a result
about that finite propositional abstraction, not evidence that
an opaque business-policy atom is true of real Request data.
If a proof uses additional premises, the resulting conclusion
remains conditional on them.

*** Static model contracts and current domain facts

Consider HumanHelp Claim. A source-checked rule may rely on
“the selected Organization policy callable implements the
declared typed predicate for these inputs without undeclared
relevant effects.” This is an implementation-conformance
premise, not the value of the policy for a particular
Organization today. The constructor binds the premise to
the selected callable/closure or generated implementation,
its dependencies and qualification envelope.

A direct unit test can exercise the callable but does not
prove universal conformance. A separately checked
implementation correspondence argument can discharge the
premise for the exact implementation, typed input domain
and covered effects it establishes. Its remaining compiler,
runtime, library and provider assumptions propagate.
A changed function body, captured state, dynamically
resolved Var or relevant library can invalidate that
physical correspondence without invalidating the
abstract policy theorem.

Actual Claim incorporation separately requires the
current authenticated principal, Request, Membership,
Organization association, eligibility and atomic
guarded transaction. A prior browser view or one earlier
server read is not the authoritative transaction prestate.
A trusted invocation-bound guard or actual verified
transaction observation may establish an *instance-specific*
premise under its qualified writer/basis contract. It
cannot be reused as authority for another user, Request,
command, transaction, or later retry without fresh
applicability checking.

Historical command incorporation and current state are
different propositions. If C42 incorporated and the
response was lost, command-specific trusted readback
may establish its historical disposition, while later
Reassign means the current Request state is different.
Neither a generic current card nor HTTP 200 discharges
the historical attribution premise; an old command
result does not grant new write authority.

*** Conditioned progress, resource rights and disclosure

An environment promise such as “the network eventually
recovers,” “a provider eventually incorporates work,”
“the scheduler is fair,” or “the user eventually
chooses Refresh” is a progress assumption with a
specified trace and failure domain. It is not a fact
created by a timeout declaration, an SSE event, or
an automatic retry. The corresponding liveness theorem
is conditional on precisely the required fairness,
quiescence, retention, service, client lifetime and
user-action premises. Failure to establish progress
does not automatically imply a safety violation;
proven safety does not manufacture liveness.

In the HumanHelp remote board, an advisory about an
updated Request may create refresh *debt* but cannot
establish that the user authorized acquisition.
The one-use Refresh grant is a current scoped resource
right with a specified acquisition budget and
installation obligation. Actual authorized invocation
and completed physical adoption establish their own
instance-specific propositions. An old grant cannot
discharge another Refresh permission, and a local
newer card observation cannot discharge unrelated
whole-board coverage. Lost or coalesced invalidations
may require an explicitly permitted reconnect/repair
read or a weaker continuity promise, not fictional
permission from prior SSE delivery.

Distributed knowledge premises also require the correct
role, issuer, recipient and disclosure boundary.
Communicating a typed value does not establish current
model authority; withholding a private Organization fact
cannot be repaired by pretending the browser knows it.
The system may communicate an authorized server-derived
public decision instead, under that decision's own
issuer and scope contract. Information release,
effect authority and historical/progression knowledge
remain separate checks.

*** Profiles, contradictions and unsatisfied premises

Every assurance profile states which local obligations
are mandatory, which premise categories may remain
open, what corroboration they require, and where
dynamic instances must be checked. A stronger profile
cannot be achieved by silently reclassifying unsupported
syntax, proof-rule failure, missing writers, escaped
policy, unknown code or unavailable checker as
application truth. A weaker conditional profile is a
distinct requested and reported result, with the
open-premise closure preserved.

Within its supported logic the verifier detects
contradictory checked declarations, incompatible
semantic commitments and inconsistent premise
uses where feasible. It is not a decision procedure
for arbitrary opaque Clojure code or third-party
business policy. A propositional proof from
inconsistent premises may be formally valid yet
vacuous for a realizable application. The root
assurance report therefore separates conditional
logical derivation, satisfiability/non-vacuity
under its declared abstraction, actual enabled
positive product paths, and physical execution
evidence. None substitutes for another.

An assumption's applicability can expire or be
withdrawn without making the underlying abstract
conditional theorem false. A provider upgrade
outside an accepted envelope, revoked service
credential, changed implementation binding,
retired execution, expired basis/lease, or
observed assumption violation invalidates the
*dependent deployment or instance claim* until
requalified. The dependency impact analysis
propagates that change to exactly the relied-on
claims; it does not automatically falsify
unrelated semantic results.

*** Qualified external providers and failure envelopes

A premise such as XTDB “atomic guarded incorporation”
must identify the exact operation and transaction
semantics, actual gateway/writer, provider and
adapter versions, relevant configuration,
observation/correlation method, safety envelope
and independently testable failure behavior
being relied on. “XTDB is correct” is not a
useful indivisible discharged proposition.

The same requirement applies to Ring/Biff
authentication, HTMX event/lifecycle/adoption
behavior, browser APIs and source/asset
installation, compiler and language runtime,
message delivery and other external services.
Trusted origins and observation provenance
must be established by their actual boundary.
Successful qualification tests can support a
bounded trust decision and detect regressions;
they do not establish universal provider
semantics for untested executions or versions.

A premise-qualification ledger records both
the allowed realization envelope and what
is actually observed/established. On a
provider/host change the consumer rechecks
applicability before reusing evidence. Missing
qualifications yield explicit open obligations,
a rejected strong profile, or a justified
different weaker profile—not an automatic
upgrade to a theorem. The report names
which claims now lack an applicable premise,
their dependencies and the earliest
responsible requalification or physical
enforcement boundary.

*** Positive and adversarial acceptance obligations

The finished framework's conformance corpus
must contain successful conditional and
genuinely discharged cases, alongside
negative near-neighbors. At minimum:

- One claim G uses P1 but not another exported
  claim's P2; the report attributes exactly P1.
  An unrelated premise must neither leak into G
  nor suppress a premise actually used by G.
- A checked accepted E proving P under Q
  produces G conditional on Q; copying E's
  success map or setting P's =:discharged?=
  does not produce G.
- A real direct proof of P with no open premises
  removes P from G's *open* list while retaining
  the original P-to-E audit trail. Reciprocal
  certificate justifications are rejected.
- Same-label Membership premises for Alice and
  Bob, different Organizations or transaction
  bases remain distinct; cross-use cannot be
  admitted by matching names.
- An unissued assumption description that
  matches the required structure still fails
  as purported proof. A well-formed supported
  propositional derivation retains all its
  used premise IDs rather than dropping them.
- A legitimate current Claim under an authorized
  principal and guarded XTDB incorporation has
  qualified runtime evidence; a stale pre-read
  or later current card does not establish
  historical commit attribution.
- An unchanged Organization interface with a
  replaced policy callable requires renewed
  implementation correspondence; unaffected
  abstract rule proof may remain reusable.
- A valid remote advisory and single user-Refresh
  grant support exactly their authorized reads;
  a queued second read or wrong-scope whole-board
  adoption fails at the physical permission
  or coverage boundary.
- An admitted positive progression argument
  states fair scheduling, provider availability,
  retention and user action when needed; a
  timeout or absent user action does not
  falsely establish eventual completion.
- Satisfiable abstract initialization and actual
  eligible Create/Claim paths pass independent
  non-vacuity/product gates; inconsistent
  assumptions and reject-all construction
  cannot be counted as successful product proof.
- Qualified provider version and conformance
  evidence are accepted within their envelope;
  unqualified upgrade or observed provider
  contract violation invalidates affected
  deployment claims.
- A permitted public, server-derived decision
  reaches the browser with matching provenance;
  a private field or mere schema-typed payload
  is not silently granted disclosure authority.
- A valid weaker conditional profile reports
  all its open premises. A stronger mandatory
  discharge cannot silently fall back to a
  trusted assertion or lower guarantee class.

Each test oracle must identify the proposition,
expected disposition, subject, use-time scope,
required checking/physical evidence and *first
responsible enforcing boundary* in advance.
A source-level checker is not credited with
catching a real HTMX or XTDB defect without
qualified observation. The positive controls
must continue to admit genuinely useful
Create, Claim and declared recovery/Refresh
behavior; a checker that rejects all cases
does not satisfy this design.
