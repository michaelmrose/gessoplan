## Editorial outcome and boundary

Retain R5's proposition-scoped definitions, evidence-status taxonomy,
source-derived immutable obligation ledger and semantic-interface hierarchy.
Strengthen their construction and applicability rules without promoting a
descriptive EDN report to proof, letting arbitrary private Clojure code
escape the recognized contract, or pretending a selected proof profile
establishes real XTDB/HTMX execution.

This review concerns the **timeless finished Gesso specification**;
current Gesso implementation detail belongs in this preservation
record. A reported incomplete current mechanism is not a reason
to delete a valuable future capability from normative Part IX.

## Required R4 preservation decisions

1. **Representation shape, semantic category and admission are distinct.**
   R4's `RawRuleSet`/`VerifiedRuleSet` and raw/checked observations
   are behavioral distinctions. Ordinary maps with matching fields,
   copied `:valid?` keys, purported issued record types and stale
   handles do not cross the checker or trusted acquisition boundary.
   Admission is not itself proof of everything carried by the value.
2. **Raw declaration versus proposition versus judgment.** R4's
   explicit vocabulary survives. A typed expression only asserts
   what is well formed; a proof result must state the checked
   proposition, interpretation, context, premises, subject,
   checker identity and actual result class.
3. **Assertion, assumption and guarantee never collapse.** Application
   intent may be stated or trusted. A conditional guarantee
   retains its supporting named assumptions even when a
   deployment profile permits the claim; explicit trust
   is not a machine-checked discharge.
4. **Evidence classes are not a scalar ranking.** R4's
   machine-checked properties, runtime-enforced premises and
   trusted assumptions remain distinct. R5 additionally
   distinguishes exact finite checking, assembly evidence,
   physically observed instance facts and test evidence.
   A conditional abstract proof plus a green browser test
   cannot be reported as one universal theorem.
5. **Obligation coverage differs from proof correctness.** An
   internally valid derivation for every submitted node
   may omit a required operation, affected branch, writer,
   initial state or product capability. A complete source/
   selected-profile ledger must be independently checked.
   Removing HumanHelp Reassign from the proof catalog
   cannot satisfy a product contract requiring Reassign.
6. **Schema/domain/relationship facts have limited scope.** R4's
   typed Request ID, cardinality and path declarations do
   not establish currently stored foreign entities, query
   completeness or authorized access. Trusted acquisition,
   Malli value checks and physical transaction guards are
   separately owned and scoped.
7. **A semantic interface is an exported contract, not a namespace.**
   It includes only the typed, relevant public commitments:
   inputs, outcomes, effects, refined knowledge, authority/
   disclosure, named guarantees, assumptions and identities.
   The model's private FX details do not silently become
   public authority evidence for another model.
8. **Meaning identity and concrete implementation binding differ.**
   Versioned normalized identity permits avoiding needless
   reproof after irrelevant formatting edits; changed Clojure
   implementation, callback, middleware, provider or served
   JS still requires source-to-physical correspondence evidence.
   Compatibility beyond exact identity needs a checked
   relation, not matching display names or digests.
9. **Certificates and reports are not authoritative by shape.**
   R4's candidate/checked distinction and certified-value
   public API discipline survive. A serialized file,
   signed claim or cached `:verified?` becomes trusted
   only through the relevant proof-checking/issuer and
   current dependency boundary, not because it was
   produced by an earlier trusted build.
10. **The hierarchy extends through actual application realization.**
    Schema/domain/graph/rule/FX/Core, Choreo, projection,
    ExecutablePlan, ApplicationAssembly, browser asset,
    concrete render/installed host and root report have
    different exported guarantees. Later stage construction
    does not upgrade earlier evidence without a new judgment.
11. **Opaque predicate and foreign-model policy remain visible.**
    Arbitrary Malli predicates, Clojure callbacks and external
    Organization eligibility can participate through typed
    explicitly named implementation/trust contracts. A
    single concrete check neither proves the function
    for all states nor establishes correct business intent.
12. **First-error diagnostics and positive capability.**
    A failed mandatory obligation blocks the stronger
    downstream artifact with typed explanation. A real
    valid admitted Claim, qualified opaque contract and
    current scoped read must pass alongside negative
    copied/stale candidates. A reject-all certification
    implementation is not a valid design.

## Current Gesso source correspondence — actual narrower precursors

- `src/gesso/assurance/claim.clj` implements a bounded,
  **unadmitted descriptive** claim/evidence-class vocabulary.
  `:kernel-checked`, `:runtime-enforced`, `:assembly-verified`
  and related labels are *claimed classification data* until
  separately accepted by an appropriate checker.
- `src/gesso/assurance/assumption.clj` describes named,
  subject/context/implementation-bound assumptions but
  explicitly does not discharge them; runtime current
  identity cannot be produced by a copied assumption map.
- `src/gesso/assurance/admission.clj` uses in-process,
  registry-backed structural handles for subject identity
  and premise matching. Its namespace limits those handles
  to local structural admission, not a proven business
  proposition, durable certificate or general JVM sandbox.
- `src/gesso/assurance/dependency.clj` checks exact live
  structural dependency graphs and staleness against
  current source declarations. It does not authenticate
  physical deployed host/XTDB writers.
- `src/gesso/assurance/opaque.clj` validates typed
  input/output boundaries and describes effects and
  termination for opaque Clojure/external computations.
  Its application-declared effects are not evidence
  of complete confinement or verified implementation.
- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/proposition.clj` parses
  bounded subject-bound typed propositional source; its
  outputs are unadmitted descriptions, not judgments
  that prove their own propositions.
- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/obligation.clj` derives
  an initial and a preservation obligation for each
  declared invariant from guarded replacement source.
  It does not check the implication, verify real
  initializers or certify all physical writers.
- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/operation_interface.clj`
  derives a source-bound **conditional** operation
  interface containing original guard/read/write/frame,
  dependencies and preservation status. It is a
  copyable report, not an issued runtime proof token.
- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/application_assurance_report.clj`
  composes current source-relative closure reports,
  retaining permanent open obligations for installed
  application adoption, proof-kernel soundness,
  physical writer completeness, abstract/physical
  refinement and browser/HTMX/SSE correspondence.
  Matching source and report fingerprints cannot
  discharge these external conditions.
- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/certificate.clj`,
  `src/gesso/assurance/proof/kernel.clj` and
  `src/gesso/assurance/report.clj` reinforce the
  distinction between in-process checked derivations,
  typed conditional certificate handles and copyable
  descriptive root reports. They do not provide
  a general trusted distributed certification authority.

These are source-inspection findings, **not observed results of
running Gesso's test suite**. They support refining the plan's
terminology without claiming that every finished-system
guarantee is already implemented.

## Independent positive/adversarial cases

1. Valid source-owned Claim semantic interface is admitted;
   a same-shaped raw map with `:valid? true` is refused.
2. A well-typed RequestStatus proposition passes its
   syntax/type judgment, without being claimed true for
   all current Request database documents.
3. Valid bounded conditional induction retains named
   premises; a forged `:discharged` status is rejected.
4. A changed Organization public eligibility contract
   invalidates affected Claim and downstream Choreo
   judgments even if their old source text is unchanged.
5. An irrelevant whitespace-only edit does not need
   to invalidate unrelated abstract semantic evidence;
   changed callback implementation is independently
   requalified for concrete correspondence.
6. A complete closed Claim operation catalog admits
   its selected finite obligation; removing the
   separately required Reassign capability still
   fails independent HumanHelp acceptance.
7. A real guarded read proves only scoped current
   Request/assignment facts; a copied transaction
   readback map cannot establish internal prestate,
   historical C42 cause or complete writer coverage.
8. An admitted optional/nullable Malli field cannot
   silently convert absent to present nil or
   assume all persisted records satisfy it.
9. A typed Organization graph path is valid even
   when an actually missing target Request or
   revoked Membership requires runtime rejection.
10. A matching ExecutablePlan fingerprint cannot
    certify altered emitted JS or wrong installed
    Ring route; actual build and host evidence
    remain separate gates.
11. An opaque rule can be permitted in an
    explicitly weaker profile while its
    implementation-conformance premise remains
    open; the same source cannot silently
    obtain a stronger unconditional certificate.
12. A failed proof/certificate admission names
    the original source, first unsatisfied
    obligation, expected category, open dependency
    and downstream artifact that was not issued.
13. A malformed proof is classified differently
    from a true model counterexample and
    resource-exhausted search.
14. All legitimate positives are exercised:
    rejecting every input cannot pass the
    same certification profile's conformance
    and non-vacuity gates.
