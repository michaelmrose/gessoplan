## Independent R4 preservation challenge

The original R4 Part IX has **fifteen** major second-level topics. The reconciliation assigns each to the sixteen R5.1 source units. This acceptance pass challenged especially easy-to-lose material against the revised *published* wording rather than merely trusting the crosswalk:

1. **Certified values and public Clojure API.** The supported API must not let ordinary downstream callers substitute raw same-shaped data for issuer-controlled checked classes. Opaque process-local handles have bounded threat assumptions; durable imports are inert and rechecked. This preserves R4's Clojure-specific category discipline without pretending Clojure has Rust privacy or requiring wrappers for ordinary values.

2. **Proof/kernel separation.** The executable semantic machine does not become a duplicate proof-only state machine. The elaborator cannot choose the obligations it wishes to pass. A small checking kernel or a dedicated checker establishes only its exact admitted derivation/judgment under named interpretation assumptions. The design never requires HumanHelp authors to write parallel TLA+, Quint or Recife source.

3. **Full original-source obligation coverage.** A valid smaller source/DAG may correctly describe its smaller abstraction but cannot silently qualify the original application's complete invariant/operation catalog or its actual production writer set. This survives in sections 09.05–09.07 and 09.09–09.10, including concrete adversarial pruned-Unclaim cases.

4. **Strong conditional/premise distinction.** Named open model/provider assumptions remain attached to the particular claims that use them. Real discharge requires independently admitted evidence at an appropriate scope and preserves transitive premises. Claims about a current principal, particular transaction, browser lease and progress/fairness remain distinct from pure reusable lemmas.

5. **Exact semantic versus implementation and currentness.** The R4 stale Organization-to-Claim case survives with repaired rule and checked directional compatibility; original source, emitted executable, installed host, loaded asset and ephemeral runtime resource are not represented by one misleading digest. Valid abstract proof reuse remains possible without silently requalifying changed physical code.

6. **Business-policy review versus mechanical validity.** R4's type error, unsupported use-site knowledge, valid-but-product-wrong rule and generated counterexample distinctions survive. Source-owned policy is subject to application-owner review, not arbitrary proof-kernel alteration or automatic LLM certification.

7. **Physical and user-level HumanHelp.** The pilot remains a complete obligation from real model public interfaces through Claim, Choreo, plan, production XTDB/host execution, command-specific history and permitted physical browser adoption, with paired positives. It does not replace independently required HumanHelp Create, Unclaim, Reassign, other lifecycle actions, selective advisory/Refresh behavior and the product response-flow choice.

8. **Conformance and diagnostics.** The mutation corpus's first-boundary/positive-neighbor/independent-effect requirements remain; bounded tests are accurately distinguished from universal refinement or runtime authority. First-blocker diagnostics and the final invariant remain source-relative and reviewable.

The fifteen-topic mapping is a semantic preservation audit, not a formal proof of line-by-line R4/R5 equivalence. It legitimately retains R5's stronger organization and more precise conditional construction rather than restoring every obsolete R4 example or implementation hint.

## Cross-part acceptance and still-open product decision

- **Part I:** Owns the two referenced full-language/three-way assurance anchors, with checked source, installed enforcement and independent product acceptance distinct.
- **Parts II–III:** Own the one application semantics, role-local knowledge, Choreo and projections. Part IX checks proof/admission of these semantics; it does not introduce another application source or independent browser authority machine.
- **Part IV:** Owns actual protected model transitions, stable command binding and real backend disposition. The four `:committed`, `:rejected`, `:already-incorporated`, `:commit-unknown` outcomes are not HTTP status, presentation acceptance or current card state.
- **Parts V–VI:** Own browser optimism, owner/resource retirement, authorized adoption, actual target completion and qualified continuity. A source-issued certificate cannot create an extra collection fetch or restore a retired DOM owner.
- **Part VII:** Owns closed application construction across source, routes, workers, writers, emitted/served artifacts, rendered affordances and actual installation. Part IX's declared proof graph is not a replacement for that physical source closure.
- **Part VIII:** Owns real RequestAssignment, authenticated model principal/policy, fourteen independent product completion gates and the *still unselected* `decision-humanhelp-response-flow`. Accepting a certification *design* that requires a concrete future response choice is not accepting the missing choice as made.
- **Part X:** Owns stronger formal-to-production refinement, trusted provider correspondence and multi-node realization; the newly reconciled Part IX asserts the same semantics across topologies without claiming router/failover verification.
- **Parts XI–XII:** Remain separate release, implementation-order and completed-system responsibilities, not silently satisfied by this Part IX review.

The three Part IX cross-part link targets resolve uniquely to Parts I and VIII. This acceptance therefore does not change the selected remote-advisory/one-user-Refresh product policy or manufacture an application product answer.

## Actual implementation/assurance obligations still open

The accepted future-system design remains stronger than Gesso v1622's inspected source evidence. Important qualifications deliberately kept open include:

- Independently justified proof kernel and checker semantics, general checked hypothetical-context premise discharge, proof/implementation conformance and non-vacuous original-source obligation coverage over every requested semantic family.
- All relevant real host, dynamic route, background/initializer, raw/external XTDB writer and physical dependency census; current transaction-internal guard and precise effect causality beyond bounded in-process/pinned-readback observations.
- Actual issued executor adoption by deployed Ring/Biff, source-owned request principal/target mapping, correct public Request/RequestAssignment policy, loaded browser plan/asset identity and physical HTMX/OOB target installation.
- Complete physical concurrent Claim/Unclaim/Reassign safety and durable original-command reconciliation under losses, retries, retention and unrelated later writes, without conflating a command's past with the current Request card.
- Correct advisory-only multi-client remote state, creator-local selective Create, one user-authorized Refresh, local cohort search/sorting and continuity, and all independent positive HumanHelp browser/product gates. The response-flow product choice remains unselected.
- Qualified external XTDB, Clojure/JVM, compiler, HTMX, browser, provider, fairness and multi-node replay/failover assumptions; correct scoping of actual tests versus all-schedules and liveness conclusions.
- Full demonstration of the mutation/independent physical-effect oracle corpus and genuine developer experience across supported profiles, not just definitions of test cases or copyable successful reports.

These open implementation/product tasks **do not block acceptance of the design as a normative target**, because the document states their required witnesses, failure modes and evidence categories without misrepresenting current Gesso as complete. They **do block** any stronger current-implementation, deployed-system or complete HumanHelp product certificate.
