## Editorial decision

R5's streamlined compositional mutation taxonomy
is the preferred normative organization. Restore
R4's precise baseline/isolated-change/expected
gate/independent-effects runner contract and
usable schematic fixture examples. A mutation
corpus should *discriminate* between authentic
correctness and failure at the supported source,
checker, issuer, dependency, host and physical
boundaries; rejecting everything is not soundness
or product acceptance.

Each case is both a valid neighboring control and
one challenged assertion of a specific guarantee.
First-boundary attribution and independent
effect observation must survive even where a
particular runtime implementation uses different
internal error names or cache paths.

## R4 preservation and strengthened decisions

1. **Multiple independently valid baselines.**
   Tiny closed proof terms are necessary for
   checker conformance but not a substitute for
   full application source, genuine HumanHelp
   Claim and real installed browser/model control.

2. **Case oracle precedes the mutant.**
   Record the original source, affected
   proposition, injection point, expected
   status/reason, first enforcing boundary
   and prohibited construct/effect *before*
   exercise. Unexpected acceptance cannot
   retroactively become a business-policy
   exception.

3. **Physical and proof failures differ.**
   A malformed Choreo source should fail its
   source checker; a forged browser principal
   or direct XTDB writer may require trusted
   ingress/atomic model enforcement. Test
   the boundary that can actually see it.

4. **Original source mutation is unadmitted.**
   Mutate fresh normal declarations, not an
   already registered opaque certificate.
   Forgery instead edits persisted inert
   package candidate bytes before
   deserialization; physical host substitution
   occurs at its own installation/effect point.

5. **Valid upstream changes need not be errors.**
   An O15 Organization closed-enum
   replacement may be coherent while
   existing Claim C21 cannot compose against
   it. Direct checker and production cache
   paths may fail at different genuine
   first boundaries; neither may install
   stale C21 under the new source.

6. **Schema/typing mutation breadth survives.**
   Nominal endpoint/cardinality, optional
   versus nullable/missing, closed-world
   variants and ordered enums require
   positive and negative typed judgments.

7. **Rule meaning is tested, not string shape.**
   Short-circuit refinement, nil/absence,
   integer bounds, reader footprints and
   simultaneous guarded assignments need
   discriminating independent input states
   or justified static rejection.

8. **Kernel and induction mutation breadth.**
   Incorrect inference steps, premise
   propagation, partial derivations,
   omitted source operations/invariants
   and vacuous initialization cannot
   issue stronger evidence. Contradictory
   assumptions and incomplete finite search
   retain distinct classifications.

9. **Source-required closure is separate.**
   A structurally valid pruned dependency
   DAG can pass generic graph checking
   while failing original declared
   operation coverage. A generic DAG
   success is not a positive proof
   of the full application.

10. **Knowledge/authority are point-indexed.**
    Browser role labels, private fields,
    stale observation and unsupported
    receive paths cannot inherit
    authority or branch-local knowledge
    through a copied proof result.

11. **Premise substitution remains scoped.**
    A source-checked conditional proof
    still carries its used assumptions;
    another principal/Request/basis,
    or a copied boolean `:discharged?`,
    cannot erase them.

12. **Physical writer and history are not
    source-only facts.** Real C42 commit
    versus duplicate retry, protected
    Request/assignment guard, unknown
    delivery, writer interference and
    command readback require actual
    privileged-effect observation or
    explicit trust qualification.

13. **Browser tests originate in browser.**
    Injecting a synthetic wire command
    is a valid lower-level fixture
    but does not demonstrate that a
    real rendered control emitted
    the needed plan/transport envelope.
    One authorized Refresh is
    not two reads; OOB completion
    matters for actual adoption.

14. **Independent instrumentation is
    mandatory for negative effects.**
    A test/mutant's own report cannot
    testify that it did no write or
    target installation. Absent or
    unqualified instrumentation yields
    incomplete test, not proof of
    prevention. Post-hoc detection
    is not pre-effect enforcement.

15. **Determinism is part of the corpus.**
    Fresh source, cache/issuer and
    browser/XTDB isolation, reproducible
    bounded corpus seeds, controlled
    concurrent scheduling and stable
    diagnostic priority avoid reporting
    accidental fixture drift as semantic
    regression or mutant kill.

16. **Positive controls protect usability.**
    Eligible Claim, Unclaim and Reassign,
    genuine repaired C22, supported
    conditional opaque contracts,
    coherent product policy edits and
    semantically equivalent source changes
    must be admitted where allowed.

17. **Unsupported and incomplete are
    not successful checks.** Timeout,
    unknown provider/host, failed
    test setup or unsupported
    formal feature cannot increase
    the observed correctness of a
    stronger requested claim. A test
    may pass by correctly *rejecting*
    unsupported strong admission but
    must not claim the capability exists.

18. **Causality remains independently
    qualified.** One test-local XTDB
    collector or matching ledger
    records may support a bounded
    witness, not a theorem of
    deployed all-writer causal
    completeness, atomic prestate
    or browser/refinement safety.

19. **Application truth stays outside
    mechanical proof inference.**
    A coherent age threshold or
    manager policy changed by its
    owner can be intentionally
    accepted despite heuristic
    suspicion. Removing a promised
    HumanHelp operation fails
    independent product acceptance,
    even if the reduced proof catalog
    becomes internally safe.

20. **Coverage reporting is scoped.**
    Kill rate, validation suite count,
    source files visited and test
    assertions passed are not
    proof-kernel soundness or
    source-to-physical universality.
    Every conclusion carries the
    actually tested subjects
    and evidence classes.

## Gesso v1622 implementation evidence

The existing code has real separate mutation
families, not one production-grade
general-purpose end-to-end mutation runner:

- `test/gesso/assurance/proof/kernel_systematic_mutation_test.clj`
  contains eight valid propositional sources,
  deterministic generated perturbations
  (>600 cases asserted in the source),
  a test-local independent finite Boolean
  valuation oracle, step/goal rejection
  checks and the exact preserved premise
  IDs. It explicitly disclaims soundness
  metatheorem, typed predicate truth and
  XTDB/browser behavior.

- `test/gesso/assurance/proof/kernel_typed_atom_evaluator_mutation_test.clj`
  and `kernel_typed_atom_normalization_mutation_test.clj`
  exercise independent typed denotation,
  present/nil and short-circuit logic,
  mutation of the real evaluator/checking
  seam and selected source/normalized
  correspondence. The evaluator
  mutation test itself says finite
  diagnostic disagreement is not
  universal CLJS/JVM equivalence or
  transaction authority.

- `test/gesso/assurance/proof/application_dependency_mutation_test.clj`
  includes the decisive source-coverage
  mutant: a structurally valid generic
  DAG missing `:request/unclaim` source
  nodes passes generic graph checking
  but source-derived original catalog
  checking rejects
  `:source-dependency-coverage-mismatch`.
  It also tests changed premises,
  cycles, roots and stale source.

- `test/gesso/assurance/proof/application_writer_coverage_mutation_test.clj`
  examines declared/issued/host-observed
  writer disagreements and explicitly
  keeps physical completeness premises
  open. A simulated source/host list
  is not independently verified
  actual deployed writer coverage.

- `test/gesso/assurance/proof/physical_xtdb_transition_provenance_mutation_test.clj`
  injects synthetic collector/provenance
  defects and includes an embedded XTDB
  commit/rejection test. Its source
  explicitly limits the local origin
  witness to the pinned controlled
  collector/JVM and disclaims
  independent production-node
  authentication, deployed handler
  adoption and universal physical
  transaction receipt.

- `test/gesso/assurance/proof/induction_premise_mutation_test.clj`,
  `guarded_transition_mutation_test.clj`,
  `operation_interface_provenance_mutation_test.clj`
  and `issued_executor_plan_mutation_test.clj`
  cover additional narrow rule,
  assumption, operation and issued-
  plan mutation boundaries.
  Their individual source tests do
  not in aggregate establish a
  single admitted source-to-installed
  Gesso/HumanHelp universal theorem.

The inspected repository contains useful
test harness and independent oracle
design precedents. Their assertions are
not newly executed passing test results.
No claim is made that the full Part VIII
multi-user, fault-injected Chromium,
actual deployed Biff/XTDB and
external-writer acceptance matrix
has passed. This remains required
for the finished framework and
proving application.

## Positive/adversarial acceptance pairs

1. **Schema field:** Original Request
   and typed status admit. Negative:
   field removed before source
   checking gives source error.
2. **Valid new schema:** An O15 enum
   composes with repaired C22.
   Negative: old C21 cannot
   be admitted through stale
   dependent evidence.
3. **Reader semantics:** Present
   nil and absent remain
   distinct under valid
   guarded source. Negative:
   truthiness/short-circuit
   mutant changes effect or
   reader footprint undetected.
4. **Kernel proof:** Genuine
   derivation and exact premise
   set accepted. Negative:
   fabricated proof step/goal
   passes despite independent
   Boolean counterexample.
5. **Induction source:** All
   original operations and
   invariants checked. Negative:
   pruned Unclaim source
   hides behind a valid DAG.
6. **Conditional premise:** E
   justifies P under Q and
   downstream inherits Q.
   Negative: P is marked
   unconditionally
   discharged by a copied map.
7. **Role knowledge:** Browser
   branches on authorized
   public decision. Negative:
   private uncommunicated
   discriminator is used
   without provenance.
8. **Command outcome:** C42
   compatible replay returns
   a true historical result.
   Negative: later Request
   snapshot invents
   incorporation or
   second submission.
9. **XTDB guard:** Actual
   protected Claim mutation
   preserves one primary.
   Negative: separate
   pre-read permits competing
   unauthorized assignments.
10. **Writer inventory:** Named
    host paths are accounted
    for within the observed
    scope. Negative: unknown
    raw/external writer is
    falsely reported
    impossible.
11. **Issuer handle:** Original
    source issuance works.
    Negative: same-shaped
    report or constructed
    object becomes admission.
12. **Durable input:** Safe
    bounded proof package
    is rechecked. Negative:
    executable reader tag,
    missing proof edge or
    forged signature is
    accepted as theorem.
13. **Browser origin:** Real
    rendered enabled Claim
    emits generated command.
    Negative: manually
    injected test envelope
    is credited as browser
    operation.
14. **Target adoption:** Eligible
    scoped card HTMX update
    installs visibly. Negative:
    HTTP 200, late retired
    owner or wrong OOB
    target counts as
    completed adoption.
15. **One Refresh:** User
    grants exactly one
    scoped collection read.
    Negative: remote
    advisory or local
    Create generates
    ungranted further
    acquisition.
16. **Opaque provider:** An
    explicitly accepted
    conditional model
    contract retains its
    premise. Negative:
    unsupported strict
    proof auto-downgrades
    to a trusted success.
17. **Physical provenance:**
    One controlled collector
    can issue its bounded
    observation. Negative:
    fabricated node/second
    diagnostic transaction
    is claimed to prove
    original request
    causality.
18. **Positive production:**
    Eligible Claim and
    selected other lifecycle
    actions work. Negative:
    reject-all baseline,
    permanently disabled
    Reassign or reduced
    semantic catalog
    qualifies as product.
19. **Business intent:** Coherent
    owner-approved policy
    change remains an
    accepted control.
    Negative: heuristic
    suspicion alone
    triggers unsanctioned
    proof rejection
    or source rewrite.
20. **Harness completeness:**
    Controlled expected
    mutant rejection and
    valid baseline both
    observed. Negative:
    missing effect probe,
    timeout or fixture
    contamination is
    counted as a kill.
21. **Scope reporting:** A finite
    evaluator mismatch is
    reported as such.
    Negative: bounded
    checked trace called
    deployed universal
    refinement.
22. **No silent downgrade:**
    Source/proof/host
    unsupported condition
    blocks a stronger
    requested artifact.
    Negative: anonymous
    ordinary HTMX fallback
    executes a semantic
    operation with no
    admitted source/plan.

Every case is tied to its
real checking or physical
observation boundary.
The positive controls
are not optional. The
expected result and first
enforcement point are
recorded before the mutant
is introduced. Host and
effect diagnostics without
independent provenance
remain incomplete, not
successfully prevented.

## Editorial outcome

09.15 retains R5's manageable
semantic mutation families
while recovering R4's
detailed runner/injection/
independent-oracle contract.
It adds source-relative
completeness, separate
transaction causality,
concurrent execution,
browser-originated
command and selective
adoption cases that
reflect accepted R5.1
Parts IV–VIII and
reviewed 09.01–09.14.

**Nonclaims:** The existing
Gesso test sources were
inspected, not run; no new
HumanHelp production
mutation suite, real
XTDB/Chromium acceptance
result, universal
formal soundness theorem
or current all-application
coverage is asserted.
