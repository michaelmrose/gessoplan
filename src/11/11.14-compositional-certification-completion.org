*** Compositional certification completion
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: milestone-compositional-certification
:END:

The compositional-certification milestone establishes a *working,
non-vacuous and source-complete chain of specified semantic judgments*
for its declared artifact classes, operation catalog, application
scope and assurance profile. It is neither a generic =:certified=
flag nor a promise to prove all Clojure programs. The authoritative
definitions of each judgment, its proposition, issuer/checker
boundary and dependency obligations are in Part IX; the
production-correspondence frontier is in Part X.

A completion decision must name both what was genuinely checked
and what remains conditional. A verified derivation, a conditional
abstract induction, a qualified model executor, an installed
handler, an observed browser interaction and an independently
proved checker meta-theorem are *different* claims. Their evidence
can be linked through correctly established relations, never
identified merely because the application report lists them
under one operation name.

*** Complete the supported source-to-judgment chain

For the selected supported artifact and operation classes the
milestone requires the following actual capabilities:

| Semantic boundary | Required operational result | Not established by that result alone |
|-------------------+-----------------------------+--------------------------------------|
| Typed rule construction | Closed versioned syntax, value types, short-circuit semantics, safe presence/refinement reads, interpretation and conservative normalization | Correctness of an arbitrary opaque Clojure callback or the desired business policy |
| Typed rule and guarded-operation judgments | Exact original source, input/reader paths, permitted transition and frame obligations with typed rejection of malformed cases | That actual XTDB writers implement those transitions |
| Distributed use-site admissibility | Role and program-point knowledge, provenance, disclosure, authority, refinement, progression and resource rights are checked at their source-owned uses | That a queued transport observation was authorized, delivered or adopted by a real browser |
| Global/projection/executable-plan relations | Projection and lowering are tied to the original Choreo subject, selected observable behavior and checking scope | Unconditional physical browser/HTMX/SSE trace refinement |
| Proof kernel and dedicated checkers | The admitted closed inference rules and required semantic judgments are actually checked, with recognized unknown/unsupported cases | Independent soundness of the implementation of the proof kernel itself |
| Conditional invariant/induction checking | Checked initial-state and preservation obligations for each invariant and required operation in the declared catalog | Complete physical writer coverage or all real initial states without separate evidence |
| Source-derived obligation and dependency closure | Original declarations determine every required proposition, branch, producer, dependency and premise for the selected profile | A graph supplied by its own claimant is complete merely because it is acyclic |
| Issuance, consumption and currentness | A genuine checked result is bound to the exact proposition, source, profile, checker versions and applicable dependency generation | Permission to run a model command, install a DOM target or infer a deployed release |
| HumanHelp real Claim pilot | One substantial public-model Claim produces traceable semantic, construction and qualified production/browser evidence | Every HumanHelp product capability or universal deployed-system refinement |

A checker implementation may fuse these stages or use several
focused namespaces. Completion concerns their observable
semantics and trusted boundaries, not a mandatory number of
compiler passes, a required external proof language or a
particular public function name. Arbitrary host-language
implementation remains available through an explicit,
profile-permitted implementation premise; an unsupported
closed judgment cannot silently become a proved theorem.

*** Require independent source completeness and useful positives

The accepting boundary obtains the *original* model, rule,
operation, choreography, projection and application
declarations with their actual source owners, relevant
imports and independent required-capability inventory.
A proposed derivation does not get to define the set
of propositions it must prove. Required obligations
include every supported operation and applicable branch,
read/write/frame condition, role-local use, required
initial state, guarded outcome, resource exit, relevant
dependency and expressly selected invariant.

Obligation and dependency coverage is checked independently
against that original source. A cyclic proof-justification
graph is invalid; an acyclic graph that omits Reassign,
the rejection branch, an original Organization rule or
an opaque implementation dependency is *also* invalid
for a claim that requires those subjects. Runtime
choreography loops are not automatically circular proofs.
An admitted abstract subset may legitimately pass only
when its narrower subject and exclusions are explicit;
it does not meet a larger original application
denominator by changing the reported label.

Certification is not satisfied vacuously by an
unsatisfiable initial-state specification, a rule
checker that rejects every Claim, unreachable
user-facing positive operations, or an inconsistent
collection of admitted premises. Where the selected
claim requires inhabited initialization, reachable
allowed transitions or real participant usefulness,
these are separately checked or explicitly qualified.
Correct rejection is paired with an eligible positive
under the same chosen checker and application
boundary. Product policy remains independently
owned: a sound derivation can enforce a coherent
but unintended HumanHelp rule.

*** Distinguish derivation, assumption and proof soundness

The small kernel checks exact propositional or other
supported derivations under their named hypotheses.
Dedicated checkers establish their own defined
typing, guarded-transition, finite-model, induction,
distributed or composition judgments. A developer
or elaborator cannot issue a certificate merely
by returning =:valid?=, =:proved=, a matching hash,
a copied checker summary or a self-described
dependency graph.

A checked derivation says that its conclusion follows
from the named premises under the *specified calculus*
and the implementation's checker trust contract.
It does not prove that every atomic business predicate
holds in live Request data, that the selected business
policy is desirable, or that the kernel's own algorithm
is independently sound. Independent kernel soundness
and production-to-formal checker correspondence are
separate external formal obligations of 11.15.
When that stronger scope is selected they cannot be
satisfied by local passing fixture examples alone.

For conditional induction, each declared invariant
requires a check of the intended initial-state
condition and preservation under **every declared
operation** in the required catalog, with the
actual transition, simultaneous writes, readable
prestate and unchanged frame interpretation.
The source-derived catalog cannot shrink when a
difficult writer fails. Whether the specified
initial states include *all physical initializers*
and whether *all actual physical writers* refine
the declared operations remain separate qualified
claims. A valid conditional theorem must continue
to display those premises instead of becoming an
unconditional domain invariant.

Assumptions are propagated along the particular
accepted justification for the guarantee consumed.
A genuine discharge is a checked substitution or
other admitted entailment from evidence of the
*same proposition and compatible context*, including
source, parameter, principal, authority, temporal
basis, implementation and issuer boundaries.
Replacing an open P with evidence of P under Q leaves
Q open where applicable. A caller-supplied
=:discharged?= flag, identical premise label or
different tenant's authorization does not remove P.
Checked premise identities, dependencies and
rejected substitutions remain inspectable.

*** Bind real issuance and safe evidence reuse

The ordinary construction path checks source shape,
typing, elaboration, coverage, dependency closure,
proof rules, premise policy and coherent currentness
before issuing a source-bound, typed admitted product
for the exact supported claim. A human-readable root
report is derived from the same genuine checked
records but is not an issuer or authorization
capability. A raw map or test fixture can describe
a proposition; it cannot impersonate the issuer
of a checked proof or an admitted executor.

Process-local handles require their intended current
issuer/registry and dependency context. Any durable
serialized package is treated as untrusted input
until decoded and independently re-admitted or
authenticated and rechecked under its declared
threat, rule-version, source and premise model.
A signature can authenticate attributed bytes under
its trust contract without proving the signed
business proposition. Reuse is valid only when the
exact source and applicable semantic/checker
dependencies remain qualified; changes to
normalization, trust assumptions, domain rules,
issued executor, backend, compiled artifact or
installed route invalidate the *affected kinds*
of evidence. A reusable abstract theorem does
not requalify changed physical implementations.

The application assembly may consume the admitted
result only for its matching selected operation,
plan and source generation. The trusted command,
installed Ring/HTMX and browser boundaries separately
enforce current authority, execution correspondence
and physical resource ownership. Certificates and
optional sidecars do not determine runtime business
transition outcomes or grant permission to replay
a logical command. Mandatory construction/installation
gates do retain actual enforcement authority over
their own stage; those gates do not derive it from
caller-editable reporting fields.

*** Certify substantial HumanHelp Claim, not a toy surrogate

The mandatory pilot under [[#req-humanhelp-certification-pilot]]
uses the *actual public production model* ownership
for User, Membership, Organization, Location,
Request and RequestAssignment where those dependencies
matter. It does not invent =:request/helper-id= or
rename the completed lifecycle state to
=:completed= to simplify a model. The Request status
vocabulary includes =:done=, and the active
primary assignment is a separately guarded
authoritative fact.

The Claim proof/evidence chain includes:

1. An authenticated acting helper and exact Request
   target under current model/Organization/Membership
   eligibility, with the target-helper distinction
   represented where the operation requires it.
2. Request lifecycle guards, primary-assignment
   existence/absence and cardinality, permitted
   cross-model dependencies and all original
   relevant invariant and operation contracts.
3. The abstract guarded multi-record transition,
   its same-transaction atomicity requirement,
   write footprint, negative outcomes and frame
   obligations under the selected model.
4. Stable logical command C42 and exact canonical
   binding, with independent transport attempt
   identity, compatible replay and historical
   result disclosure, and honest submitted-unknown
   semantics rather than invented rejection.
5. Required durable publication intent/outbox
   obligations, guarded writer coverage, other
   lifecycle and administrative entrypoints,
   actual initialization assumptions and
   physical backend correspondence limits.
6. Choreo roles and knowledge/disclosure placement,
   source-derived projections and executable plans
   for committed, rejected, already-incorporated
   and commit-unknown outcomes.
7. The actual selected application assembly,
   issued executor, rendered enabled Claim
   affordance, HTMX route and checked browser
   asset/response and resource ownership relations.
8. Independently qualified real production
   transaction and browser/user observations
   for the stronger operational-realization
   claim, with scope, physical premises and
   unresolved gaps explicitly recorded.

A one-scalar Request-state induction or a
Clojure map named =:claim/proof= cannot establish
multi-record RequestAssignment correctness,
actual transaction guards, command attribution,
full writer closure, source-to-executor refinement
or the real browser user path. Conversely, a
genuine conditional source-relative Claim result
is useful and may be issued under a profile
that exposes its unproved physical edges
without inventing a stronger certification.

The pilot's positive control permits an eligible
helper to Claim a Request under the public model.
Competing helpers and stale role/organization
authority must not create two primaries; forged
client identity cannot become server authority.
An unknown C42 incorporation remains separate
from later current Request state. Alice's own
result and Bob's remote advisory/one-user-Refresh
board acquisition are not conflated. The pilot
does not independently complete Unclaim, Reassign,
Response or the rich board: the Part VIII
original product denominator is unchanged.

*** Require positive and adversarial conformance

| Eligible checked behavior | Invalid adjacent case that must fail or remain explicitly conditional |
|---------------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Well-typed valid business rule has the intended exact reader/presence semantics | Nil and absent input, short-circuit branch or opaque function is normalized as if it were proved equal |
| Closed checker proves supported conclusion from the real premise list | Unknown rule, forward proof reference or fabricated =:proved= flag is accepted |
| Source-derived obligation closure includes all original Claim/operation branches | A structurally sound graph omits the unfavorable guard or Reassign from claimed full catalog |
| Valid initializer and enabled Claim provide non-vacuous required controls | Unsatisfiable initial state or reject-all system is presented as product certification |
| Real invariant induction uses every required declared transition | Failed operation is removed from the operation catalog to keep old induction green |
| Genuine E establishing P is composed into G with correct inherited assumptions | Same-name P under another actor/basis or evidence E under Q silently discharges all premises |
| A valid typed role-local use receives its qualified allowed knowledge | Forged or stale transported claim is treated as authoritative rule-use permission |
| Exact source and checked plan bind to issued admitted artifact | Elaborator-generated proof report is copied into a certificate-looking map |
| Current checked source-relative evidence is reused only under valid dependencies | Changed rule/normalizer, source or proof checker inherits stale success |
| Process-local certificate or durable package is re-admitted under its real issuer policy | Serialized success map or self-signed report becomes an executable permit |
| Real public Request and RequestAssignment Claim contract is the proof subject | Toy scalar Claim or invented =:request/helper-id= counts as production proof |
| Concurrent helper Claim obeys one-primary invariant under selected guards | Both helpers win because only pre-read tests were run |
| Genuine C42 retains binding, authorized result and unknown-outcome distinctions | Lost response is relabeled rejection, fresh C43 sent, or current card asserted to prove C42 |
| Every required authoritative writer/initializer is covered or expressly open | Abstract catalog is called the complete physical XTDB writer universe |
| Browser Claim uses the actual issued plan and resource owner where claimed | Passing synthetic HTTP call certifies generated, loaded browser semantics |
| Conditional proof retains backend and runtime premises in root evidence | Root green status converts unproved physical refinement into theorem |
| Legitimate selected opaque Clojure implementation remains usable with honest premise | Mandatory proof silently turns into an unchecked trusted assertion |
| Independent true product capabilities are still named and positively exercised | Claim pilot completion deletes Response and other missing HumanHelp features |

The actual checking method, first rejecting
boundary, proposition, selected subject,
accepted premise, positive oracle and
negative counterexample are declared for
each required case. An abstract checker
cannot claim to have observed an actual
XTDB or Chromium effect merely because
its fixture passed. A real browser trace
cannot establish an unbounded inductive
claim merely because one user interaction
worked.

*** Milestone decision and boundaries

Completion requires a non-vacuous checked
proof/certificate chain through the
selected semantic artifact classes
and at least one real source-owned
multi-record HumanHelp Claim pilot.
Every mandatory obligation in that
milestone's original source-derived
denominator is either checked or
admissibly conditional under its
selected profile; missing,
unsupported, stale or unexecuted
mandatory checks remain blockers.
The issuer records the exact scope,
premises, checker/proof kinds, tested
positive controls, applicable sources
and evidence destinations.

The claim ledger distinguishes (a)
source-relative typing, judgments and
proof derivations; (b) checked issuance
and application composition; (c)
independent external checker-soundness
theorems; (d) abstract-to-physical
transaction and writer correspondence;
(e) installed host/browser execution
and product acceptance. They cannot
be reported as one automatically
discharged =:certified= state.

The milestone does not require proof
of arbitrary host-language code or
the full externally verified kernel
of 11.15 unless the selected profile
explicitly includes that stronger
duty. It also does not require all
HumanHelp product capabilities of
11.13 to have passed simply because
the Claim certification pilot is
complete. The genuine original
capability and proof catalogs remain
visible as separate required
denominators. The broader external
theorem and physical deployment
obligations are owned by 11.15,
Part X and their selected release
profiles.

The executable semantic core remains
the runtime semantic source. The
proof kernel checks derivations,
dedicated checkers establish named
judgments, and the issuer binds
their admissible conclusions to
exact subjects. None becomes a
second source of business rules,
a general runtime authorization
machine or a passive proof sidecar
whose text controls domain outcomes.
