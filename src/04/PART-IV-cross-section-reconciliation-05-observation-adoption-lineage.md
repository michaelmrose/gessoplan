## Cross-section finding

A board acquisition at an admitted frontier and its subsequent physical
whole-target installation are separate judgments. A P18 board read can be
complete for the declared Location cohort and satisfy O1's *acquisition*
requirement while an installed P19 creator-local Request card S has newer,
separately admitted evidence. A naive P18 HTML replacement may erase S,
its selectively adopted membership and its required "Your new Request"
confirmation. A monotonicity check on one broad board basis does not
independently prevent that per-card regression.

The opposite shortcut—claiming that P18 full-cohort data plus S/P19 yields
a complete P19 board snapshot—is also unsound. The scopes, temporal evidence
and authorization provenance remain distinct.

## Repairs made

- **04.12:** the admitted acquired observation binds executed query, actual
  coverage, visibility, basis and candidate. Copying basis metadata onto
  unrelated or partial HTML does not establish a matching read or completion.
- **04.13:** bundle satisfaction preserves separate full-board and
  card-level subjects. A P18 cohort plus P19 card is not complete P19
  collection knowledge and cannot discharge unmatched P20 board debt.
- **04.15:** installation permission considers actual DOM write/replace
  footprint. A whole-board swap cannot erase independently adopted
  newer card/confirmation or be called semantic non-regression.
- **04.16:** one explicitly granted A1/P18 read racing the author's
  confirmed S/P19 is a normative HumanHelp acceptance history.
  The system may selectively install the P18 base cohort while retaining
  S/P19, or defer unsafe replacement. No ungranted second board read.
- **04.17:** paired controls require independent observation of
  installed DOM, query-to-candidate provenance, local visibility,
  O1/O2 debt and read-grant accounting.

## R4/R5 and implementation correspondence

R4 distinguished advisory knowledge from current authoritative contents and
did not allow a later local observation to certify unrelated collection
membership. Accepted R5.1 Part VIII defines the complete Location cohort,
remote updates only on explicit Refresh, immediate correlated creator result
and an accessible confirmation even under an excluding local search filter.
This pass preserves that product policy while adding an explicit physical
non-regression and provenance join; no universal per-fact storage design is
prescribed.

Inspected Gesso `application-assurance` v1622:
`gesso.live.browser.adapter` retains a confirmed installed basis for a
logical scope after a correlated HTMX after-swap and checks monotonicity
before allowing a swap; `gesso.live.browser.observation` records installed
events. Neither source independently demonstrates that arbitrary whole-board
HTML preserves separately newer card/membership or the actual "Your new
Request" result area. The finished system must establish these claims under
its qualified physical realization. No Gesso/browser/XTDB tests or machine
proofs were executed during this editorial revision.
