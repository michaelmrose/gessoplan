# R5.1 Part II — 02.01 Values and Evolving Values: R4 preservation audit

**Baseline:** canonical R4 on `main`, Part II, "Values and evolving values".
**Candidate:** `r5.1`, `02.01-values-and-evolving-values.org`.
**Status:** section-level normative comparison only, not implementation proof
or completed Part II acceptance. The original `r5` candidate remains unchanged.

## Preserved R4 requirements

- Immutable observations, revised interpretations, supersession, provenance,
  causal/time relations, and valid-time-aware current views survive.
- The semantic graph of facts does not require a universal persisted fact DAG.
  HumanHelp's public models/XTDB remain authoritative for their owned facts.
- Representation equality does not establish semantic equality of authority,
  provenance, basis, principal, protocol version, or admission.
- Shape, nominal semantic category, actual admission, and proved proposition
  remain distinct; records/tags do not automatically confer proof or authority.
- Browser-carried reconstructed values cannot reproduce server-side admission
  by choosing a class, tag, copied issuance ID, or serialized bytes. Trusted
  issuance/revalidation/reacquisition is required at the next use boundary.
- Real publication and backend consistency are not proved by a type wrapper.
- The R4 employee-observation example's principal and purpose binding,
  server-issuer trust, current policy recheck, potential cross-host verification,
  and requirement to protect eventual mutation at incorporation survive in
  R5's more compact combined Expense/Employee acquisition example.
- The author's ordinary path remains an observation contract and Clojure
  functions rather than manual construction of three Employee wrapper types.

## This revision's narrow corrections

1. **Omission versus established negative.** A field missing from a partial
   projection is not known absent. Absence of a modeled optional field needs
   an applicable completed schema/read/guard judgment. Missing required data,
   denied/incomplete/insufficient-basis reads, and unknown facts are distinct.
   This aligns with Parts IV and IX and preserves R4's negative-evidence intent.
2. **Production Claim semantics.** F2's "claimed by user U" remains a semantic
   historical description, not an invented physical Request helper field:
   HumanHelp's relationship is modeled by RequestAssignment (Part VIII).
3. **Illustrative typed source.** The example's `Money` type and field-path
   `:bindings` forms do not assert that Part IX's baseline reader grammar
   directly admits arbitrary nominal money types or such field paths. A real
   checked claim needs explicitly supported representation and checked
   elaboration, or named conditional implementation premises.
4. **Joint observation consistency.** Individually valid Expense and Employee
   snapshots at incompatible bases do not establish the stated relationship
   or one coherent combined authoritative observation.

## Deferred cross-part verification

Part IV owns concrete XTDB basis and physical transaction/retention semantics;
Part VIII owns public HumanHelp production relationships; Part IX owns typed
IR and source-to-judgment admission; Part X owns implementation correspondence.
Later Part II sections may require a revisiting correction. R4's extensive
manual Employee wrapper/registry code is not restored because it would
weaken the superior R5 authoring boundary; its acceptance obligations have
been preserved instead.

No R4 normative guarantee was intentionally removed. These source-level
conclusions do not prove that any named example API is implemented.
