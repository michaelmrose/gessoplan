# R5.1 Part II — 02.09 Knowledge

**Baseline:** R4 `main`, Part II, “Knowledge.”
**Candidate:** `r5.1` source section 02.09.
**Status:** focused R4 preservation audit; not a runtime qualification,
formal theorem about physical executions, or whole-Part-II acceptance.

## R4 normative requirements retained

- Knowledge is relative to an actor/role and justified by provenance, not by
  a map key, packet, or successfully decoded value.
- All five provenance families remain: input, communication, authoritative
  observation, derivation, and explicitly accepted assertion.
- Required input knowledge follows the admitted entry contract; a browser
  cannot turn a supplied principal ID into trusted principal knowledge.
- Communication must justify sender knowledge and the declared receive;
  open payload extras do not silently become semantic knowledge.
- Authoritative knowledge may come from a qualified representation, including
  server-rendered HTML, with authority and observation premises intact.
- Deterministic derivation must state its inputs and semantic contract;
  arbitrary opaque Clojure does not prove cross-role equality.
- Asserted facts remain visible trusted premises, never automatically upgraded
  to proved derivation, communication, or authoritative observation.
- Roles need adequate knowledge to choose branches, send/consume facts,
  derive values, enter operations, construct provisional states, and claim
  agreement; privacy may deliberately prevent dissemination.
- Checker explanations retain derivation support; a bare `{:knows? true}`
  cannot substitute for inspectable evidence.

## Stronger R5 treatment preserved

- Knowledge is an explicitly scoped judgment: proposition, subject,
  authority, projection/query/temporal context, observation basis, principal
  context, support dependencies, and applicable lifetime.
- Possessing an input value is distinct from knowing a domain proposition
  about it. Sender authenticity is distinct from truth of its statement.
- Dependent current knowledge can become inapplicable after correction,
  replacement, revocation, or loss of an assertion without erasing history.
- Opaque and effectful derivations retain unknown proof premises; result
  comparison is insufficient proof of purity or deterministic correspondence.
- Historical knowledge is separate from current permission to disclose,
  retain, operate on, or forward the information.
- Source-derived diagnostic transitions are not proof that the physical
  browser, network, or database executed as reported.

## Changes in this revision

1. Mark the symbolic C/R/B/V fixture and session label as explanatory data,
   not acquired authority, authenticated principals or runtime credentials.
2. Separate proof of role-local key availability from proof of scoped
   proposition truth, derivation soundness, and physical implementation
   correspondence. Each stronger claim retains its own obligation.
3. Add nine concise negative cases for asserted provenance, undeclared
   transport keys, message truth, path-dependent branches, cross-subject
   evidence reuse, incomplete queries, opaque derivation, nonportable
   cross-actor computation, and permission changes.
4. Distinguish missing knowledge from scope/authority, opaque premise, and
   authorization failures rather than folding them into one positive or
   negative generic outcome.

## Current Gesso correspondence and limitations

- `gesso.choreo.knowledge` contains portable role-local knowledge and the
  five provenance kinds, including explicit authoritative observation
  progression witnesses. Its data predicates and constructors do not
  independently authenticate the provenance they record.
- `gesso.choreo.verify` performs definite protocol-value, role-knowledge
  and definite-authoritative must-analyses over the admitted choreography.
  Its own declared exclusions include provenance authenticity, progression
  ordering, full realization, projection refinement and browser execution.
- `gesso.choreo.proof` verifies finite compiler/projection structural
  properties and deliberately does not issue a general knowledge-soundness
  or dynamic trace-refinement theorem.
- `gesso.assurance.proof.opaque` keeps source-bound abstract results and
  explicit open premises; local witness checking does not establish that a
  physical callback computes the correct business result.

## Cross-part obligations

- 02.10: authoritative acquisition, read coverage, subject/query/time and
  basis progression needed to justify an observation.
- 02.11–02.12: actor agreement and comparison of knowledge/frontiers without
  inventing universal order.
- Parts III–IV: knowledge-preserving projection, command admission and
  authoritative operation realization.
- Parts VI–VII: admitted browser observation, current ownership, and actual
  application/producer correspondence.
- Parts IX–X: derivation evidence, open assertions, proof kernel claims and
  physical execution refinement.

R4's five knowledge sources, role-local use constraints, privacy boundary
and requirement for inspectable derivations are retained. The
`req-knowledge-provenance` anchor is unchanged. No current Gesso checker is
claimed to prove all of the stronger R5 judgments.
