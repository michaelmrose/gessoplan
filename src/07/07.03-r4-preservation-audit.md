## R4 obligations retained

1. **A graph, not a bag of booleans.** The assembly exposes typed
   semantic and physical subjects, identities, dependencies, source
   ownership, checker results, failure edges and explanations. It
   is not itself a second choreography or a proof checker.
2. **Operation-dependent semantic/physical nodes.** R4 distinguished
   the admitted operation and choreography/role, executable plan,
   generated and served asset, actual transport and ingress,
   trusted executor/model, typed settlement, historical command
   recovery, authoritative acquisition and rendered projection.
   These remain concrete application obligations when required by
   the selected operation. Server-only and read-only paths do
   not gain invented browser mutation nodes.
3. **Every required edge is checked.** R4's Claim graph included
   affordance/operation, choreography/projection, plan/artifact,
   transport/route, route/server/model, settlement/recovery,
   authorized acquisition and rendering. The R5.1 table already
   covered these. The revision makes source and checked independent
   binding provenance, exact physical scope and lifecycle explicit.
4. **Real failure localization.** R4's missing-plan, wrong-route,
   missing-clock, and missing-reread examples remain separately
   falsifiable, rather than generalized as one preflight failure.
   The new twelve-case history table preserves these plus
   uncertain command, remote one-Refresh, historical incorporation,
   orphan writers and invalid physical adoption.
5. **No orphan realization nodes.** R4 explicitly prohibited
   generated plans lacking semantic owners, semantic routes
   omitted from the operation registry, Live acquisition with
   no managed projection, and unowned server realizations.
   R5 had compressed this treatment; 07.03 now restores an
   explicit bidirectional coverage obligation with
   available-but-inactive declarations classified separately.
6. **Evidence belongs to the original proposition and source.**
   R4's edge-report examples deliberately retained checked
   relation, sources, observed binding, mechanism and remaining
   assumptions; a status field alone cannot prove its own
   admission or convert a copied same-shaped map into issued
   authority. R5.1 preserves the stronger Part I evidence
   dimensions and cross-checks real dependent instances.
7. **No unresolved required structural edge in closed scope.**
   Runtime current principal authorization is dynamic model
   state, not an unfinished assembly edge. A selected strict
   policy may reject an unsupported required checker obligation,
   while a trusted external premise has an explicit scope and
   cannot excuse a known wrong route.
8. **No automatic promotion to universal proof.** Even successful
   physical route wiring and checked artifact presence do not
   certify opaque handler body semantics, actual deployed
   adoption, all writers, current XTDB state, or browser/HTML
   physical completion. Evidence claims remain typed.

The long R4 Clojure illustrative maps for
claim-realization, claim-edges, checked-claim and artificial
broken-artifact/wrong-route/no-clock/no-acquisition checks were
not copied verbatim. Their authoring and falsification obligations
are preserved as typed relations and paired positive/negative
examples. The proposed old checker API and approximate status
maps do not become mandatory shipped APIs.

## Preserved R5 architecture and added precision

The preferred R5 07.03 already supplied fourteen representative
relations, a proposition-scoped evidence row, distinct
unsupported/inconclusive/contradicted dispositions, and a
realistic Claim closure matrix including stable command identity,
path argument correspondence, typed provider, unknown outcome,
physical installation and Live recovery.

07.03 adds:

- Distinct semantic, physical, installed and evidence nodes,
  with a well-founded proof dependency *argument* even where the
  application/Live realization graph contains feedback.
- Source-owned derivation of required edges, independent physical
  choices and the application policy, without either side
  rewriting the other.
- Bidirectional graph inventory: every required behavior has
  a realization, and every installed semantic/effect writer is
  owned or explicitly unclassified. Unknown is not empty;
  available-not-active is not a license for an installed writer.
- The actual effect footprint and chosen deployment generation
  as part of the scope of closed-world claims.
- Evidence mechanics distinguished from copyable diagnostic
  reports and a private issued executor; neither a digest nor
  a success discriminator grants authority.
- Separate historical command, pinned backend observation,
  current authorized readback and physically adopted UI
  propositions, protecting against retrospective outcome
  misclassification.
- A five-way semantic distinction between established, open,
  unsupported, inconclusive and contradicted, without mandating
  those exact labels for every implementation API.
- Explicit composition conditions: matching original subjects,
  source dependencies, application/host premises, checker version,
  supported abstraction, and retained bridges. Finite
  exploration cannot become inductive or universal physical proof.
- Twelve cases including successful eligible Claim, missing
  plan, R1/R2 target mismatch, missing Biff clock shape, missing
  permitted reread, consumed remote Refresh grant, later
  Reassign, orphan writers, dynamic HTML gap, checker timeout,
  stale deployed asset, and legitimate read/server-only paths.

## Current Gesso v1622 correspondence

- src/gesso/live/application_preflight/operation_summary.clj
  derives a sorted, operation-keyed explanation of route,
  browser plan, execution capabilities, settlement,
  publication and acquisition from the current nested
  OperationAcquisitionAssembly. It does not own a second
  registry or certify arbitrary model functions.
- src/gesso/live/application_preflight/assurance.clj
  distinguishes the closed runtime backbone, optional
  rendered Hiccup snapshots and dynamically checkable
  canonical Gesso/Biff HTML boundary. It explicitly
  records failure to establish production adoption and
  the intentional raw Ring response, handler replacement
  and alternative startup escape paths.
- src/gesso/assurance/proof/application-dependency.clj
  builds source-relative graph and induction-ledger
  dependency closure for the DECLARED abstract catalog.
  Its private source handle does not prove deployed
  application/writer completeness, XTDB physical
  refinement or universal kernel soundness.
- src/gesso/assurance/proof/application-writer-coverage.clj
  joins the independently derived declared, issued
  and host-observed operation-ID inventories while
  retaining actual writer completeness and executor
  equivalence as *not established*. Matching IDs are
  structural evidence only.
- src/gesso/assurance/proof/application-assurance-report.clj
  recomputes conditional source-relative evidence from
  original sources and explicitly declines to issue
  authority, certificates or universal proofs.
- Available relevant tests include
  test/gesso/live/application_preflight/operation_summary_test.clj
  (per-operation required-capability projection and
  missing acquisition) and
  test/gesso/assurance/application_induction_mutation_test.clj.
  Source/test structure was inspected. No fresh Gesso
  JVM, formal, XTDB or Chromium test execution is claimed.

Gesso presently implements meaningful pieces of the source-derived
graph. That is not an assertion that the finished whole-application
graph, deployed writer inventory, actual browser correspondence or
full HumanHelp acceptance is discharged.

## Cross-part and next-section obligations

Parts I–III own assurance vocabulary, original semantic source,
knowledge and choreography correctness. Part IV owns protected
RequestAssignment writes, current authority, command incorporation
and authorized readback. Parts V–VI own provisional UI, exact plan
and host/asset identity, physical completion, one-Refresh
authorization, subscription and recovery. Section 07.02 owns the
source composition and generation lifecycle; 07.04 must assign
mandatory evidence policy, operation exercise, writer coverage and
nonvacuity; 07.05–07.15 must join actual installed dispatch, model
execution, renderer, provider, asset, Live, explanation and physical
production gates. Part VIII still requires independent positive
HumanHelp behavior. Parts IX–XII retain the separate proof kernel,
semantic-to-physical correspondence, release and final invariants.
