## Central question

Can the finished Gesso claim to have **one authored meaning** while using several executable machines, a projection/compiler, independent checking oracles, a small derivation kernel, diagnostic sidecars, and both Clojure/JVM and ClojureScript/browser realizations?

**Yes, provided "one meaning" does not mean one physical function or one automatically sound implementation.** The business operation is authored once and has versioned semantic contracts. Gesso's global Choreo transition semantics, projected role machines, browser coordination adapter, trusted model implementation and proof mechanisms each have different work. Their intended *relations* must be checked at their legitimate boundaries. A sound abstract global model does not show that projection preserved all choices; a correct projection does not show physical messages reached recipients; a browser adapter state transition does not show that HTMX installed the effect; a checked derivation does not authenticate a real model guard or give a user permission to submit it.

The R4 \`main\` Part III already separates global choreography, role-local realization, knowledge and trusted model authority. R4 Part IX requires source-bound compositional evidence rather than proof by reputation. Preferred R5 strengthens the explicit canonical authoring, portable machines, dedicated checker and proof/runtime division. R5.1 additionally separates exact executable identity from global semantic subject, individual occurrence provenance from an invented trace, derivation checking from kernel soundness, and conditional abstract claims from physical and issuer applicability.

The reconciled architecture is **one source of semantic ownership, several scoped interpretation and evidence boundaries**. A browser semantic adapter and a global choreography interpreter may both count as executable semantic cores in their *respective* scope. Part I explicitly says there need not be one universal \`step\` function for all layers. A proof kernel is **not** the executable core; a diagnostic emitter is **not** either one.

## The complete relation, without unauthorized shortcuts

\`\`\`text
original model/rule source + admitted Choreo and application subject
  -> typed/validated semantic contracts and normalized identities
  -> executable global Choreo semantics
  -> verification, projection and exact role-local plan set
  -> role-local machines and separately admitted environment / communication
  -> normalized browser event -> pure browser adapter -> declared abstract effects
  -> current resource/effect permit -> physical HTMX / DOM / shell / server action
  -> independently sourced observations of actual occurrence and outcome

In parallel, not as a second runtime:
  original declared subject and complete original-source obligations
  -> elaboration candidates / structural proof / finite or correspondence checks
  -> checker independently reads source and evaluates exact judgment
  -> named premises, provenance, bounds and dependency ledger
  -> optional external S0/S1 calculus proofs and qualified S2/S3/S4 transfer
  -> trusted issuer/admission only for the proposition actually established
  -> no new principal, model fact, command result, browser lease or physical grant
\`\`\`

The upper path is not accepted because its lower sidecar says it is correct. The lower path is not accepted because replay of the upper path produced a similar-looking report. The joins require original-source and artifact identity, exact semantics and witness provenance, and whenever a physical claim is made, evidence of the **actual** selected runtime, host and effect. Independent oracles address shared mistakes; they do not become universal theorems merely by using different namespaces.

## Structural projection, executable witnesses and quantification

A global Choreo specification admits role-local machines only when the projected behavior is legitimate under the selected semantics and assumptions. The knowledge and projection contract has two sides:

1. **Preserve required behavior.** An authored authoritative operation, branch, local action, await or communication must be represented by the responsible role's projected continuation with its actual closed data and admissibility obligations. A receiver cannot branch on a discriminator it has never learned, unless behavior is equivalent or a separately justified knowledge source exists.
2. **Forbid new behavior.** An emitted role-local receive or authoritative operation must have an exact legitimate original global origin. A compact projected receive can intentionally merge alternatives, but must preserve all relevant source alternatives and their distinct guards/payload contracts. A projected machine is not permitted to invent an unowned global transition.

Both are necessary; neither one establishes all-schedule trace refinement, liveness, or actual transport delivery. A successful *finite explicit schedule replay* is about that schedule. The checker must state the admitted observation alphabet, permitted hidden/stuttering local steps, relevant causal order, complete source/role termination constraints and remaining open environmental premises. Global \`:return\` is not automatically a participant-known local terminal message.

A role-local send does not establish recipient receipt. The role-local receive's plausible shape does not establish a real preceding sender. Environment \`:await\` is a different kind of source from \`:communicate\`: an authorized server observation cannot be inferred from an event keyword alone. Trusted-side operations must independently admit principal, guard, resource and authority.

A useful structural origin proof and one or several successful concrete witnesses can coexist with an *unproved* universal theorem. The final report must preserve the quantifier: **for one supplied schedule**; **for all enumerated finite cases**; or **for all executions satisfying explicitly proven premises**. None may silently substitute for another.

## Exact identity, sidecars and diagnostic noninterference

The executable plan set binds its actual global semantic subject, roles, compiled continuations, implementation closure and assembly generation; optional diagnostic sidecars identify the exact selected plan locations. Similar names, schema shape, source spans, runtime locator counts or a copied hash cannot reconstruct those bindings. Two different global choreographies can emit the same local executable plan yet have different global terminal outcomes. Matching local plan bytes therefore does not establish global semantic identity. Conversely, compatible rolling-release plans need not have byte-identical payloads.

A diagnostic must consume an immutable, bounded, privacy-filtered trace of what the **actual selected step** decided. It cannot run the guard/effectful builder a second time to create an explanation. The actual source locator may be synthetic or correspond to multiple authored alternatives; selection must be recorded without silently choosing a convenient invented origin. Rejected-before-selection, stale and malformed events may have *no* successful locator. A later physical-effect failure does not rewrite the earlier selected semantic transition into one that never occurred.

If optional diagnostic metadata is absent, stale, mismatched or maliciously forged, it must not change valid admitted runtime transitions, principal/authorization, command identity, resource rights or physical effect permits. If a separate build policy requires a mandatory assurance gate, that policy is checked at *construction admission*, not implemented by making runtime behavior depend on optional sidecar truth. A correct-looking sidecar with a copied \`:valid? true\` or matching executable digest is untrusted evidence, even when its explanation happens to be accurate.

Diagnostic information itself can leak private Request/actor or source-policy details. Redact before giving traces to less-trusted observers, and preserve current disclosure checks. A source locator or proof report is not a credential for reading protected history.

## Pure adapter to actual browser/host relation

The browser boundary is **normalized event -> pure adapter state/effects -> physically admitted host effect -> normalized completion or failure**. The actual shell does not determine Choreo truth or reissue effect rights; the adapter does not possess the DOM, EventSource or physical Ring/XTDB credentials. The host can still fail after semantic permission is issued, so the corresponding evidence must retain both occurrences.

A valid actual browser refinement check needs:

- the exact current admitted runtime plan and active execution/resource/target identities;
- actual issued normalized events, not a caller-authored plausible history;
- a source/target-correlated record of each protected physical request, message delivery, OOB/main swap, DOM mutation, continuation resource and completion where relevant;
- a final physical mutation gate that rejects stale-generation work even if an old callback was legitimately started;
- a declared relation between role-local machine observable occurrences and source-authored global operations, including per-occurrence causal order and permitted hidden steps;
- independent trusted evidence when the conclusion mentions domain model, principal, backend commit or provider basis.

For example, HTTP 200 plus an adapter's \`:allow-swap\` effect proves neither that HTMX performed the swap nor that the correct current target accepted all required OOB fragments. When a toolbar installs but the board cohort fails, installation is partial; an optimistic status or compatibility-looking DOM tree cannot impersonate complete semantic adoption. An old execution's timer completion may be physically emitted after its target generation retires; it must be ignored or permitted only as inert diagnostics, not applied to the successor instance.

When several real executions share a target, independent execution IDs do not themselves serialize the shared DOM. The accepted physical relation must retain actual target ownership and conflict/merge/retirement policy. Conversely, the relation must not impose an invented total order on truly independent commuting local actions if the declared semantics does not require one. The correct causal relation is more precise than either "every event is serial" or "same ID, therefore safe."

## Five external/formal transfer claims that remain independent

Part X deliberately separates the external-formal and physical trust progression:

| Stage | What an established result would mean | What it does not automatically establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **S0** — inference-rule soundness | Every admitted *initial* propositional inference schema preserves entailment under the defined Boolean interpretation, including open-premise behavior | The production parser, source typing, backend or role projection is correct |
| **S1** — derivation-system soundness | Every admitted complete ordered derivation yields its goal under exactly inherited named premises | Every user-supplied Clojure proof candidate or checker invocation was parsed/admitted correctly |
| **S2** — real Clojure checker correspondence | The actual parser, normalizer and admitted checker, for its selected supported inputs and limits, corresponds to the S1 formal relation | Arbitrary external business predicates or installed application effects satisfy those formulas |
| **S3** — typed-atom correspondence | Supported typed rule evaluation, presence/nil and original variable bindings correctly realize the admitted Boolean atom interpretation | Unknown real-world domain inputs, opaque Clojure guards or absent authorization facts are true |
| **S4** — issuance and production transfer | A current issuer, original-source-required obligations, premise inheritance, consuming source and installed realization are properly bound under the relevant conditional contracts | Generic universal deployment safety without separately qualified Ring/XTDB/HTMX/browser and writer-coverage boundaries |

This is a **plan for five separate proof and implementation claims**. A correct S0/S1 calculus theorem does not assert S2/S3/S4. Finite truth tables over uninterpreted atoms cannot prove every supported typed Clojure expression, much less the current Membership of a user. Bounded abstraction and finite model checks must state their enumerated domains and nonvacuity controls.

The initial propositional kernel has exactly its original closed seven families: premise, truth introduction, implication reflexivity, conjunction introduction/elimination, implication elimination, disjunction introduction. It does not contain a general rule saying an arbitrary atom or typed business fact is true. The separate guarded-transition derivation checker has a different admitted rule system and relation premise; its checked conditional conclusion must not be mislabeled the seven-rule kernel's theorem or the runtime guarded transaction.

Independence matters twice: the checker must reread and validate original source rather than accepting a proof producer's report, and stronger meta-soundness/correspondence must not be claimed merely because a diagnostic oracle shares the same production parser or evaluator bug. Independent source-based truth-table checks, finite models, typed-atom oracles and targeted mutations are useful evidence within their tested scopes; they do not establish universal theorem transfer.

Assumption discharge is itself a typed checked judgment: actual trusted evidence must imply precisely the claimed proposition under the correct subject, scope, source version, principal/authority and temporal basis, retaining inherited assumptions. A \`:discharged? true\` field, a premise with the same name, a source digest or a green checker report cannot mint a current model fact or effect lease. An opaque business callback may be used under a named contract, but stronger formal claims retain its unresolved implementation premise until independently qualified.
