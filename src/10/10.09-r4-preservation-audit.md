## Editorial result

Preserve the preferred R5 emphasis that every assumed formal capability has a concrete source and production responsibility, restoring R4's fuller worked diagnostic and owner-specific repair path. The revision deliberately avoids a secondary handwritten formal application model and avoids claiming that a formal counterexample is an observed production incident. Its stronger record differentiates *pre-effect enforcement*, post hoc observation, universal physical refinement, source-relative proof and conditional environment assumptions.

R4's explicit developer experience—the ability to ask both which production operation a theorem constrains and which actual evidence discharges its assumptions—is retained as a required end-to-end query, not only as explanatory prose. ## R4/R5 and accepted-section preservation decisions

1. **Exact semantic source is the authority.** A generated formal subject derives from the original admitted model, operation, typed rule or choreography. Application-authored parallel TLA+/Quint/Recife definitions may be useful independent tests but cannot replace the admitted source meaning by name alone.

2. **Traceability is bidirectional, not reverse simulation.** Formal claims, premises and counterexamples must locate their source and concrete enforcing boundary. This mapping does not establish that every abstract trace is physically realizable or that all production traces refine it.

3. **Formal assumptions create physical debts.** A modeled authenticated principal, current observation, atomic transition, unique active primary, future event delivery or fresh browser resource creates a distinct qualified implementation obligation. Writing the assumption in a theorem does not discharge it.

4. **Each debt has a first real enforcing boundary.** The diagnostic distinguishes original preflight/type checks, authentication/session state, current public-model authorization, backend transaction conflict/guard, historical command resolution, actual effect-time DOM gate and independent product acceptance.

5. **Physical and abstract sets are not interchangeable.** Original source catalog closure, submitted plan/vector conformity, host/route adoption, physical writer closure and actual transaction refinement require their own witnesses. Matching source names or reported digests are not an enforcing boundary.

6. **Same logical command is not one physical attempt.** C42 may have H1/H2 deliveries. Durable protected command identity and replay, not a per-attempt process-local one-shot or HTTP request ID, controls actual incorporation and historic outcome.

7. **Guarded Claim is a true multi-record relation.** The public model requires current Request lifecycle and Membership/Organization/Location policy, an appropriately protected RequestAssignment one-primary absence predicate, domain effects, frame and command-result record under the admitted atomicity semantics. An external pre-read cannot substitute for current transaction state.

8. **Quantifier direction is explicit.** Every relevant installed physical execution must map to an allowed admitted abstract trace for strong safety refinement. Realizability/completeness of all abstract traces is a separate claim if required, and bounded single-call comparison establishes neither universally.

9. **Spurious abstract traces require investigation.** Overapproximated finite/abstract models can admit behaviors not realizable in production; that does not automatically demonstrate a deployed XTDB defect. A modeled true counterexample still refutes its *selected formal model* under the stated assumptions and bounds.

10. **Safe model can coexist with unsafe production.** A correct abstract atomic guard may pass even if the real installed writer uses stale pre-read and unguarded PUT. That is a failed or unresolved production correspondence obligation, not a contradiction of a correctly formulated formal theorem.

11. **Proof failures are typed, not all bugs.** Valid counterexample, unsupported judgment, checker timeout/budget exhaustion, stale source, vacuous initial state, unqualified physical observation and actual counterexample concretization require different classifications and repair owners.

12. **First blocker and privacy are preserved.** Keep the earliest responsible obstruction, stable structured reason, real source location and a minimally adequate authorized trace. Copyable reports do not expose private Membership, commands, tenant information or trusted capability handles.

13. **Translation bugs belong to the framework.** Generated TLA+/Quint/Recife/Lean artifacts are derivatives; a framework-maintained lowering mismatch requires correction of the translator and regeneration, not silent edits to a checked/generated file.

14. **Policy changes belong to HumanHelp.** An invariant that is well typed but differs from product intention is not corrected by having a proof tool invent a business rule. The application owner approves changed source policy; such a change is reported and requires new proof and product acceptance.

15. **Source checks and physical repairs must be re-exercised independently.** An approved source guard repair regenerates proofs and issued plans; a backend enforcement repair requires independent actual write/failure tests. A post-effect report cannot retroactively prevent an invalid write.

16. **Finite counterexample reduction preserves meaning.** A minimized typed-rule valuation or model trace remains under its stated domain and grammar; shrinking to a different discrepancy or suppressing an original operation cannot count as repair.

17. **Original positive capabilities survive.** An empty Init, deleted Unclaim/Reassign or reject-all gateway may make a narrow model look safe while violating source, admission completeness or independent Part VIII product requirements.

18. **Liveness adds environment premises.** Safety simulation and reachable recovery are not eventual settlement under all schedules. Fairness, durable retention, permission, delivery/gap repair, availability and explicit user-Refresh actions must be scoped.

19. **Single and multi-node semantics remain the same.** Physical failures, credential and node coverage, routing, durable command/outbox and registration-frontier assumptions add realization obligations, not a separate authorization model.

20. **No certificate or effect authority arises from a report.** A copied diagnostic, source-correct proof node, local XTDB observation or assistant repair proposal cannot grant a Claim, protected read, physical retry or DOM adoption.

## Sixteen paired positive/adversarial acceptance controls

1. **Real source:** A valid generated model and issued plan share the exact original operation; an independently edited formal file with the same ID cannot establish correspondence.
2. **Original completeness:** Source-rederived Init, operations and invariants are present; an omitted Unclaim in a structurally consistent submitted DAG cannot pass.
3. **Actual abstract counterexample:** The defective pre-read-only Claim candidate yields the scoped failing race; a correctly guarded model is not blamed simply because its action shares the Claim name.
4. **Safe source/unsafe host:** A valid abstract Claim theorem coexists with an intentionally unguarded deployed writer; production refinement remains open or fails, not reclassified as an abstract counterexample.
5. **Current actor:** Trusted authenticated helper/tenant authorization permits a genuine eligible Claim; a browser-supplied helper role cannot authorize it.
6. **Predicate conflict:** Two different eligible Claim commands compete and one primary wins under the qualified actual backend; two separate pre-reads and distinct assignment inserts without shared exclusion cannot be accepted.
7. **Command history:** Compatible C42 H1/H2 reconciliation preserves actual historic incorporation; a current claimed card or missing pinned ledger row cannot fabricate the command outcome.
8. **Evidence origin:** A captured gateway call/readback is reported as bounded physical observation; copied success maps, wrong caller-supplied node or concurrent other writer cannot certify universal causality.
9. **Effect-time browser owner:** A valid current target receives only permitted installed content; a retired callback reusing the DOM ID is denied before its physical mutation.
10. **Conditional delivery and user choice:** A genuinely retained advisory can support named conditional progress; SSE delivery alone does not grant a remote collection Refresh.
11. **Unsupported/timeout/vacuous:** Completed supported proof is distinct from timeout, unknown syntax, empty Init, exhausted budget or missing checker; none silently becomes a strong checked theorem.
12. **Source-located diagnosis:** A counterexample has original source, exact bounds, failing rule and the separately open physical question; a diagnostic does not invent a deployed XTDB trace.
13. **Source/translation repair:** Approved semantic guard or framework lowering repair regenerates checked artifacts and tests; editing only generated formal code is insufficient.
14. **Real physical repair:** An actual guarded backend writer prevents the prohibited effect; post hoc mismatch readback is not reported as prevention.
15. **Positive product:** Eligible Claim and independently required HumanHelp lifecycle/board capabilities remain usable; reject-all cannot satisfy completion.
16. **Multi-node scope:** Single-/multi-node Claim retain the same semantics and privacy/authority; physical node failure, replay/gap, writer coverage and fair progress remain separately qualified.

Every test predeclares its positive neighbor, injected adversarial mutation, exact source and formal abstraction, actual identity/evidence generation, first responsible source/checker/enforcing boundary, scoped observation, and permitted disclosure. A successful rejection at the source checker does not establish that an untested backend bypass was prevented. A physical diagnostic after incorporation does not rewind the transaction.

## Editorial outcome

10.09 preserves the R4 and preferred R5 original-source reverse traceability, generated-formal-as-derivative architecture, defective Claim counterexample and owner-approved repair loop. It adds a more precise obligation/physical-enforcement ledger, soundness/refinement direction, nonvacuous positive requirements, first-blocker/provenance classification and honest physical nonclaims. It is a future Gesso design contract, not a statement that all implementations are complete today.

**Nonclaims:** No runtime or external formal tests were executed; no all-schedules deployed Claim proof, full physical writer confinement, authenticated XTDB transaction history, complete HumanHelp product, full Lean/Recife/Quint semantics or independent Part X acceptance is asserted.
