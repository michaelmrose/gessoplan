## Governing editorial decision

Retain R5's clearer separation of closed typed rules, distributed use-site
judgments, proof kernel, source-derived obligation closure, conditional
induction, assumptions and genuine issuance. Restore R4's emphasis on
proof-producing elaboration, repair-oriented diagnostics, typed semantic
model/domain/interface layers, actual source-to-assembly relation and
non-vacuous accepted HumanHelp Claim.

Part IX provides the exact core semantics and the Claim pilot contract.
This section defines what *completion of that bounded milestone* requires;
it does not introduce another judgment calculus or duplicate the full
proof system. A checked derivation under the selected calculus does not
establish the independent meta-theorem that the production checker
implements that calculus soundly. An installed browser or XTDB result
requires a distinct physical correspondence claim, even if the same
root ledger explains the links.

## Thirty-two R4/R5 preservation and strengthening decisions

1. Completion names exact artifact classes, original source, application
   scope, supported judgments and selected profile.
2. Typed rules have a closed versioned syntax, type/value semantics,
   short-circuit refinement and conservative normalization.
3. A genuinely accepted valid rule and appropriate malformed/invalid
   cases must exercise the supported subset.
4. Arbitrary opaque Clojure remains permitted through classified
   assumptions, not silently proven by parser acceptance.
5. Domain/model/schema source interfaces retain independent owners
   and declared implementation commitments.
6. Knowledge, provenance, authority, refinement, progression and
   resource rights are checked at role-local use sites.
7. Projection and executable-plan identities remain bound to global
   Choreo semantics and named observables.
8. Semantic source/projection correspondence does not itself prove
   actual browser HTMX/SSE implementation refinement.
9. Proof-producing elaboration proposes derivations rather than
   issuing a result on its own authority.
10. The small kernel checks admitted proof terms under its actual
    closed inference and context rules.
11. Dedicated semantic/finite/induction/distributed checkers retain
    their separate judgment and resource-limit boundaries.
12. A checked derivation and checker meta-soundness are different
    propositions; the latter requires stronger external evidence.
13. The original source, not an elaborator's supplied list, fixes
    the complete required obligation denominator.
14. Validated dependency acyclicity is not proof of required source
    coverage; missing branch, writer or producer remains blocking.
15. Induction checks all relevant declared invariants, initialization
    and every declared transition, with correct frame/guard meaning.
16. Abstract source catalog completeness and actual physical writer
    or initializer completeness remain different evidence subjects.
17. Inhabited allowed initial states and eligible positives prevent
    unsatisfiable/reject-all vacuous completion.
18. Source-dependent assumptions propagate along the *used* proof path.
19. Discharge is checked entailment/substitution with matched subject,
    actor, scope, basis, issuer and inherited premises.
20. Same-label/foreign-scope evidence or a copied discharged flag
    does not establish a genuine premise.
21. Genuine in-process issuer and typed admission check exact subject,
    source, checker version, generation and profile.
22. Re-admission after serialization is a real checker/trust boundary,
    not acceptance of copyable report fields.
23. Evidence reuse and cache/currentness account for semantic and
    physical dependency changes separately.
24. An accepted certificate is not runtime permission to execute,
    write, retrieve command results or install DOM effects.
25. HumanHelp Claim uses genuine public Request/RequestAssignment
    multi-record, Membership/Organization/Location interfaces.
26. Request =:done= and separate primary assignment remain production
    model facts; invented helper field/complete state are rejected.
27. Current authenticated helper, guards, one-primary safety and other
    relevant operation/writer dependencies are in the pilot's scope.
28. Stable C42 binding, compatible replay, committed/rejected/already-
    incorporated/unknown outcomes and historical disclosure are distinct.
29. Real durable publication intent and physical transaction/writer
    correspondence remain open until specifically qualified.
30. Accepted source Claim, actual issuer/plan/route and installed
    browser evidence have separate authenticated/qualified joining edges.
31. Pilot positive/negative conformance does not meet the independent
    required HumanHelp product denominator, including Response.
32. This milestone's conditional checker chain does not automatically
    establish external formal kernel correctness, whole XTDB/browser
    refinement or the separately named broader milestones.

All essential R4 milestone obligations survive, with R5's sharper
semantic/source/physical distinctions maintained. No vital worked
Claim requirement or proof/certificate contract is intentionally lost.

## Actual Gesso v1622 implementation correspondence and limits

These source files were inspected at the pinned application-assurance
commit; no new JVM, XTDB, Chromium, kernel proof, source-to-physical
refinement or HumanHelp test was executed for this editorial revision.

- src/gesso/assurance/proof/certificate.clj admits narrow, opaque
  in-process source-relative proof handles for propositional,
  guarded-transition and conditional-induction kinds. Its namespace
  explicitly excludes proof of independent kernel soundness,
  physical writer completeness, initialized deployed state and
  real XTDB/CLJS transition refinement.
- src/gesso/assurance/proof/obligation.clj derives UNEVALUATED
  invariant initialization and transition-preservation obligations
  from typed original guarded-replacement construction. Emitted
  obligations are not themselves discharged theorems.
- src/gesso/assurance/proof/operation_interface_composition.clj
  composes original-source conditional abstract operation contracts,
  preserves open premises and detects potential read/write
  interference. It does not issue a certified physical writer
  inventory, prove concurrency correctness or grant runtime admission.
- src/gesso/assurance/proof/application_assurance_report.clj
  recomputes a nonauthoritative conditional source-bound report
  and explicitly preserves open independent kernel soundness,
  actual installed adoption, physical XTDB writer completeness,
  universal execution refinement and browser correspondence.
- src/gesso/live/application_preflight.clj checks selected whole-
  application relations and generated browser artifacts within
  a defined construction/runtime boundary; it does not certify
  arbitrary host adoption, raw HTML bypass or all dynamic writers.

These are genuine pieces of a future compositional assurance chain,
not proof that the entire described milestone is operationally
completed or that the independently accepted Part IX specification
has been fully implemented. The design requires source-derived
issuance, checker coverage, qualified premises and real pilot
conformance; local snapshots and explanatory summaries cannot
supply unexecuted evidence.

## Eighteen prospective paired Claim/certification controls

| # | Positive acceptance condition | Invalid nearby shortcut or failure |
|---|-------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| 1 | Exact typed rule and presence semantics pass | Missing/nil/path behavior collapsed by normalizer |
| 2 | Supported valid proof checked with original premises | Unknown inference or forged proved flag accepted |
| 3 | All source-required Claim/operation branches covered | Closed graph omits negative Claim or Reassign |
| 4 | Useful eligible Claim admitted under satisfiable initial | Vacuous unsatisfiable or reject-all system passes |
| 5 | Every required declared operation preserves invariant | Failing writer removed from catalog |
| 6 | Genuine matched E discharges P under correct scope | Other tenant's P or E-under-Q yields unconditional G |
| 7 | Role-local knowledge and authority judged | Transport observation equated to authority |
| 8 | Accepted plan tied to source and genuine issuer | Copyable summary impersonates certificate |
| 9 | Reuse preserves current checker/source semantics | Old valid proof survives changed normalizer |
| 10 | Durable package checked on import | Serialized map becomes execution grant |
| 11 | Public Request/RequestAssignment Claim is subject | Toy scalar or invented helper attribute substituted |
| 12 | Actual two-helper race respects one-primary | Two pre-read-based Claims both accepted |
| 13 | C42 outcome/replay/unknown binding retained | Unknown treated rejected, C43 issues duplicate |
| 14 | Writer and initializer correspondence qualified | Abstract catalog claimed complete actual XTDB universe |
| 15 | Actual issued plan/browser/route claims joined | Fixture HTTP request sold as browser refinement |
| 16 | Conditional proof ledger keeps physical premises | Green root report asserted universal proof |
| 17 | Legitimate opaque Clojure interface admitted conditionally | Mandatory closed proof replaced with assertion |
| 18 | Other HumanHelp capabilities remain independently required | Claim pilot silently completes Reassign/Response |

The conformance corpus is a future requirement, not a newly
executed test result. Actual checker, source/physical and product
oracles require distinct evidence and subjects; neither a
successful finite fixture nor a generated report demonstrates
unbounded runtime correspondence.
