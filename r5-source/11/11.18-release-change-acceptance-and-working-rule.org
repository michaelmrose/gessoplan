** Acceptance for changes to the release mechanism

The release and certification mechanisms are themselves *part of the
system being qualified*. An application can have correct rules and
still be deployed incorrectly because an obligation was not
discovered, a stale certificate was trusted, a source-equivalent
adapter change escaped physical requalification, or a tested
handler was never installed. This section defines change-driven
and adversarial acceptance of the *actual release path*.
Parts I–X own the underlying semantic, proof and physical
contracts; 11.01–11.17 own candidate admission, evidence,
activation, compatibility and completion scopes. This section
checks their composition without creating another business
policy, issuer or arbitrary host-language proof mechanism.

A release-system test must have an independently fixed
original requirement, a declared eligible positive,
a controlled invalid or interrupted alternative, the
specific expected first rejecting boundary, an observable
result/absence-of-effect oracle, and an attributable
implementation/environment subject. A passing report
produced by the mechanism under test is not an independent
oracle that the mechanism was invoked or correctly enforced.

*** Challenge dependency invalidation and trustworthy evidence reuse

A correct change-impact decision follows the actual
meaning and implementation dependencies of each claim.
Semantic identity, normalized source, proof and checker
rules, premise qualification, concrete callable/provider,
exact emitted bytes, installed host/route and client
continuation are distinct coordinates. A change can
invalidate one layer without changing another. Both
unsafe reuse *and unnecessary global invalidation*
are rejected when the selected reuse contract promises
incrementality.

| Controlled change | Required re-evaluation | Independently positive counterbalance |
|-------------------+------------------------+---------------------------------------|
| Change Claim eligibility or a rule's presence/refinement meaning | Rebuild the changed original-source semantic/operation/plan dependency cone and recheck its required proof and interface claims | Unrelated unchanged search rule/theorem remains reusable under verified dependencies |
| Change an opaque implementation, XTDB adapter, model writer or physical provider while retaining semantic operation identity | Requalify the affected implementation and actual authoritative transition/writer correspondence; keep a sound abstract theorem only in its original scope | Legitimate current Claim still passes through the qualified changed writer |
| Change normalizer, proof rule, checker, proof importer or issuer version | Recheck the dependent inference and certificate-applicability subjects under current admission rules | A valid unchanged supported derivation can be re-admitted and still use its justified premise set |
| Corrupt, forge, truncate or replay a cached package or passing report | Safe decode and genuine checker/issuer re-admission reject or independently reconstruct from original source | Unchanged genuine immutable issued evidence can still be reused under its specified currentness contract |
| Change compiler, manifest, browser bytes, decoder, handler, deployed route or serving provider | Requalify the exact affected build, installation, transfer, load and protected runtime boundaries | Supported old/new clients use only the direction and assets their release actually admits |
| Change principal, Membership, credential, provider-incarnation or backend safety premise | Fence affected new protected effects/disclosures and requalify the actual current premise | Other authorized and otherwise unaffected requests continue within their admissible scope |
| Add, remove or fail to discover an original product capability, non-HTTP writer, proof branch or external obligation | Recheck independent requirement and source completeness; block unchanged full claim where an obligation is missing | A separately approved narrower preview is accurately labeled without pretending to complete the original claim |
| Reorder physical startup, activate concurrently, replace a host or fail after partial initialization | Qualify the actual installation, effect fence, coherent activation and cleanup/rollback applicability | Still-qualified previous work continues and valid new activation succeeds under its actual policy |

The release ledger records the changed proposition and
every dependency reconsidered, which evidence was
validly retained, which physical claim became stale,
and why unrelated evidence remained applicable.
A green cached summary cannot issue or preserve
a certificate by itself; an abstract theorem that
remains valid does not authenticate a newly deployed
adapter, selected handler or browser bytes. Required
checks that cannot execute stay explicitly nonpassing,
not implicitly accepted because recomputation is costly.

*** Test the exact decisions and mandatory gate vocabulary

The selected release intent and original independent
capability/proof denominator are fixed *before*
candidate gate evaluation. Construction then derives
its closed required ledger from actual source and
selected deployment scope, including branches,
writers, protected reads, browser paths and
required positive user capabilities. A generated
partial catalog, missing UI control, unreachable
test, absent proof producer or disabled policy
cannot shrink the unchanged intended subject.

Every mandatory gate retains the closed result
classification =:passed=, =:failed=,
=:incomplete=, =:unavailable=, =:unsupported=,
=:skipped=, =:stale= or =:missing=. A malformed
result is an error, not a ninth success value.
The actual checker/observer and applicable evidence
must justify a =:passed= disposition; a copyable
status row or unobserved test completion cannot.
Failing, unexecuted or expired required gates block
the exact requested decision. A valid trusted
premise can remain *conditional* if the selected
profile permits it; it is not independently proved,
and a mandatory failed theorem cannot be moved
into a premise list without changing the claim.

The four release decisions are challenged separately:
candidate admission; actual installation; traffic
activation under coherent overlap and safe existing
work; and named milestone completion. A candidate
may be correct without being installed, a genuine
installed guarded Biff handler may not be selected
by a running listener, and a locally activated host
does not establish global mixed-node compatibility.
The four independently scoped completion milestones
of 11.13–11.16 are likewise not aliases for the
four release decisions or a single green bit.

Test the required *positive* candidates under the
same actual gates as invalid ones. A release
mechanism that rejects all candidates, disables
every protected button, fails all external
proof checks or never emits an executable
artifact cannot claim success because its
negative safety controls are all green.
A legitimate supported conditional proof must
retain precisely its permissible premises,
while a release that originally requests
a stricter proven result must refuse any
post-failure silent downgrade to a narrower
assurance profile.

*** Observe activation, rollback and physical failure windows

The qualifying observation ladder reaches the
selected physical boundaries: original semantic
checker; genuine proof issuer/re-admission;
generated application assembly; actual Ring/Biff
handler and protected non-HTTP writers; emitted,
served and loaded browser artifact; chosen backend
transaction; subscription/router transport;
and independently authorized browser acquisition
and DOM installation. Each observation is credited
only at its real boundary. A test-only wrapper,
invented receipt or observed HTTP 200 cannot
authenticate a different production gateway
or prove business command incorporation.

For each possible failure schedule identify
the earliest *actual* protected effect,
the candidate/host/asset generation and
applicable current authority, the fault
injected, the intended rejecting/fencing
boundary, any effect already committed,
and the permitted final user-facing status.
An invalid attempt should fail before the
forbidden physical effect where the
enforcement contract requires it.
Detection after an irreversible commit
may be diagnostically useful but cannot
be counted as prevention of that effect.

Concurrent installation and activation
must not expose a half-published mix
of routes, executors, guards, providers
or browser assets. A stale candidate
cannot silently overwrite a newer
active generation. Partial initializer
failure accounts for acquired sockets,
workers and publications even if
there is no returned resource map;
cleanup failures retain the primary
gate error and independently fence
unadmitted effects. Fallback to a
previous candidate is allowed only
while its *current* backend, security
and release premises remain valid.
Rollback is new checked activation
against actual migrated state and
retained work, not reversal of
committed domain history.

A response timeout or host restart
after possible authoritative submission
does not imply that the command was
rejected. The original logical
command binding and identity survive
release retirement, compatible
reconciliation and any admitted retry.
A current Request read does not
attribute a prior C42. Durable
publication, SSE delivery, current
authorized acquisition and visible
browser adoption have independently
named status and recovery obligations.
Retiring old execution removes
obsolete effect and DOM rights
without erasing truthful historical
command evidence.

*** Require real browser, distributed and product oracles

For every profile that claims real
user or installed-system behavior,
exercise the actual rendered
user control and issued plan,
selected protected backend/handler,
source-owned outcome, loaded
browser/HTMX/SSE runtime and
qualified physical read/DOM result.
A manually synthesized internal
command bypassing the rendered
affordance has narrower coverage.
A successful optimistic effect,
transport event or stream frame
is not an independently observed
authoritative transaction or
remote user's adoption.

HumanHelp retains its independent
product capability denominator,
public model authority, six
lifecycle mutations and still-open
Response product choice. The
remote lifecycle adoption policy
is already selected as advisory
until explicit authorized Refresh,
subject to its narrow own-command
and affected-card recovery.
Neither release restart nor
router reconnect creates a
one-use Refresh grant or
clears incomparable newer
advisory debt. An outdated
browser resource cannot
overwrite newer authorized
state. A full HumanHelp
operational claim must show
real eligible success for the
required user capabilities,
not only rejections or Claim.

A multi-node claim requires
independently running processes,
shared authoritative semantics
under actual selected XTDB
premises, protected command
result and outbox failure
windows, subscription
registration fences, stale
router/filter failover,
authorized replay or
qualified re-acquisition,
and actual A/A, A/B, B/A,
B/B directions. A healthy
backup router with an
incomplete interest filter
is not proof that a required
notification was delivered.
Conservative redelivery
remains eligible when its
authorization, resource
and recovery constraints
are demonstrated. No
universal participant
fabric or one particular
router implementation
is demanded.

Conditional liveness
retains its backend
availability, fairness,
history retention,
network, reconnect and
explicit user-action
premises. An offline
recipient who has not
Refreshed is not
reported as having
adopted all updates.
An unavailable or
timed-out physical
observer cannot issue
a positive result merely
because it did not
observe a counterexample.

*** Acceptance matrix: eligible release versus nearby defect

| Positive required behavior | Invalid nearby result or mechanism to reject |
|----------------------------+----------------------------------------------|
| Changed Claim rule invalidates complete semantic cone; unrelated search proof remains reusable | Unrelated proof always rebuilt or changed rule keeps stale proof |
| Current abstract theorem reused with changed physical adapter separately qualified | Old adapter's successful XTDB test silently applies to changed writer |
| Genuine proof source/issuer re-admits only supported derivation | Corrupt cached success or copied report becomes proof capability |
| Exact mandatory operation and product denominator stays fixed | Missing Response or writer disappears from generated inventory |
| Correct strict profile blocks when required theorem is unavailable | Mandatory proof is called optional or trusted after failure |
| Explicit allowed assumption stays conditional with dependent claims | Accepted premise is labeled proved or missing obligation invented to block valid narrower claim |
| Each required missing, stale, skipped, incomplete, unsupported, failed or unavailable check blocks | A missing shard, status string, timeout or unsupported backend is counted as pass |
| Legitimate eligible Claim works through actual selected enforcement gate | Reject-all route or test-only guard passes negative-only suite |
| Genuine checked Biff handler is selected by the live listener | Correct unused handler map is mistaken for installed server adoption |
| Current exact compiled JS is served and loaded with applicable receipt | Stale JS, reused filename or forged artifact hash is called current |
| Concurrent B activation preserves coherent binding and predecessor condition | Late C, built against A, silently overwrites active B |
| Failed partial setup retains primary error, owned cleanup and still-valid A | B cleanup stops A shared resource or accidentally exposes B worker |
| Invalid old credential/backend premise fences A even if B installation fails | Previously green A is kept accepting unsafe writes as fallback |
| Real rollback qualifies old code against current migration and pending work | Historic passing binary is restarted with incompatible old writer |
| C42 may commit, response is lost, and current authorized resolver preserves uncertainty | Timeout becomes rejection, or retry allocates new command C43 |
| Current protected lookup observes genuine historical command record | Current Request state or user-supplied command ID is treated as proof of past effect |
| Committed effect and required outbox intent survive dispatcher/host loss | Sole in-memory SSE callback disappears and report declares delivery complete |
| Duplicate delivery asks authorized receiver to recheck state, without repeated Claim | Duplicate invalidation repeats authoritative mutation or leaks tenant metadata |
| Subscription read/registration and router failover preserve scope and coverage | Healthy router with stale filter silently misses interest interval |
| Permitted A/B and B/A directions have qualified routes, decoders and assets | Happy-path sticky session hides invalid failover to old server |
| Current principal and browser target rights are checked after release change | Old assembly proof freezes Membership or late callback installs into retired DOM |
| Bob's remote board remains advisory until one eligible Refresh | Host restart or SSE message automatically adopts unrelated remote row |
| Independently required real HumanHelp features have positive browser controls | Disabled Reassign/Response is counted as working because Claim passes |
| S0/S1 formal subject and S2–S4 conditional transfer remain distinct | Finite fixture or report claims universal kernel/physical proof |
| Actual two-host fault and bounded load cases have attributable observations | Simulated two-host labels or fast one-worker run certified as production |
| Required job inventory and test receipt provenance are verified | Unexecuted worker, flaky failure or caller-authored success row is hidden |
| Legitimate narrower preview has separate original claim identity and excluded obligations | Failed strict release keeps same ID after shrinking its scope |
| Named conditional-liveness premises are met before progress is claimed | Dead network or missing user Refresh is described as convergence |

These are future positive/adversarial conformance
requirements. Their existence in a document,
test name or coverage table does not establish
that they were executed. Positive behavior
must use the same real admission/enforcement
path whose invalid neighbor is challenged.
The acceptance report names original required
subjects, actual evidence issuers or observers,
selected hardware/software/environment,
reproducible fault schedule, verified
non-effects, unresolved premises and the
first actual enforcing boundary for each
relevant case. Production and formal claims
are not interchangeable merely because
both report success.

** Working rule for every semantic or release change

For every framework or application change
participating in the compositional chain,
ask *what exact proposition this layer
establishes, for what original subject,
under which premises, through what
genuinely checked or observed evidence,
and which next layer is entitled to
consume it*. If that question cannot
be answered at the required interface,
the corresponding edge remains an
explicit allowed premise or an
unsatisfied obligation for the
selected claim. It is not silently
inferred from matching names,
successful status maps or a
running server.

*** Keep a typed handoff at each layer

The ordinary change and release
workflow accounts for:

| Handoff | Required question before the next claim |
|---------+-----------------------------------------|
| Business rule and model to admitted operation | Are the independently selected product meaning, source owner, types, guards, readers, effects, frame conditions, and writer dependencies complete? |
| Operation to global Choreo and role-local projection | Are placement, knowledge/provenance, authority, progression, outcomes and terminal/recovery paths preserved by the checked source-relative relation? |
| Projection to browser/server executable construction | Are plans, canonical routes, transport envelopes, effects and resource/acquisition ownership matched to the original admitted semantics? |
| Typed artifact to derivation and proof checker | Does genuine accepted proof or dedicated checking discharge the exact proposition, with all relevant hypotheses and checker versions retained? |
| Proof/checker result to issuer and application assembly | Are the exact source, dependencies, required obligations, original denominator and current proof premises validated before any admitted certificate/assembly product is issued? |
| Assembly to installed handler, backend and browser | Does the *actual* listener, protected writer, selected XTDB/provider, built/served/loaded browser and runtime shell implement the checked subject within the named premises? |
| Current execution to participant observation/adoption | Do authoritative result, command incorporation, source/progression, user authorization, Live notification, acquisition and present DOM resource ownership justify this effect? |
| Installed realization to activation and release | Are current candidate, installation, traffic/overlap, rollback, resource lifetime and four milestone scopes individually justified by applicable evidence? |
| Prior release to changed release | Which semantic, issuer, trust, physical, product and environment dependencies changed, what evidence remains reusable, and what must be rechecked? |

A direct physical shortcut may
be a valid independently
admitted implementation,
but it must satisfy its
own actual semantics and
release profile. Application
authors should declare
business rules and Gesso
construction, not maintain
parallel proof-assistant
policies or manually
manufacture a certification
record. The executable
semantic core remains the
operational source;
a proof kernel checks
derivations, dedicated
checkers establish their
named judgments, and
production guards enforce
current runtime rights.

*** Track the exact evidence class and residual frontier

The framework reports which
claims are *proved under
a named formal system*,
*checked by a genuine
source-relative proof
issuer*, *exact-finite
checked within an explicit
domain*, *assembly-verified*,
*physically qualified*,
*runtime-enforced*,
*tested through observed
paths*, *asserted only*,
or *trusted under visible
premises*. These are
qualifications of specific
propositions, not
substitutable values on
a single ascending ladder.
A real installed browser
test cannot issue an
unbounded mathematical
theorem, while a formally
proved inference rule
does not show that the
running server actually
uses the corresponding
guard.

Evidence propagation
retains every undischarged
premise along the actually
used justification. A
trusted backend atomicity
assumption must be
qualified at its named
backend and scope; a
proof subject cannot
replace a verified actor
or current Membership;
a report cannot act as
an authorization token.
An unavailable required
checker is blocking under
the originally selected
profile. A permitted
conditional smaller claim
must keep its distinct
identity and all its
declared accepted
assumptions.

When a layer changes,
the owning application
or framework constructor
reconsiders only genuinely
dependent claims, while
still validating source
completeness and current
applicability. The
generation and host
binding of the old
completed record remain
historical. A stale
physical receipt does
not become fresh through
a semantic digest,
and sound unaffected
theorems need not be
reproved solely because
a different file changed.

*** Final rule: admit only the actual compositional subject

A candidate is admitted
because the original
requirements, selected
proof and operational
obligations, current
issuer and dependency
checks actually justify
that candidate. An
installation is eligible
because the *real*
selected host, protected
writers, browser assets
and external premises
satisfy their physical
qualification. Activation
is eligible only under
current coherent
traffic/continuation,
resource and rollback
conditions. A milestone
is complete only for
its exact required
capabilities and evidence
scope.

These decisions are
not reversible historical
decorations: their
dependent runtime rights
can cease when a current
principal, backend or
release premise becomes
false. The release
mechanism must fail
closed at the first
actual enforcing
boundary, preserve
truthful committed and
unknown command state
through its own failures,
and support permitted
positive user operations.
No automatic rollback
of authoritative domain
facts, universal
distributed liveness,
or proof of arbitrary
Clojure/browser/XTDB
execution is invented.

The intended system
therefore does *not*
claim "everything is
formally verified."
It can identify which
source-owned guarantees
are proved, checked,
conditionally justified,
physically qualified,
runtime-enforced,
observed or still
open; which current
realizations they
concern; and exactly
when an implementation,
premise, application
or deployment change
requires re-admission.
If a required handoff
cannot demonstrate
its semantic or
physical relationship,
the requested stronger
construction, release
or completion claim
stops there rather
than allowing
incompatible premises
to drift into an
application that
merely appears to work.
