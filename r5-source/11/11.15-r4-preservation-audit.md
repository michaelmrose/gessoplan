## Governing editorial judgment

Keep R5's superior bounded and conditional target. Make the exact five
Part X results S0–S4 independently visible in the *milestone* rather than
collapsing them into one "formalized" label. The first closed calculus
uses seven inference rules and opaque Boolean atoms; it does not claim
arithmetic, arbitrary Clojure policy, meta-soundness of the actual
production checker or a model of all XTDB and browser executions.

The strongest requirement is independently machine-checked S0/S1
together with justified S2–S4 production transfer appropriate to the
selected profile. A trusted translator or implementation correspondence
is acceptable only as an explicit conditional premise where the selected
claim permits it. If a profile demands proved production checker soundness
or actual browser trace refinement, its unproved edge cannot be reduced
to a qualified report after failure. The theorem subject, proof origin,
imported artifact, current semantic dependencies and real issuer must
match before evidence may enter a release claim.

The revision also clarifies that sound production admission need not
be logically complete: rejecting some admissible formulas may be
safe, though the selected useful supported subset and positive
product controls remain independent obligations. This avoids both
claiming complete kernel implementation from rejection examples
and certifying a reject-everything tool as useful.

## Thirty-two R4/R5 preservation and strengthening decisions

1. The milestone identifies exact theorem/proposition subject and selected profile.
2. The first formal target is the closed propositional kernel, not arbitrary Clojure.
3. Every seven-rule constructor and side condition has independent semantics.
4. The admitted formula syntax and inference rule set are distinct concepts.
5. Opaque Boolean atom valuation does not prove a business fact about Request data.
6. Named used premises remain open; unused labels cannot authorize strengthening.
7. S0 formal rule soundness covers legal arities, indices and conclusions.
8. S1 formal derivation soundness covers every finite ordered valid derivation.
9. S0/S1 formal relation is independently defined, not a call to production.
10. Abstract entailment and satisfiable/reachable business initiation are separate.
11. S2 relates every production-accepted supported proof input to the formal relation.
12. Parser, normalizer, source identity, references and issuer admission are in S2 scope.
13. Malformed physical inputs omitted from the formal model must not bypass the transfer.
14. Soundness of what is accepted does not imply checker completeness over valid proofs.
15. S3 binds typed atom meaning, readers, presence/nil and actual evaluator assumptions.
16. S4 binds source-derived obligations, genuine issuer and consuming artifact generation.
17. Checked S0/S1 alone do not prove S2–S4 or production safety.
18. Trusted S2/S3/S4 bridges stay named and conditional when allowed by the profile.
19. Required stronger proved bridges cannot silently downgrade to trusted premises.
20. Production source/issuer and installed physical effects remain separate correspondence subjects.
21. Semantic-to-physical safety transfer requires initial coverage, step simulation and reflection.
22. Liveness additionally requires fairness, divergence and environment progress premises.
23. External proof packages include theorem versions, imports, trusted kernel, axioms and toolchain.
24. Genuine proof terms/checkable artifacts and observed machine-checking replace status maps.
25. Signatures identify trusted attributed bytes under assumptions, not proposition truth.
26. Evidence import/recheck binds current theorem subject, checker/issuer and source origin.
27. Changed production parser may invalidate S2 while S0/S1 remain mathematically reusable.
28. Finite exact search, partial search and unbounded theorem are distinct evidence categories.
29. Unsupported syntax, resource exhaustion and unavailable checker are nonpassing statuses.
30. Independent oracles detect potential common-mode source/formal translation bugs.
31. Application authors declare Gesso rules; proof backend is a replaceable framework mechanism.
32. This separate external milestone does not imply physical XTDB/browser refinement,
    whole HumanHelp product acceptance or multi-node production completion.

All R4 essential formal correspondence and trust duties survive.
R5's more precise separation of the bounded logical theorem,
production checker transfer and conditional release assumptions
remains intact. No mandatory later Part X proof family has been
silently promoted to completed baseline work.

## Actual Gesso v1622 correspondence and nonclaims

- src/gesso/assurance/proof/kernel.clj defines a restricted
  seven-rule propositional derivation checker for independently
  reparsed original source, named premises, well-formed step
  references and final goal. Its namespace explicitly disclaims
  independent meta-soundness, runtime authority, and arbitrary
  Clojure/XTDB/CLJS effects.

  JVM-local checked handles for propositional, guarded-transition

  kernel soundness and physical execution/refinement.
- src/gesso/assurance/proof/application_assurance_report.clj
  produces a nonauthoritative conditional original-source
  report and deliberately retains independent kernel soundness,
  installed-application adoption, physical XTDB writer
  completeness, universal physical transition refinement and
  browser correspondence as open obligations.

This source review found **no basis** to treat the currently
implemented local checker as independently machine-proved
sound, the production parser/issuer as transferred to an
independent S1 calculus, or arbitrary installed XTDB/browser
execution as a formally verified refinement. It also did
not establish that such a prover or formal CI task does
not exist elsewhere; the claim is deliberately limited
to the implementation sources inspected.

No Lean, other external prover, JVM, real XTDB, Chromium,
physical installed-host, HumanHelp or multi-node verification
jobs were run for this editorial revision. The future
contract is not a record of executed theorem verification.
