# R5.1 Part III — 03.18 Necessary Communication Versus Authoritative Reread

**R4 baseline:** `main` Part III, “Projection preserves necessary communication but does not worship messages,” including the HumanHelp Request publication and ephemeral typing contrast.
**R5 candidate:** the same principle and the modular worked examples in 03.19–03.20.
**Disposition:** focused R4 preservation and forward-design audit; not a release-status report or whole-Part-III acceptance.

## Normative R4 obligations preserved

- Communication is one possible evidence path, not the definition of knowledge.
- The projector preserves a necessary communication and does not generate redundant messages solely because a global choice affects another role.
- Justified role-local entry facts, authoritative observation, derivation, and other admitted paths may establish the needed fact when allowed by the *authored* semantics.
- Durable Request authority can serve as a rendezvous for separate executions, while an ephemeral typing indicator has a different loss, ordering and expiry contract.
- No projected role receives unearned facts or private authority merely because a global value is available.

## Superior R5 treatment retained

- Keep the next two source units' separate, detailed Request publication/adoption and typing-intent/expiry contracts, rather than reintroducing R4's larger mixed example in this editorial section.
- Durable Request publication uses a command-bound accepted outcome, scoped advisory, authoritative acquisition and adoption; the advisory is not authoritative content.
- Ephemeral typing uses authorized reports, source ordering, local timers and permitted loss, without demanding durable replay.
- Preserve the distinction between a current authoritative state and the historical outcome of a particular operation.

## Focused changes

1. Differentiate a required *authored communication occurrence* from a fact that could be learned by a different authorized path. Altering the observable protocol requires an explicit authored alternative or justified refinement.
2. Define knowledge-path substitutability relative to the exact needed proposition, subject, issuer, role, command history, temporal scope and disclosure, not data shape.
3. Specify that authoritative rereads establish admitted current facts but do not automatically prove command-specific historical outcomes; a retained receipt or changed continuation may be required.
4. Make recovery obligations source-dependent: durable rendezvous needs initial/reconnect/lost-advisory repair, while ephemeral signals may expire without replay.
5. Distinguish advisory, acquisition, physical installation, and semantic adoption without claiming that all durable facts require an advisory.
6. State liveness assumptions rather than presuming eventual messages, reads, user actions or subscriptions.
7. Add four semantic-path examples and six adversarial counterexamples.

## Current implementation correspondence (audit only)

- `gesso.choreo.verify` distinguishes global value availability from definite role-local knowledge and definite-authoritative provenance; its assumptions and language coverage are bounded.
- `gesso.choreo.project` preserves declared communications, tracks foreign authoritative barriers and rejects certain uncommunicated divergent branches; its emitted plan is not itself a universal refinement proof.
- `gesso.choreo.knowledge` distinguishes communicated and authoritative sources while requiring explicit progression for changed authoritative observation bases.
- Gesso Live's browser adapter owns normalized effects and resource/generation checks, but the full application-specific adoption and delivery-closure guarantees belong to the assembled realization, not to one source file.
- These are lessons for the completed design, not its limits or an implementation-status statement in the canonical text.
