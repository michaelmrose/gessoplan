# Part III — whole-part reconciliation 08: claim and installed-effect applicability

**Branch:** `r5.1`.
**Disposition:** focused whole-part cross-part assurance reconciliation.
Part III's final acceptance remains open. This audit records intended

## Source-derived issue

Passes 05–07 distinguished durable work from manager sends,
receiver consumption, worker-origin communication, and physical
notification fulfillment. They also distinguished three chosen
global notification graphs. The worked plan still needed a
precise *claim-to-evidence join*: a valid terminal proof for
one global plan is not a physical certification for a later
worker or a deployment-wide release.

Parts IV, VII, IX, X and XI consistently distinguish issued
command/outcome evidence, actual retained work, installed worker
and provider paths, conditional claims, and selected release
profiles. But the Part III generic global/physical relationship
did not explicitly enumerate the bridge obligations for the
separate work-owned execution.

## Normative changes

1. **03.04:** add a property-by-property evidence table for:
   review/global terminal; C2 authoritative incorporation;
   protected durable work; admissible worker execution; physical
   effect issue/acceptance/fulfillment; and conditional progress.
   Each row names its subject and what weaker evidence cannot
   establish.
2. **03.04:** require trustworthy identity and issuer joins across
   independently admitted operations, work items, attempts,
   physical providers and artifact/deployment subjects.
   Choreo acceptance never manufactures an installed worker.
3. **03.04:** require the requested release profile to determine
   whether work-recorded, physical completion or conditional
   eventual resolution is mandatory. A stronger claim cannot be
   inferred from weaker terminal evidence, but valid weaker
   applications remain admissible.
4. **03.25:** add four adversarial cases covering unsupported
   delivery claims, missing installed recovery, mismatched
   work-to-provider correlation, and dispatch mistaken for
   human-visible receipt.
5. **03.25:** add two positive constructions demonstrating
   scoped composition with actual installed bindings, and
   lawful review completion while later delivery debt remains
   open under a conditional progress contract.
