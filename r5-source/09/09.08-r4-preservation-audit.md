## Governing decision

Retain R5's stronger architecture of a typed distributed environment
with *separate knowledge, provenance, authority and projection judgments*.
Unlike simple typechecking, distributed admissibility binds a predicate
to a specific participant, point, source observation and intended use.
Unlike a single execution replay, a universal projection theorem must
quantify over every permitted local schedule and transport behavior
under its stated abstraction. R4 requires both concepts and a
certificate hierarchy that does not stop at abstract choreography;
R5.1 now supplies concrete, falsifiable boundaries for each.

The section remains part of a **timeless future-system specification**.
Gesso implementation evidence documents genuine precursors and current
nonclaims. It is not used to reduce the required finished framework
to whatever today's v1622 tests already cover. The user still writes
normal Clojure/Gesso rules and application construction; framework
checkers produce the distributed proof obligations.

## R4 preservation decisions

1. **Role-local rule admissibility is separate from intrinsic typing.**
   A request.private-review predicate can be a valid typed Boolean
   and still be inadmissible in the browser. The checked judgment
   binds role, exact program point, reachable branch, fact subject,
   producer, provenance, source scope and any authority/basis/lease
   requirements. Merely placing a field in global syntax does not
   grant every participant access.

2. **Knowledge and authority are different categories.** The browser
   may know a historical Request/command fact but lack current
   permission to mutate it. An authenticated model operation may
   permit invocation without guaranteeing commit. A schema-valid
   value or caller-supplied role does not grant an authorized principal.
   Source-derived claim and actual server-enforced claim must remain
   separate evidence classes.

3. **Definite availability requires all relevant incoming paths.**
   A must-analysis preserves a fact at a join only if it is known
   with compatible subject and provenance on every reachable path.
   One positive branch is insufficient; a checked conditional
   representation may retain separate facts without promoting
   them to unconditional knowledge. Unreachable alternatives
   require a checked exclusion, not missing proof generation.

4. **Provenance is tied to an exact observation and can degrade.**
   Entry, communicated, authoritative, derived and asserted
   origins carry different qualifications. Overwriting a key
   with an assertion cannot retain prior authoritative provenance
   automatically. An old basis, different tenant, overwritten
   field or retired generation cannot inherit current knowledge
   merely by sharing a familiar keyword.

5. **R4's knowledge-transfer constructors remain differentiated.**
   Admitted entry values belong to the named role. Sender
   communication conveys only matched, declared and authorized
   required fields; optional payloads remain optional.
   A valid =:await= environment event is not the same
   as a participant receive. A possible future
   authoritative read does not prove an actual
   observation, and open envelope extras cannot
   mint new semantic knowledge.

6. **Confidentiality is not solved by sending more data.**
   To repair a browser branch lacking the private approval
   field, the application can keep the policy decision
   server-side and send only an authorized derived result.
   The new disclosure remains subject to public model,
   issuer, scope and recipient policies. R4's typed
   message contract alone was never permission to
   reveal private Organization/Request details.

7. **Authoritative basis and progression are qualified.**
   A basis token is an opaque value under its provider's
   partial-order and scope contract; the browser cannot
   manufacture ordering from arbitrary timestamps.
   A past authenticated observation does not establish
   a current XTDB transaction prestate. Successive
   rereads, signed message origin and physical acquisition
   require their own actual boundaries and premises.

8. **Outcome semantics preserve historical command identity.**
   =:committed=, =:rejected=, =:already-incorporated=
   and =:commit-unknown= are authoritative dispositions
   with variant-specific evidence. Presentation
   =:accepted= / =:rejected= / =:unknown= remains
   separate. A current Request card, generic HTTP 200,
   or later status does not reconstruct whether C42
   historically incorporated; a lost response is not
   equivalent to a rejection.

9. **Remote advisory adoption is an independent product policy.**
   Accepted R5.1 Part VIII selects remote lifecycle
   advisory-only, including already-visible Request rows.
   SSE debt does not authorize whole-board refresh.
   Creator-local S can be installed selectively without
   importing R; a user-issued one-read Refresh requires
   actual eligible query and physical installation,
   scope/coverage and leftover debt accounting.
   Conditional Choreo knowledge does not itself
   issue that browser resource.

10. **R4's distinct structural, simulation and progress scopes survive.**
    Structural boundary/origin preservation checks do not
    establish all-schedules trace refinement. Concrete
    global/local lockstep and weak replay are valid
    evidence about *one* supplied history. Universal
    refinement requires a quantified relation
    over admitted local schedules and a justified global
    explanation; equivalence requires the converse,
    and temporal progress needs availability/fairness
    hypotheses beyond mere structural terminal reachability.

11. **Global communication and projected queue semantics differ.**
    Local sends, pending queue messages, delivery, drops,
    duplication, retry and environment events are distinct.
    A global semantic communication corresponds to
    the appropriate projected delivery, not an arbitrary
    enqueued but never received envelope. Legal hidden
    local steps can commute; protected authoritative
    commits and observation origins cannot be
    reordered without a checked relation.

12. **Role-local completion does not certify global termination.**
    Finishing one projected role is not proof all participants
    reached the appropriate global outcome. A terminal
    compatibility judgment must include which roles
    completed, pending messages/resources, the declared
    result and assumptions about late faults or divergence.
    A local =:return= label is insufficient.

13. **Complete original-source obligation generation is a gate.**
    All relevant entry, branch, receive, await, negative,
    cancellation, timeout, unknown outcome, resource/retirement,
    continuation and terminal cases must be derived from
    the original checked choreography and independently
    required operation/product inventory. Correct proofs
    for *volunteered* branches do not certify an omitted
    Reassign or unreachable-by-assumption failure path.

14. **The implementation chain goes beyond projection.**
    Source/semantic projection, lowerer, ExecutablePlan payload,
    JS asset manifest, actual served bytes, installed Ring/Biff
    routes, current model/writer and browser instance have
    distinct correspondence obligations. Hashes and structural
    checkers may verify equality under declared semantics,
    but do not attest to a physical event's issuer or
    prevent a wrong served artifact by themselves.

15. **Positive and negative controls are non-vacuous.**
    A legitimate entry/communication/authoritative observation,
    disclosed public derived policy, source-bound Choreo,
    compliant projection and real Claim/Refresh path must
    succeed under their profiles. Mutations must reject at
    the first applicable static, dynamic, disclosure,
    model or installed-host boundary. A reject-all system
    is not a valid distributed assurance design.

## Gesso v1622 correspondence: source inspection, not test execution

- `src/gesso/choreo/knowledge.cljc` provides a pure role-local
  knowledge representation with separate =:input=,
  =:communicated=, =:authoritative=, =:derived=
  and =:asserted= provenance. Its own source stresses that
  data shape does not prove the semantic or physical
  authority of the represented issuer. Authoritative
  observation/basis is a separate named case.

- `src/gesso/choreo/verify.cljc` performs source-derived
  finite graph, definite-value and role-local must-knowledge
  analyses. Its documentation explicitly limits claims:
  source/classification checking does not prove actual
  backend authenticity, optional sender fields, universal
  projection refinement or liveness. Entry-knowledge
  options are checked rather than blindly accepted.

- `src/gesso/choreo/project.cljc` projects a verified
  choreography into role-local plans, with distinct
  sender/receiver and environment await representations,
  closed message contracts and rejection of ambiguous
  receiver alternatives. It does not independently
  certify execution/physical transport correctness.

- `src/gesso/choreo/proof.cljc` has several narrow
  structural properties for reachable boundaries,
  successor, completion and exact global origins.
  The current `ProjectionStructuralCertificate`
  is explicit about its structural class and
  nonclaims, including all-schedules trace refinement,
  physical authority authenticity and complete
  runtime observation freshness.

- `src/gesso/choreo/semantics.cljc` specifies the
  portable executable global semantic transition
  system, including global `:communicate=` and
  separately interpreted distributed observables.
  It is deliberately independent of the verification
  and projection namespaces so the reference machine
  is not simply the same code path.

- `src/gesso/choreo/realization.cljc` is a deterministic
  in-memory harness for separately projected
  role-local machines with explicit message queue
  and schedule. It does not simulate HTMX, XTDB,
  true network scheduling or installed browser
  execution, and is not a general refinement proof.

- `src/gesso/choreo/correspondence.cljc`
  provides concrete lockstep and weak global/local
  witness checking; it explicitly does **not**
  quantify over all possible projected schedules
  or prove liveness. Hidden steps and role-local
  terminal matching retain distinct meaning.

- `src/gesso/live/browser/refinement_correspondence.cljc`
  checks one browser-observation shape/order witness
  against a model replay. Its source explicitly
  keeps actual network event origin, real browser
  instance linkage, physical handler identity
  and XTDB incorporation as open prerequisites.

- `test/gesso/choreo/verify_test.cljc`,
  `proof_test.cljc` and `correspondence_test.cljc`
  contain positive and adversarial definitions
  about definite knowledge, reachable source
  boundaries, receive/send origin, weak hidden
  commutation, terminal compatibility and
  basis-progression conditions. They were
  inspected, **not executed** during this revision.

These sources make the prospective specification
credible and provide concrete cross-model tests.
They do not establish all-schedules universal
projection correspondence or deployed browser/
host authenticity. Where the future design
requires those stronger claims, the current
narrow property remains a valid component,
not a substitute for the missing bridge.

## Independent positive and adversarial histories

1. Correct entry knowledge for the declared owner role
   is accepted; the same fact forged as browser
   entry knowledge for another role is rejected.
2. A required message field sent by a knowing,
   authorized sender transfers knowledge to
   its matched receiver; an absent required
   field or wrong sender fails.
3. Optional communicated fields remain
   optional and open envelope extras
   cannot mint stronger knowledge.
4. Branch fact produced only on one
   incoming path is not definitely
   available at an unqualified join.
5. An authoritative Request observation
   supports the bounded field/scope
   predicate after its actual acquisition;
   a similarly shaped assertion
   does not provide authority.
6. Overwriting authoritative R17 status
   with local/asserted data cannot
   preserve a previous strong
   provenance proof.
7. Browser branch on protected private
   review fails when disclosure/
   knowledge is unavailable; server
   derived public approval succeeds
   under the authorized policy.
8. The same old basis/status for R17
   cannot become a fresh protected
   guard for a new actor or current
   transaction automatically.
9. Client-supplied helper role or
   Organization eligibility cannot
   replace the server's current
   Membership/model authorization.
10. Real commit-unknown remains
    historically uncertain rather
    than being inferred rejected
    from a later Request card.
11. Creator S is installed without
    importing remote R; SSE advisory
    debt does not trigger a
    board-wide collection fetch.
12. User-permitted one-read Refresh
    completes its actual DOM
    installation and retains
    uncovered advisory debt.
13. Structural projection checks
    all required reachable message
    and continuation boundaries
    on a valid global Choreo.
14. A source mutation removes a
    required receiver alternative,
    changes a branch discriminator
    or substitutes a global
    source origin; structural
    checking fails appropriately.
15. A lockstep concrete witness
    succeeds for one legal
    send/receive schedule, not
    every possible transport choice.
16. A weak witness accepts
    a legal hidden-local commute
    while rejecting illegal
    authoritative reordering.
17. A global terminal result
    is not inferred just because
    one local role has returned
    while other roles/messages
    remain pending.
18. A duplicated or delayed
    envelope cannot impersonate
    a distinct original sender
    occurrence or current grant.
19. Changing browser artifact
    while copying plan stamp
    cannot establish installed
    source/plan correspondence.
20. A real trusted server Claim
    succeeds in an authorized
    positive case; a forged
    principal and a retired
    browser execution are
    rejected at actual boundaries.
