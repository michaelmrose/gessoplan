** Systematic mutation and conformance corpus
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: req-certification-mutation-corpus
:END:

The mutation corpus is an *executable adversarial specification* of
Gesso's admission, semantic interpretation, proof, source-closure,
application realization and physical-effect boundaries. It must establish
that a supported invalid change is rejected by its actual responsible
gate *and* that an unchanged valid program remains constructible and
usable under the same declared profile. It is not a percentage of code
covered, a replacement for the independent HumanHelp product oracle,
or a proof of soundness of all Clojure, XTDB, HTMX or the browser.

The corpus contains several independently meaningful positive baselines:
small closed source terms for exact semantic checking; valid proof and
conditional-premise constructions; real admitted operation, Choreo and
ApplicationAssembly sources; and the production-backed HumanHelp
Request/RequestAssignment Claim slice from Part VIII and 09.13. A
mutation that only changes a checksum before the claimed semantic
decision is reached exercises identity rejection, not the more
interesting purported invariant/authorization checker. Both are
useful, but their conclusions must be named separately.

*** Predeclared case contract and expected dispositions

Each case is data describing a precise *claim*, not an opaque
"this test should fail." Before execution the corpus fixes:

- immutable baseline/source identity, model/runtime profile, selected
  application/deployment generation and relevant provider versions;
- isolated mutation operator and its actual introduction point:
  original unadmitted source, proposed elaboration, stored untrusted
  package bytes, admitted dependency *candidate*, installed artifact,
  physical effect boundary, or an external environment/failure seam;
- affected proposition and checked judgment, assumed source coverage,
  the specific forbidden artifact or runtime effect, and expected
  valid behavior of an independent positive control;
- expected result, first enforcing boundary, stable structured
  classification and permissible alternative *early* failures
  only where their cause is actually prior in that path;
- authoritative checker, observation instrument and physical
  evidence needed to decide whether an unsafe effect occurred,
  including the test's own assumption/inspection limitations.

A case may be *correctly rejected*, *accepted by design*
because it is well typed and coherent under the selected rules,
or *correctly admitted with precisely stated conditional
premises*. These are not interchangeable pass labels. An
unexpected acceptance, wrong reason, broken valid control,
unauthorized effect, forged evidence promotion, or
unjustified release disposition fails the case. Resource
exhaustion, checker crash, test harness interruption,
missing instrumentation and an unsupported checking mechanism
are *incomplete test outcomes*, even when the requested
strong profile properly refuses admission. A negative
test whose specifically expected disposition is
=:unsupported= may establish that unsupported-feature
boundary works, but not that the requested feature is
implemented.

Each runner records the *actual* first cause and
observation. If the direct composition API rejects
stale dependency C21 against O15, its expected result
may be =:semantic-dependency-mismatch=. The normal
production cache may instead miss, re-elaborate and
reject unchanged C21 for unknown old policy field;
that is acceptable only for a distinct predeclared
production-path oracle ensuring no stale executable
Claim is admitted. It must not change a direct
composition test's expected failure after the fact.

*** Required mutation families and protected meanings

| Family | Perturbation and first relevant guarantee |
|--------+--------------------------------------------|
| Schema and nominal identity | Missing/retyped field, optional to required or reverse, nullable versus absent, different nominal enum, closed variant added/removed, relationship endpoint/cardinality drift; a valid new schema can make an old consumer stale without itself being malformed. |
| Rule interpretation and evaluation | Boolean truthiness versus presence, nil versus missing, short-circuit or branch refinement reordering, illegal ordered-enum use, integer/codec boundary, different reader footprint or stale field; compare actual typed meaning, not just parsed shape. |
| Propositional derivation | Forged premise, shifted conclusion, changed antecedent, forward reference, incorrect conjunction child, injected checker rule, hidden inherited assumption or false goal under the original premise context. |
| Guarded transition and frame | Candidate prestate used as atomic prestate, wrong guard, omitted simultaneous assignment, wrong poststate, absent/nil conflation, invalid frame or effect outside the protected declared write footprint. |
| Inductive obligations and finite search | Omitted base/invariant/operation, changed shared initial state, incomplete reachability, contradictory/vacuous Init, partial exploration labelled exhaustive, valid safe fragment substituted for full original source. |
| Required source/dependency closure | Source-required graph edge/operation removed while the residual DAG remains structurally valid, changed upstream interface, fabricated compatible bridge, stale source+stale graph passed as current, or unjustified proof cycle. |
| Premise propagation and discharge | Copied assertion presented as proof, inherited premise dropped, evidence from different actor/Request/Organization/transaction used to discharge a required premise, or checked conclusion stripped of new conditional premises. |
| Choreo and role-local admissibility | Unknown discriminator, hidden private branch, unsupported receive overlap, lost actor/source knowledge, asserted-to-authoritative provenance promotion, communication omitted, unsupported outcome/recovery path or projection attached to wrong Choreo identity. |
| Outcome and durable command history | HTTP status masquerading as commit, compatible C42 replay written twice, incompatible command binding reused, old historical outcome inferred from current card, =:commit-unknown= converted to rejection, missing retention or unauthorized command-result disclosure. |
| Browser scope and resource ownership | Wrong target, OOB partial install, superseded execution/lease, stale plan, duplicate one-use Refresh capability, remote advisory silently becoming acquisition, or creator-local S importing unrelated remote collection R. |
| Certificates and untrusted admission | Success map, forged fingerprint, checker-status field, manually created opaque handle, stale/cyclic dependency, executable reader/unknown tag, partial durable package or unsupported checker version accepted as an issued certificate. |
| Issued implementation and actual host | Changed closure/Var, substituted handler, uninstalled issued executor, forged route/source inventory, unregistered direct XTDB writer, modified compiled browser bytes with copied stamp, unqualified provider change. |
| Physical effect and observation | Caller-supplied principal/node/prestate, second diagnostic transaction, mismatched real invocation/result, co-observed ledger/command records called causal without binding, post-effect failure reported as prevented incorporation. |
| Completeness and checker budgets | Truncated model/trace, weakened type domain, omitted mutant path, timeout, unusable external solver, opaque unsupported code or infrastructure failure reported as proved/accepted without proper scope. |
| Product policy and user capability | Accepted coherent but surprising rule, original HumanHelp Claim/Unclaim/Reassign positive retained, independent required capability removed from both program and proof, disabled Claim to satisfy only negatives, or unselected response flow declared complete. |

These families are *minimum semantic coverage*, not a fixed list
of particular file names. Every class requires a meaningful
positive neighbor. A type checker can correctly reject
=emailed < 18= while accepting a well-typed =age > 18=
policy even if the owner intended =age >= 18=. If
the latter violates an independently declared invariant,
the expected result changes to a property counterexample,
not retroactive classification as malformed syntax.
Human intent is not an implicit rule generated by
the mutation harness.

Typed semantics also require accepted and rejected
conformance cases for optional =absent= versus present
=nil=, nominal equality, ordered-vector versus
unordered-map normalization, short-circuit presence
guarding, branch-local knowledge, simultaneous writes,
reader footprints and actual source-derived operation
and invariant completeness. A changed rule can be
syntactically valid yet fail its *use-site* authority,
disclosure or temporal provenance obligation.

*** Injection points and immutable category discipline

Source mutations operate on fresh *unadmitted*
production declarations, before their source
construction/checker pipeline. A mutation of
a purported elaborated proof is candidate input
to an independent original-source checker.
Serialized-certificate forgery mutates inert
package bytes before safe decoding and
rechecking. It must never be modeled by
mutating a private, issuer-registered live handle
as if such a handle were an ordinary writable
application map.

An implementation mutation may replace an
unadmitted callable or dynamically selected
host dependency before issuance; a later
deployment substitution must be injected at
the actual trusted installation or runtime
boundary. The two test different claims.
An already issued certificate may describe
an unchanged abstract theorem while its
*physical applicability* fails against the
changed handler, provider, served asset,
or owner generation. It is not sound to
credit a semantic checker with detecting
a post-installation substitution that
it could not observe.

All mutation operators declare their
allowed edit footprint. Accidental changes
to the baseline source, unrelated fixture
users, provider trust assumptions or
execution order can invalidate the test
rather than exercise the intended claim.
Corpus generation is deterministic from
a recorded seed and original inputs,
or reports the chosen randomized seed,
shrinking/minimization history and
replayable schedule. Isolation includes
fresh checker registration, caches,
XTDB test node/session, browser state
and app generation where appropriate.
An order-dependent pass is a test bug
until its environmental assumptions
have been stated and controlled.

For physical mutations, the harness
uses an actual permissible effect
seam: model guarded submit, real
route/authentication dispatch,
host-selected writer, generated
browser-origin command, emitted
and served client asset or real
DOM target/adoption. A browser test
that manually inserts
=__gesso_live_optimistic_command=
exercises the server protocol
only; it is not the real
browser-to-command acceptance
oracle. Injecting a fake
submitted transaction receipt
does not establish an actual
XTDB observation. Separate
tests may deliberately exercise
synthetic payload validation,
but they report that narrower
boundary.

*** Oracle ownership and prohibition of post-hoc credit

The mutant's returned report, exception
or output value is *not* its own
independent success oracle. A
checker-origin verification result
can justify its stated internal
judgment; whether a forbidden
artifact was issued or a physical
effect occurred requires an
independent collector at the
relevant trusted boundary.
For example, an instrumented
writer sink must observe whether
a RequestAssignment was mutated;
an unrelated test-local boolean
=:effect-observed? false= supplied
by the mutant is not evidence.

The runner must distinguish all of:

- *Prevented:* admission or effect gate
  rejected before prohibited construction,
  submission, disclosure, acquisition or
  adoption, and a qualified independent
  observer establishes its absence
  within the declared observation window.
- *Detected after effect:* a checker
  accurately reported an inconsistency
  after an actual forbidden effect.
  This is useful diagnostics, but fails
  a policy whose obligation was prevention.
- *Unknown/incomplete:* the collector
  cannot establish whether a write,
  browser update, transport command
  or physical submission occurred,
  or the evidence retention horizon
  expired. Absence of a report or
  =nil= is not proof of non-occurrence.
- *Correct positive:* the legitimate
  original source was admitted and
  executed through the same relevant
  path with an independently observed
  permitted result.

The first enforcing gate can be rule
typing, proof kernel, required-source
closure, dependency composition,
assembly/preflight, trusted ingress,
atomic model writer, installed plan
or browser effect resource. A
negative test must exercise *the
earliest actual applicable one*
under the declared profile, not
force every failure into a
generic proof error. Conversely,
a properly rejected abstract
mutation cannot claim that the
XTDB writer was tested at all.

A corpus-specific exception from the
test harness is an infrastructure
event unless the expected check
genuinely comes from the production
boundary being exercised. Unexpected
errors and console failures are not
suppressed to make a browser test
green. The harness proves no absent
behavior for physical paths that its
instrumentation did not cover.

*** Representative case data and runner obligation

A finished framework can expose a
declarative corpus runner without
making application developers
write proof terms. The following
is a *conceptual fixture schema*,
not an existing Gesso API or
a set of guaranteed exported
namespace functions:

#+begin_src clojure
{:case/id :organization/stale-claim-certificate
 :baseline/ref :humanhelp/request-claim
 :profile/ref :humanhelp/checked-conditional
 :injection {:at :certificate/compose-candidate
             :change :organization/O14-to-O15
             :candidate :old-claim-rules-C21}
 :claim/ref :request/claim-eligible
 :required-boundary :source-dependency-composition
 :expected {:status :rejected
            :reason :semantic-dependency-mismatch}
 :must-not {:admit :old-Claim-executor
            :perform :request/claim-incorporation}
 :independent-oracles
 [:fresh-original-source :issued-handle-registry
  :qualified-writer-sink]
 :positive-control/ref :humanhelp/repaired-claim-C22}
#+end_src

The corresponding production-path case
can instead exercise a cache miss and
fresh elaboration of unchanged C21,
expecting unknown removed field or
another predeclared earliest genuine
source failure. Both must establish
that old C21 is not installed. An
unchanged source/name with a changed
opaque implementation must have a
*different* strong-realization
mutant: valid abstract proof reuse
does not authorize the new physical
writer without qualified binding.

R4's conceptual runner and
=:mutant/id=, =:baseline=,
=:mutate=, =:exercise= and
=:expect= declarations remain
a useful design precedent.
The implementation may choose
different public data names.
Required behavior is not dependent
on those exact identifiers.
A faithful runner reconstructs
fresh baseline source for each
case, verifies its valid
control first, exercises the
mutated source at the declared
boundary, and collects separate
checker and independent
artifact/effect evidence.
It compares the result against
the *predeclared* oracle and
emits a stable witness of
where the mutant was rejected
or deliberately accepted.

For readability, one schematic
outcome record is:

#+begin_src clojure
{:case/id :projection/missing-helper-knowledge
 :baseline {:status :accepted
            :evidence/ref baseline-evidence}
 :actual {:status :rejected
          :boundary :choreo/use-site
          :reason :knowledge-not-established}
 :independent {:issued-invalid-plan? false
               :observed-unauthorized-effect? false
               :scope/ref inspected-boundary}
 :disposition :correct-rejection}
#+end_src

Such a record only warrants the
disposition if independent
observation is genuine and the
scope is appropriate. An arbitrary
map containing the same fields is
not proof that the runner executed.

*** Positive, accepted and conditional controls

An adversarial suite is incomplete
without successful constructions.
For each injected defect, the
baseline is independently rerun
through the same supported path.
The corpus also requires:

- semantically unchanged source
  formatting or unordered-map
  entry order that may reuse a
  qualified abstract proof while
  refreshing relevant diagnostics;
- a legitimate new Organization
  closed-enum policy and fully
  repaired Claim C22 whose checked
  dependency closure is accepted
  rather than rejecting all
  interface evolution;
- a coherent application-owned
  age/approval policy change
  correctly classified as
  *accepted by design*, separate
  from independent product policy
  review and release acceptance;
- a trusted opaque model
  implementation permitted
  explicitly under one conditional
  profile with its premises intact,
  contrasted with an inadmissible
  strong profile requiring
  evidence that is not present;
- non-vacuous initial states,
  enabled Claim/Unclaim/Reassign,
  real eligible Create and the
  chosen positive browser paths
  under current model authority;
- actual command C42 replay,
  rightfully rejected new
  incompatible command,
  truthful submitted-unknown
  handling, creator-local S,
  remote advisory R and one
  scoped explicit Refresh;
- benign incidental code/asset
  changes that do not require
  rebuilding unrelated
  abstract theorems, alongside
  invalidation of affected
  installation correspondence.

Positive product gates remain
independent from proof/construction.
Removing Reassign from both the
source catalog and generated
model may leave a safe reduced
machine while failing HumanHelp's
required user capability. A
checker that refuses all Claim
attempts cannot satisfy a
positive Claim oracle. A
successful Claim pilot cannot
complete Part VIII's unselected
response-flow product decision
or other independent gates.

*** Independent semantic and physical conformance

Differential conformance compares
truly separate semantic
interpretations where practical,
rather than two wrappers calling
the same shared buggy evaluator.
For finite propositional checking,
a separately implemented Boolean
valuation oracle can distinguish
a semantically invalid accepted
step; it cannot establish theorem
truth of the opaque business
predicates represented by atoms.
For typed rules, the independent
denotation needs to preserve
presence, nil, nominal types,
short-circuit order and exact
reader footprints. Tests of
normalization must actually
execute the source and normalized
forms over discriminating
input states, not merely
compare their pretty printing.

Where a typed/rule mutation
changes the proof statement,
the test must show a witness
that activates its altered
meaning or demonstrate the
specific static rejection.
A mutation that never changes
the evaluated predicate is
not counted as a meaningful
semantic disagreement. A
proof checker may reject
semantically valid but
unprovable statements under
its selected limited rules;
that is an honest
unsupported/unproved status,
not evidence that the
formula is false.

Transition and induction
oracles need complete declared
initializations, relevant
state assignments and
operation cases. Finite search
reports actual bounds,
visited states, truncation
and whether a counterexample
is reachable under the exact
abstract model. A model
counterexample gives a
reproducible abstract trace,
not a physical XTDB history.
A solver that times out or
sees no states must not
report invariant preservation
as an unconditional theorem.

For installed host/XTDB and
browser tests, the corpus
records a qualified
environment version,
source/asset/plan and
service generation, actor,
command, application
declaration, expected
source-to-physical binding
and actual independent
observation. Tests should
cover concurrent guarded
RequestAssignment Claims,
changed Membership,
adversarial routing/writer
bypasses, valid durable
replay and lost result,
real generated browser
command, settled and
unknown UI, and actual
target-specific OOB
completion or failed
installation. Causality
and source completeness
remain conditional until
qualified evidence actually
establishes them; matching
transaction keys, ledger
rows and cards can be
mere co-observation.

Physical safety and
qualified progress must
have separate oracles.
A lost SSE invalidation,
missing outbox worker,
retired browser lease
or refresh-denied remote
viewer can satisfy one
safety proposition while
leaving a bounded recovery
or environment-progress
obligation open. The
expected positive behavior
follows the *selected*
HumanHelp Live/Refresh
policy, not automatic
whole-board convergence
invented by the harness.

*** Reporting, audit and completion boundary

A reproducible corpus report
preserves case ID,
original and mutated
source identity, profile,
injection point,
expected/actual structured
disposition, checker and
independent observation
origin, first enforcing
boundary, positive control,
tested semantic/provider
versions and relevant
source/test evidence.
It distinguishes missing
case coverage, cases
unsupported by a checker,
incomplete executions,
deliberately accepted
application policy,
correctly caught malformed
programs, property
counterexamples and
physical effect failures.

A published mutant
rejection score may help
track regression in this
specific corpus. It must
not call an unsupported
feature success, infer
checker soundness, prove
that every possible policy
branch was examined, or
convert a collection
of bounded physical
diagnostics into a
universal refinement
theorem. The independent
root report ties
the particular claim
and evidence to the
source/host generation,
premises and product
gate that they actually
qualify.

The corpus can be
incrementally expanded
as Gesso grows. Its
acceptance criterion is
*meaningful discrimination
at actual trusted
boundaries*: correct
negative cases reject,
correct positive cases
remain usable, and
conditional/unsupported/
untested cases retain
honest scopes. No
future source addition,
test refactor or
cached summary may
silently weaken a
predeclared case's
expected guarantee.
