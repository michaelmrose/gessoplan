## Governing decision

Retain R5's typed per-proposition profile as the mechanism of
assurance, rather than R4's longer catalogue as a second proof
language or a global completion percentage. Restore the lost
R4 diagnostic examples and the important distinction between
source invalidity, checked counterexample and coherent but
potentially wrong product policy. Make actual profiles,
checker/evidence boundary, source-derived coverage, and
installed effect authorization independent admission gates.
Policy audit must derive from existing source rather than
maintaining a rival model.

## R4 preservation and normative decisions

1. **Profiles are versioned policies over actual claims.**
   Required judgments, source universe, accepted evidence,
   premise types, checker/rule semantics, installed context,
   applicability and positive product oracles are distinct
   policy dimensions, not one =:certified?= field.

2. **Baseline assembly is not optional.** Even a selected
   weaker profile cannot bypass the Part VII structural
   operation, route, asset, trusted ingress and concrete
   rendered-affordance requirements. Current evidence
   class and profile admission are different claims.

3. **Conditional does not mean unchecked.** Every mandatory
   local judgment still passes. Accepted opaque/provider
   assumptions are named, scoped and retained; not
   fabricated checker successes or missing derivations
   reclassified as trusted.

4. **Conditional induction includes completeness.** All
   original initialization, invariant, operation and
   preservation cases must be checked within declared
   semantics, including non-vacuous positive controls.
   An unsupported abstract operation cannot be omitted
   from its selected catalog to manufacture safety.

5. **Correspondence is a different profile dimension.**
   Source-issued callables do not authenticate installed
   routes, all physical writers, actual transaction
   causality or browser installation. A strong claim
   must require real bounded evidence or an admitted
   universal argument as applicable.

6. **Profiles themselves have dependencies and lifetimes.**
   Kernel/checker and normalization changes, withdrawn
   provider qualification, replaced implementation or
   assets require claim-specific requalification.
   Old unconditional reports cannot override newly
   requested policy.

7. **Stable first-blocker diagnostics.** R4's precise
   source/use-site/proof-chain explanation survives.
   Unknown downstream artifacts are consequences of
   the first failed dependency, not dozens of
   independent check failures. Priority is deterministic
   under source/map reordering where semantic order
   has not changed.

8. **Rich but private-safe diagnostics.** Structured
   expected/actual types, obligation, role, path,
   checker/profile, dependencies and proof status are
   valuable. They must not leak protected principal/
   tenant policy or be mistaken for issuer-registered
   certificates.

9. **Typed and distributed rejection differ.** A
   RequestStatus numeric comparison is malformed.
   A perfectly typed private Review predicate used
   without browser knowledge is rejected at the
   specific Choreo use site, not the rule definition.

10. **Stale dependency is not malformed upstream.**
    Request S18 with renamed status may be valid
    even while cached Claim rules for S17 cannot
    compose. The report names exactly what
    commitment changed and which dependent failed.

11. **Mechanical versus intent boundary.** The
    age 18/older-than-18 example demonstrates
    that a well-typed statement can be product-
    wrong without being ill-formed. Any formally
    proved invariant is about the admitted
    semantics, not a guessed business wish.

12. **Counterexamples preserve their quantifier.**
    A meaningful bounded model execution violating
    an invariant is not a physical trace of XTDB.
    Unsupported tools, timeouts or uninspected
    branches are not counterexamples or successful
    checks.

13. **Human-facing semantics remain reviewable.**
    Rule meaning, policy rationale, examples,
    correctness notes and counterexamples travel
    with source into the audit when possible.
    Prose is not a hidden axiom; contradictory
    prose yields an advisory finding.

14. **Review is advisory until product authority acts.**
    Human, static-heuristic or LLM reviewers may
    find suspiciously permissive guards, terminal
    transitions, policy asymmetry or missing
    disclosure. They cannot mint proof, silently
    change operations or bypass policy owners.
    Explicit adopted source changes trigger
    ordinary rechecking.

15. **Policy surface is smaller than all code.**
    R4's semantic compression includes
    correctness-relevant actor/target authority,
    disclosure, lifecycle, incorporation, protected
    state and outcome; incidental branch logic
    need not become a business rule.

16. **Escapes require real inspected evidence.**
    An observed correctness-affecting host branch
    outside the declared/admitted dependencies
    is a detected escape. Registered opaque
    implementations are explicit premises,
    not proof the host has no uninspected
    authorization or disclosure logic.

17. **Unknown is not empty.** A report derived
    only from operation summaries cannot
    assert it has discovered every Clojure
    policy dependency, raw physical writer
    or optional response. A reported zero
    unresolved detected escape count applies
    only to its named inspected subset.

18. **Coverage metrics are not a partition.**
    173 rules, 80 kernel-checked, 61
    finite-checked, 18 runtime-enforced
    and 14 opaque premises may overlap
    even in an illustrative report.
    No aggregate is a percent of
    correctness or an assurance
    gate without actual proposition
    scope and positive product oracles.

19. **Per-boundary positives protect usability.**
    HumanHelp Claim, Reassign, local
    Create, explicit remote Refresh,
    two-client and response-flow
    requirements are independent.
    Reject-all construction does
    not pass them, and a working
    proof never silently selects
    the unresolved response policy.

20. **No automatic semantic repair.**
    Changing an authorization guard,
    selecting an outcome fallback or
    inventing a business rule is
    a product-source change requiring
    explicit owner authority. Typed
    error suggestions may identify
    a possible safe repair but do
    not enact policy on their own.

## Current Gesso v1622 evidence and limitations

- `src/gesso/assurance/claim.clj` implements a
  structured vocabulary of assertion/evidence
  classes and scopes. `describe-claim`
  validates a *raw* input description;
  it does not authenticate an evidence
  reference or admit any certificate.

- `src/gesso/assurance/rule.clj` has a
  bounded, closed pure-data rule AST
  with explicit reader footprints
  and syntax restrictions; this
  can reject malformed expressions,
  but the namespace disclaims
  typechecking, evaluation,
  rule truth, runtime enforcement
  and behavioral correspondence.
  The `rule-type`, `rule-eval`
  and formal proof namespaces
  supply separate narrower layers.

- `src/gesso/assurance/diagnostic.clj`
  offers a source-rechecking,
  deterministic first-blocker
  explanation for five controlled
  original-source categories:
  induction, inductive report,
  application induction,
  observed correspondence and
  finite-model checking. Reports
  are copyable, non-admitting and
  explicitly unauthenticated for
  physical observation. The
  `diagnostic_test.clj` source
  exercises deterministic blocker
  selection, scope attribution
  and finite/physical limitations.
  These tests were inspected,
  not executed in this revision.

- `src/gesso/live/application_preflight/semantic_audit.clj`
  derives a read-only operation
  and declared-Live-effects
  summary from a caller-supplied
  freshly checked preflight report.
  It labels business-rule,
  authorization/disclosure and
  lifecycle implementation as
  *not inspected* and keeps
  =:complete-business-policy-inventory?=
  false and
  =:zero-undetected-policy-escapes-established?=
  false. Exact executor local
  construction registry data
  does not establish opaque
  model correctness or actual
  deployment and writer coverage.
  It does not maintain an
  independent business-policy
  registry.

- `src/gesso/live/application_preflight/inductive_policy.clj`
  contains an opt-in strict
  source-relative startup
  gate with required conditional
  induction and issued
  handler/plan binding checks.
  Its disabled/compatibility
  mode preserves existing
  legacy behavior; this is
  not evidence that all
  current Gesso deployments
  enforce the finished
  framework's baseline
  strong startup profile.
  Its own reports expressly
  leave independent checker
  soundness, physical writer
  coverage, router adoption,
  backend refinement and
  transaction authority open.

- `src/gesso/live/application_preflight/assurance.clj`
  describes source-owned
  application-preflight
  evidence and Biff/HTML
  escape hatches, including
  direct Biff startup,
  later handler replacement,
  noncanonical serving and
  pre-serialized HTML
  responses. It is
  explanation rather than
  a universal installed
  assurance certificate.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/application_assurance_report.clj`
  produces a fresh
  source-derived conditional
  abstract evidence report
  and keeps independent
  soundness, installed
  adoption, physical write
  coverage, XTDB universal
  refinement and
  browser correspondence
  as explicit permanent
  obligations, not
  “already certified”
  fields.

- `test/gesso/live/application_preflight/semantic_audit_test.clj`,
  `semantic_audit_integration_test.clj`
  and `test/gesso/assurance/diagnostic_test.clj`
  provide relevant
  source-level test cases
  without establishing
  all deployed policy
  inventory or proving
  end-to-end correctness.
  No new result is
  claimed for these
  definitions.

The finished design does
not force developers to
write their own TLA+,
Recife, Quint or typed
proof language. Normal
Gesso/Clojure declarations
and application assembly
supply source; admitted
checking derives evidence
and policy views when it
can. Where Clojure
implementation opacity or
external provider behavior
prevents proving a claim,
the appropriate explicit
premise or declared
unsupported strong
profile remains.

## Positive/adversarial conformance pairs

1. **Mandatory induction**:
   all source-derived cases
   are checked; a pruned
   operation/invariant
   source cannot pass
   a strong profile.
2. **Conditional opaque**:
   named qualified model
   implementation is
   allowed by its
   profile; a silent
   missing-proof-to-trust
   downgrade is rejected.
3. **Current profile**:
   an unchanged qualified
   abstract proof remains
   reusable; a changed
   checker/host or
   withdrawn provider
   qualification invalidates
   affected current use.
4. **First blocker**:
   a genuine source
   mismatch yields stable
   primary reason under
   irrelevant map
   reorder; a forged
   report or copied
   status cannot alter
   admission.
5. **Type mismatch**:
   RequestStatus equality
   can be valid; numeric
   enum ordering is
   rejected with actual
   operand types.
6. **Nominal relation**:
   correct Organization/
   Membership endpoints
   compose; mismatched
   nominal IDs do not.
7. **Refinement**:
   guarded optional
   field use is admitted;
   nil/absence read
   without proof is
   rejected.
8. **Use-site knowledge**:
   authorized role
   knows a permitted
   server decision;
   unobserved private
   field in a browser
   branch is rejected.
9. **Stale composition**:
   S18 is valid new
   source; cached
   Claim relying on
   S17 is rejected
   as stale, not a
   schema error.
10. **Intent distinction**:
    coherent =age > 18=
    is accepted absent
    a contrary rule;
    typed =email < 18=
    is malformed.
11. **Formal trace**:
    declared bad
    transition receives
    bounded typed
    counterexample;
    a test-generated
    trace is not
    physical XTDB
    provenance.
12. **Advisory review**:
    suspicious Claim
    authorization
    asymmetry is
    flagged; no reviewer
    silently adds a
    checked policy
    guard or issues
    a certificate.
13. **Closed subset escape**:
    source-derived
    exhaustive policy
    read set is
    established within
    admitted generated
    code; an uninspected
    opaque Clojure body
    cannot claim
    zero undiscovered
    policy.
14. **Observed escape**:
    a real inspected
    authoritative
    decision outside
    declared policy
    is diagnosed; an
    assertion that
    its branch is
    “just implementation”
    does not discharge
    the missing rule.
15. **Honest counting**:
    proposition and
    uninspected-scope
    counts retain
    overlap and unknown
    values; a numerical
    sum is not treated
    as completeness
    percentage.
16. **Product positive**:
    eligible Claim/
    Reassign and one
    scoped Refresh
    succeed when
    permitted; reject-all
    construction or
    ungranted remote
    adoption fails the
    independent Part VIII
    gate.
17. **Incomplete check**:
    resource exhaustion
    and unsupported
    inference produce
    explicit
    not-established
    status; a forged
    success or fabricated
    counterexample is
    rejected.
18. **Privacy of
    diagnostics**:
    authorized source
    and error path
    are informative;
    private principal/
    tenant data cannot
    leak into a
    browser-facing
    proof report.
19. **Strong physical
    profile**:
    actual installed
    handler/source and
    qualified transaction
    observation support
    their named bounded
    claims; a caller-
    supplied route/
    node/basis cannot
    authenticate a
    universal
    production theorem.
20. **Repair ownership**:
    valid owner-adopted
    rule change is
    rechecked; an
    automated suggestion
    must not rewrite
    policy or
    suppress the
    negative tests.

The oracles state subject,
selected profile, expected

result, independent evidence

boundary before test.
