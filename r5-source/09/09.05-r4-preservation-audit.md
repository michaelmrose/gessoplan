## Normative judgment and architecture retained

Preserve the preferred R5 architecture rather than reverting to the larger R4's tentative “one proof kernel handles everything” catalog. The finished system has **one executable semantic core**, a **small checker for explicitly supported proof derivations**, and **dedicated checkers for distinct judgments**, all sharing canonical semantic terms and typed bridge contracts. Ordinary HumanHelp authors express Clojure/Gesso business rules, operation contracts and application intent; the framework generates proof candidates and obligation ledgers. Authors do not hand-maintain a separate TLA+/Quint/Recife representation or a second business-rule machine.

The review strengthens the distinction between proof *derivation validity*, *complete required obligation coverage*, *original-source/projection/executable correspondence*, and *current installed model/browser authority*. Each has a different responsible checker or trusted boundary. A valid proof of a conditional formula can coexist with an incomplete app catalog, a wrong lowerer, or a currently unauthorized Request; those are not contradictory statements.

## R4 requirements preserved and made falsifiable

1. **Executable judgment families have determinate contracts.** R4's proof-bearing pipeline is made operational through R5's eight-family table: rule typing, rule evaluation, propositional derivation, guarded transition consequence, invariant preservation, distributed admissibility, projection/lowering correspondence and certificate composition. Each binds original subject and nominal context, premises, typed conclusion, evidence scope, applicability, checking rules and expected coverage. A caller's `:status` cannot select truth.
2. **Source-derived obligation coverage is an independent gate.** An elaborate proof generator may omit a branch and propose only derivations it can prove. A checker must independently derive or check required source obligations, including selected operations, reachable branches, initialization, transitions, message/resource/continuation edges, and independent promised HumanHelp capabilities for the requested claim. Validating all *volunteered* proofs does not establish this coverage.
3. **The proof kernel is not an alternate runtime.** The executable semantic core determines admitted transitions/effects. The kernel checks a closed mathematical derivation under named premises; it does not execute the Choreo, decide current XTDB guards, grant an authenticated actor or install an HTMX target. The relevant source-to-implementation refinement and runtime facts remain separate.
4. **A small kernel has an explicit trusted base.** R4 identifies primitive inference-rule semantics, checker code, parser/representation, nominal typing, and runtime interpretation assumptions. “Small and inspectable” does not establish kernel *meta-soundness*; that remains a required mathematical, mechanized or explicitly trusted premise under the selected assurance profile.
5. **The proof term uses a closed propositional grammar.** True/false, typed atoms, not/and/or/implies are meaningful only for the exact subject and complete declared variable context. Atomic rules recheck nominal reader types; propositional connectives are logical, not the rule interpreter's short-circuit evaluation system.
6. **No cross-atom refinement smuggling.** A proposition-level implication that mentions a separate `present?` atom does not typecheck an independently invalid dereference in its consequent atom. The permitted alternative is one intrinsically well-typed guarded rule atom, or a different explicitly checked refinement-aware judgment. R4's conditional future-observation/knowledge claims likewise do not assert a real browser has received the observation.
7. **Inference rules and their side conditions remain closed.** R5's seven minimal rules are retained: named premise, truth introduction, implication reflexivity, conjunction introduction/elimination, implication elimination and disjunction introduction. Each checks exact inputs, order, child indices, nominal subject and derived conclusion; no arbitrary “prove atom,” arithmetic axiom, magic false-elimination, general hypothetical discharge or application-registered trusted callback is introduced.
8. **All premises and proof paths remain visible.** Premise use does not discharge source truth—even a premise of `false` stays conditional. Acyclic unique step IDs, backward references, final exact goal, reachable-step checking, and rejection of unused declared premises/steps prevent hidden detached proofs or proof-DAG circular justification.
9. **Wrong inferred proof, missing coverage, wrong executable are different failures.** The R4 adversarial examples survive as three distinct boundaries: a bogus `:knowledge-from-global-syntax` rule fails the proof checker; an omitted not-approved branch fails independent source coverage; a valid proof for S1 attached to emitted S2 fails source/projection/plan correspondence. The kernel must not be credited for checks it cannot perform.
10. **Conditional theorem status differs from resource permissions.** Ordinary logical facts are reusable in a derivation, but a one-use Refresh grant, execution lease or physical resource right is not duplicated through conjunction introduction. Ownership and linear/affine resource effects belong to separate semantic judgments (09.06, Part V), actual issued resources to the runtime.
11. **Proof checking is not correctness of product policy.** A conditional derivation of Q under eligibility and policy premises does not establish their truth, current Organization/Membership permission, HumanHelp's independent Claim/Reassign contract or exact business intent. Unsupported arithmetic implication is an unsupported judgment, not proof that the business relationship is false.
12. **Finite/conditional/theorem/observed evidence do not collapse.** Exact finite exploration has an explicit state/transition scope, partial exploration remains incomplete, model counterexample differs from malformed proof, and a source report is not an independently authenticated test result. Abstract proof plus a green UI test is not universal physical writer correspondence.
13. **Fail-closed admission preserves usable positives.** A requested stronger certification profile cannot downgrade on unknown proof rules, timed-out checker, missing required obligation, forged certificate or stale source. A supported narrower conditional/opaque profile can still admit genuinely valid construction with explicitly retained premises.
14. **Independent checker development and mutation.** Deliberately buggy elaborator/proof source, wrong dependency/subject, missing required branch, corrupted lowerer, unsupported inference, stale checker version and forged report are injected at their *first real enforcing boundary*. Each negative has a genuinely admissible positive. Rejection of everything is not an acceptable kernel implementation.

Section 09.05 deliberately does **not** duplicate 09.06's guarded-transition relation, resource calculus or conditional-induction proof rules; 09.07's finite exploration/independent checker soundness; or 09.09–09.12's certificate issuance, dependency composition and deployed source-to-physical correspondence. It establishes the governing *executable judgments and initial propositional calculus* which those sections extend.

## Actual Gesso v1622 correspondence — meaningful precursors, bounded claims

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/proposition.clj` declares a closed, subject-bound typed proposition tree with `[:true]`, `[:false]`, `[:atom rule]`, `[:not p]`, `[:and ...]`, `[:or ...]`, and `[:implies p q]`. It rechecks the rule AST and nominal reader environment, rejects unused variables and invalid source shapes, and returns an **unadmitted copyable description**. It expressly does not prove or issue authority.
- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/kernel.clj` is a bounded source-rechecking propositional derivation checker (v1231). It implements the exact seven closed rules, checks original subject, nominal variables, earlier-step dependencies, exact final goal, and unused premises/steps. It expressly lacks arithmetic/SMT solving, hypothetical-context discharge, arbitrary truth axiom and unbounded proof search. It returns descriptive checked-derivation **data**, not an admitted current certificate or transaction authority.
- `test/gesso/assurance/proof/kernel_test.clj` contains positive conditional modus ponens, premise-free truth/reflexivity, conjunction/disjunction rule tests and adverse unused premise, forward/self reference, altered source/unknown rule, typed context, wrong goal and copied-report cases. These are inspected test **definitions**; no execution is claimed.
- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/obligation.clj` generates initial-state and per-invariant guarded replacement obligations from **source** and leaves explicit premises open. It does not itself prove the implications, prove initial-state existence or cover every application writer. This is a concrete precursor to source-based coverage checking; whole-application requirement completeness still requires a separate independent source/product inventory.
- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/certificate.clj` admits restricted propositional, guarded-transition and conditional-induction kinds under locally checked dependency premises. Its source documentation explicitly excludes universal induction over hidden writers, real XTDB/CLJS/browser correspondence, original real initial states and independent checker meta-soundness. A JVM-local opaque handle is not a cryptographic or cross-process proof credential.
- The separate guarded-transition and induction checker machinery corresponds to the 09.06 onward extension of R5's judgment table. Their conditional abstract conclusions must not be advertised as a proof that every production operation, external XTDB process or physical browser resource refines the model.

The verified sources show the first propositional layer is more than a speculative sketch, but do **not** establish that the present Gesso implementation already realizes the full composed certificate/proof-kernel/physical refinement target. Source inspection and tests written in a repo are not the same as tests run.

## Independent positive and adversarial test obligations

1. Valid typed atom and a premise-free truth or implication-reflexivity derivation pass within the stated formal calculus; runtime data authority remains absent.
2. Valid conditional derivation P, P⇒Q entails Q while recording *both* named open premises and the exact source subject.
3. A named `false` premise may support a conditional derivation but cannot be reported as unconditional proof of the current world or inconsistency of production state.
4. A proposition with optional read in a second atom cannot borrow the refinement established by a logically earlier separate presence atom; a single well-typed guarded atom succeeds.
5. Wrong antecedent, modified conclusion, omitted step, unknown inference tag or invalid step index is rejected by the actual kernel.
6. Self-reference, forward reference, circular step chain and unused premise/step are rejected, while a valid finite DAG passes.
7. Same-shaped source report, forged `:valid?` or copied certificate identity cannot enter a trust-requiring certified consumer without current source admission.
8. A changed proof checker/certificate version or nominal subject invalidates appropriate downstream reuse even if the claim label and displayed source name are unchanged.
9. Bogus knowledge proof from mere global-syntax mention fails; actual scoped authoritative observation with a properly admitted transition can establish its separate conditional fact.
10. One branch missing from volunteered obligations blocks a required closed Choreo certificate; complete source-derived coverage passes.
11. HumanHelp Reassign removed from both offered model and proof registry still fails its independent positive capability requirement, not merely a structural proof check.
12. Valid S1 theorem with S2 executable carrying a copied S1 hash fails the *correspondence* checker; it cannot be used as a proof-kernel soundness failure.
13. Valid abstract guarded result with actual unauthorized actor or failed XTDB current guard is denied by the trusted runtime, despite abstract derivation success.
14. A checker resource budget/timeout/unsupported arithmetic goal cannot return “proved false” or “proved true” by default; supported bounded case still passes.
15. A proof about a logical resource-lease *fact* cannot duplicate the actual one-use permission; the effect gate rejects repeated consumption.
16. A real qualified dedicated finite checker may establish its exact enumerated model claim without pretending that omitted states or deployed writers were explored.
17. Human-readable rationale for a business policy does not become a theorem; a suspicious permission discrepancy requires explicit owner confirmation and source rule change.
18. An actual valid Gesso/HumanHelp construction and supported narrow/conditional profile remain usable despite stronger proof profile being unavailable; negative gates never imply reject-all service.
