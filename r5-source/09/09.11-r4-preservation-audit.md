## Governing decision

Preserve R5's stronger distinction between an accepted *conditional*
guarantee and a genuinely discharged assumption. Recover every
critical R4 premise-source, substitution, runtime and liveness invariant,
and make the proof-ledger/evidence trust boundaries falsifiable.
Avoid treating static proofs as source for current transactions,
browser rights or deployed-provider assurances. Gesso users still
declare ordinary rules and application construction; the framework
generates proof obligations and manages discharge where possible,
not a parallel human-written theorem language.

## R4 preservation and design decisions

1. **Premises are propositions, not strings.** Exact scope includes
   subject, proposition, typed parameters, principal/tenant where
   applicable, temporal basis, origin and enforcing implementation
   or provider boundary. Identical names do not unify facts across
   roles, commands or actors.

2. **Use-relative footprint.** A dependency with multiple exports
   can carry distinct premises for each guarantee. The accepted
   derivation of claim G inherits exactly the assumptions it
   uses, neither every unrelated assumption nor an incomplete
   flat set. Alternate proof paths retain separate lineage.

3. **Discharge must be issuer-checked.** A matching declaration,
   copied report, alleged checker result or `:discharged?` flag
   cannot replace a checked proof, authenticated observation
   or qualified runtime enforcement fact of the appropriate
   category.

4. **Typed context/substitution matters.** E must establish P
   exactly or through an accepted entailment/bridge, with
   type-correct capture-avoiding substitution, current subject,
   scope and trust. Broad logical entailment from a different
   principal, basis or policy version is not admissible.

5. **Evidence carries its own premises.** If E proves P under Q,
   replacing P makes the consumer depend on Q. Deleting both
   names would silently strengthen a conditional theorem into
   an unconditional one. The proof ledger keeps P and E
   reconstructible after genuine discharge.

6. **Justification must be founded.** Two certificates cannot
   prove each other by reciprocal assumption discharge.
   Valid recursive protocols and checked fixed-point/induction
   rules are different proof categories.

7. **Not every fact is reusable.** A pure lemma can be reused
   under equal context; a one-use Refresh grant, model outcome,
   current principal authorization or retired resource lease
   cannot be duplicated or spent twice.

8. **Current kernel scope is narrow.** R5's illustrative
   hypothetical-context substitution is an intentional
   future composition judgment. Do not claim that the
   present propositional checker implements general
   assumption introduction/discharge or higher-order
   typed derivations.

9. **Model policy versus operation authority.** A source-level
   Organization implementation conformance premise is
   separate from current Request/Membership/Organization
   values and atomic Claim incorporation. One current
   transaction guard supplies only its instance-bound
   evidence under the actual writer contract.

10. **Historical versus current evidence.** Authenticated
    historical C42 incorporation is not the same fact
    as a newly observed Request card or later Reassign.
    Old true command outcomes do not make current
    authorization true.

11. **Liveness needs trace/environment promises.** Network,
    fairness, retention, provider progress and the user's
    eventual Refresh remain explicit assumptions.
    A successful timeout configuration, accepted
    retry route, or SSE event does not prove them.

12. **Remote view rights are scoped.** A one-use Refresh
    acquisition right is not generated by notification
    debt or a local creator-visible card. The required
    browser read, target installation and adoption
    coverage have independent physical premises.

13. **Knowledge and disclosure are separate.** Distributed
    role-local fact availability, provenance, private
    data disclosure and actual mutation rights are
    independent judgments; a schema-typed payload
    cannot establish current issuer authority.

14. **Weaker profile does not rescue stronger failure.**
    A named open-premise policy is legitimate, but
    missing mandatory proofs, unsupported syntax,
    absent writers, trusted-code escapes or detected
    source errors cannot be excused by silently
    upgrading assertions to checked evidence.

15. **Contradiction/vacuity are honest limitations.**
    Within the closed supported logic detect
    inconsistent premises and report when an
    entailment is vacuous. Outside it arbitrary
    opaque application policy consistency is
    undecidable. Independent positive Create/
    Claim controls and inhabited initialization
    prevent reject-all “safety proofs.”

16. **Qualified external semantics are not global.**
    XTDB guarded writer, HTMX physical lifecycle,
    browser source, authenticated session, compiler
    and providers have precise versions, config,
    trusted origin and covered behavior. A signature,
    conformance test or observed partial trace is
    not a proof of every provider execution.

17. **Applicability can expire.** Provider upgrades,
    changed Organization policy callable, withdrawn
    authority, runtime resource retirement or
    observed qualification failure invalidate
    affected deployment/instance applicability.
    Unchanged abstract proofs need not themselves
    become false.

18. **First responsible boundary matters.**
    A source checker cannot claim it detected
    missing physical provider authority unless it
    actually possesses authentic correlated
    evidence. Diagnostics classify obligations
    and provide scoped repair/qualification.

## Actual Gesso v1622 implementation evidence

- `src/gesso/assurance/claim.clj` defines evidence classes
  including `:kernel-checked`, `:runtime-enforced`,
  `:trusted`, `:application-asserted`,
  `:exact-finite-checked` and `:unknown`.
  Its returned descriptions are **raw assertions about
  evidence classes**, never checker-issued admission.

- `src/gesso/assurance/assumption.clj` accepts exact
  subject-bound premise structures, including scope,
  proposition, typed-ish parameter/context data,
  origin, dependencies and implementation boundary.
  `describe-assumption` always reports
  `:discharge-state :open`. `match-premise!`
  verifies structural identity *only*, and returns
  an explicitly open/unadmitted result. It
  does not prove the matched proposition.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/kernel.clj` implements a
  small bounded source-checked propositional
  derivation system with `:premise`, truth and
  implication reflexivity, conjunction and
  disjunction introduction, conjunction elimination
  and implication elimination. Every proof step
  tracks its inherited premise IDs; the final
  report retains `:open-premises` and
  `:assumption-discharge :not-performed`.
  The kernel explicitly lacks arbitrary
  hypothetical-context discharge or atomic truth
  axioms.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/propositional_semantics.clj`
  implements a bounded full Boolean valuation
  oracle over opaque atoms, distinguishing
  `:counterexample`, `:entails`,
  `:vacuously-entails` and `:not-checked`.
  This validates logical abstraction
  under the selected finite scope, not truth of
  the hidden typed atoms or deployed behavior.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/certificate.clj`
  checks the original proposition/transition/
  induction proof source and exact open-premise/
  obligation sets before issuing limited
  in-process handles; descriptions remain
  copyable data with
  `:assumption-discharge :not-performed`.
  The source-relative issuer does not grant
  principal authorization, actual model writer
  correspondence or general discharge power.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/compose.clj`
  rechecks original two-operation source with
  conditional relation premise propagation,
  rather than accepting copied elaborator
  evidence. It creates no universal
  two-writer physical correspondence theorem.

- `test/gesso/assurance/assumption_test.clj`
  exercises changed actor/scope/semantic
  context/origin and structural matching while
  ensuring every description stays open.
  `test/gesso/assurance/proof/certificate_test.clj`
  exercises misreported open-premise sets and
  source/certificate forgery. These test
  *sources* were inspected; no execution is
  claimed for this revision.

No current module above establishes a
general typed proof-substitution engine,
a universal opaque Clojure policy
implementation theorem, current physical
browser/XTDB assumption discharge,
or a distributed fairness guarantee.
These remain the finished-system goals
or explicitly qualified trusted premises.

## Paired positive/adversarial conformance corpus

1. **Used P1 only:** A dependent claim selects
   G1 under P1. Negative: another export's
   unrelated P2 pollutes or conceals P1.
2. **Used premises retained:** A valid
   propositional derivation returns the
   exact used IDs. Negative: caller deletes
   P1 from a success report and claims
   unconditional G.
3. **Conditional replacement:** Genuine
   checked E establishes P under Q.
   Negative: replacement drops Q.
4. **Unconditional replacement:** Genuine
   checked E establishes P without open
   premises and removes P from the final
   open set. Negative: discharge loses
   the required original P→E ledger.
5. **Scope distinction:** A premise about
   Alice/R17/O1/B1 remains separate
   from Bob/R18/O2/B2. Negative:
   same-label matching is accepted.
6. **No premise forgery:** An actual
   admitted source proof may be used.
   Negative: `:discharged? true`
   or matching EDN description is trusted.
7. **Acyclic derivation:** A proper checked
   source proof is accepted. Negative:
   two certificates cite each other
   as sole premise evidence.
8. **Opaque Organization contract:** An
   unchanged real callable can retain
   a qualified conformance result.
   Negative: changed closure/Var gets
   old evidence merely because public
   interface name matches.
9. **Runtime Claim:** Real current
   principal/Request/Membership facts
   and actual guarded transaction
   support instance-specific evidence.
   Negative: stale pre-read or swapped
   principal is accepted as current.
10. **Historical attribution:** C42's
    trusted command-specific outcome is
    recovered accurately. Negative:
    current card is used to attribute
    an old incorporation.
11. **One Refresh grant:** A remote user
    permits one eligible acquisition.
    Negative: a second read or wrong-scope
    whole-board adoption consumes it.
12. **Fair progress:** Given named
    qualified provider/fairness/user
    premises, a declared liveness
    claim remains appropriately
    conditional. Negative: timeout
    alone is used as fairness proof.
13. **Positive model:** Inhabited state
    and real Create/Claim path satisfy
    selected nonvacuity/product gates.
    Negative: inconsistent-premise
    entailment or reject-all admission
    counts as successful product.
14. **Provider envelope:** Actual tested
    supported XTDB/HTMX adapter version
    retains its qualified premise.
    Negative: upgraded unqualified
    provider inherits all old assumptions.
15. **Disclosure:** A server-issued
    permitted public decision is
    qualified for the browser.
    Negative: schema-typed private
    field is treated as authorized
    disclosure.
16. **Correct weaker class:** A user-
    requested conditional profile reports
    all open obligations. Negative:
    failure of stronger mandatory
    checker silently becomes trusted.
17. **Retired rights:** Static theorem
    remains reusable where scoped
    while old resource rights expire.
    Negative: an old cached certificate
    permits a new browser effect.
18. **Impactful qualification failure:**
    Observed provider violation marks
    affected deployment claims
    unestablished. Negative: old
    successful report overrides it.

These are *expected test oracles* for
finished Gesso, not tests executed
for this editorial revision. Some
negative cases require genuine
physical observation or deployed
source provenance rather than
source-level validation alone.

## Editorial disposition

09.11 preserves R5's clear assumption
and discharge architecture while
recovering R4 exact source premise
semantics, actual runtime liveness
and authorization limits, conditional
proof transparency, and non-vacuous
positive acceptance. It states how a
finished framework should conduct
checked logical substitution without
asserting that the v1622 kernel does
so today.

**Nonclaims:** No current
implementation-wide theorem, runtime
principal/XTDB/HTMX certificate,
provider universal semantics, executed
test result or whole-plan acceptance
is implied by this audit.
