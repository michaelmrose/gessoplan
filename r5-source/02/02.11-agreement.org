** Agreement
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: req-semantic-agreement
:END:

Agreement is stronger than two actors having values with the same shape.

Agreement means the protocol can justify that the actors share the relevant
semantic value or fact under the declared relation, scope, and frontier.

This is a judgment about those actors' relevant observations or values at the
specified points. It does not automatically mean that either actor knows the
other actor received the value, that both know that both know it, or that they
continue to share the same current value after later changes. Protocols needing
acknowledgement or stronger knowledge about receipt must declare that additional
obligation.

If A communicates X to B:

The message contract justifies A's send, B admits the matching receive, and
the sent and received values are related by the contract's declared encoding
and semantic interpretation. Under those premises, the protocol may establish
=:communicated= agreement on X at those two events. It does not establish
acknowledgement, current-state equality after later changes, or domain truth
beyond what that message contract justifies.

Agreement can arise through multiple provenance paths.

At minimum:

- =:communicated=;
- =:authoritative=;
- =:derived=;
- =:asserted=.

*** Communicated agreement

Communication can establish agreement on the communicated value.

It does not imply agreement on every fact either actor knows.

Message contracts therefore matter.

Closed payloads make the agreement boundary explicit.

*** Authoritative agreement

Two actors may independently observe the same authoritative fact and obtain
justified agreement without receiving one identical network packet.

For example:

| Actor | Physical read path | Required semantic support |
|-------+--------------------+---------------------------|
| A | Web node 1 | An admitted observation supporting scoped proposition F |
| B | Web node 3 | An admitted observation supporting the same proposition F under the required compatibility relation |

The agreement judgment uses that compatibility evidence; matching host or
packet identities are unnecessary.

The hosts differ.

The packets differ.

The required agreement is on the scoped proposition being used, not necessarily
on an identical packet, exact read timestamp, or complete database snapshot.
The relation must establish that the observations support that same proposition
under compatible subject, temporal, projection, and authority contexts. Equal
progression tokens alone do not establish this; neither do equal status values
at unrelated times.

For example, two observations that a Request is open can support agreement on a
specified state/version or valid-time proposition when the authority contract
justifies the relation. They do not prove that the Request never became claimed
and reopened between those observations. Such a history claim needs its own
evidence. A generic basis advance is not evidence that every fact was unchanged.

*** Derived agreement

Agreement may propagate through a declared deterministic derivation when the
inputs and derivation justify doing so.

The system must not infer derived agreement merely because two actors happen to
run source code with the same function name.

The derivation relation must be part of the admitted semantic contract. Where
its realization is opaque, the conclusion retains the exact deterministic and
implementation assumptions used. A nondeterministic computation may support
agreement on an explicitly shared result, but independent invocations need not
produce that same result.

*** Asserted agreement

Application code may explicitly assert agreement that Gesso cannot derive.

Such assertions are named, scoped premises under the admission policy. They
remain visible in diagnostics and proof reports, including the particular
actors, proposition, relation, and frontier to which they apply.

They cannot silently suppress a mandatory failed correspondence check or be
introduced by an untrusted participant choosing an assertion id. An asserted
agreement does not become mechanically derived agreement because the two
displayed values happen to match.

*** Shape equality is not agreement

Two values can validate against the same Malli schema without being agreed
semantic facts.

For example:

#+begin_src clojure
{:request/status :open}
#+end_src

in two browser contexts does not establish:

- same Request identity;
- same authoritative basis;
- same provenance;
- same valid time;
- same semantic observation;
- same authority.

Malli answers shape questions.

Agreement answers semantic relationship questions.

*** Agreement is scoped

Actors need not agree on all application state.

Agreement may be scoped to:

- a Request;
- a fact;
- a command;
- an operation input;
- a branch discriminator;
- an authoritative frontier;
- another explicitly modeled semantic object.

Privacy and irrelevance are legitimate reasons not to establish agreement.
