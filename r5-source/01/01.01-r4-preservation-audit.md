# R5 Part I, section 01.01 — preservation audit

Baseline: `main` (canonical R4) versus `r5` (R5 candidate).
Reviewed scope: document provenance, revision scope, part organization,
cross-cutting links, and the Purpose section through its natural end.

## Preserved or strengthened

- R4's design-vs-implementation distinction is preserved. R5 now states
  candidacy relative to R4 instead of misidentifying itself as R4.
- The twelve-part structure and one authoritative home per requirement survive;
  the ten editorial fragments are deliberately *not* new normative documents.
- R4's demand for early descriptive rejection of recognizable contradictions
  is strengthened in R5: a supported checker MUST reject when adequate
  information is available, with an owning-boundary diagnostic.
- R4's distinction between coherent but domain-wrong policies and malformed
  application construction remains explicit.
- R5 adds full-language Clojure, opaque implementation contracts,
  mechanically derived obligations, qualified backend assumptions, and
  production-path evidence. None was weakened in this revision.
- All existing anchor IDs and cross-cutting requirement links in 01.01 are
  retained unchanged.

## Editorial correction made

- The former R5 draft's `r4-draft`, R4-only opening, and R3-as-immediate-
  predecessor metadata conflicted with the branch's actual candidacy.
- The revised section identifies an R5 draft, names R4 as its direct
  preservation reference, and defines a review discipline for any omitted
  R4 obligation. Supersession remains pending full cross-document review.
- Source fragments and mechanically assembled published parts are now
  explicitly distinguished.

## Open publication issue, not a semantic deletion

R4's initial Org included a custom LaTeX cover sheet and image reference.
The current R5 Part I omits that export block. Whether to retain the
traditional cover in the generated R5 PDF is a publication/build decision
that must be resolved during release assembly. It does not justify
silently changing the semantic contract or claiming PDF parity.

## Status

01.01's normative content reviewed against its R4 counterpart; editorial
corrections incorporated. The full Part I and cross-document semantic
preservation audit are **not** complete. Sections 01.02–01.10 remain under
review. The accepted R4 plan remains available on `main`.
