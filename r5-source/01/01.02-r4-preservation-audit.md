Baseline: R4 `main`. Working source: R5.1 section 01.02. This review
concerns full-language construction, dependency direction, the Claim
illustration, and normative interpretation.

## Preserved / strengthened
- R4's separation of domain truth, choreography, projection, execution,
  physical realization, and admission evidence is retained.
- Admitted certificates and sidecars cannot grant principal authority,
  establish present facts, choose branches, or become runtime capabilities.
- Stable command identity survives retransmission; compatible replay checks
  the retained binding and current disclosure before new-command guards.
- New incorporation requires current atomic dependency protection; a
  candidate pre-read does not establish transaction-current eligibility.
- Known incorporation, authoritative rejection, and participant uncertainty
  remain distinct, including after response or publication loss.
- R4's projection-does-not-create-authority obligation is explicitly retained
  as a separate judgment. No `:kernel-checked` claim is made without evidence.
- Normative language and illustration-versus-executable distinctions survive.
