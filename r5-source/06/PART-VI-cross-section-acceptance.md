The resulting contract is satisfiable *under qualified implementation
and environment premises*: it does not demand universal atomicity

### One-Refresh and subscription-gap compatibility judgment

The joined explicit-Refresh contract is coherent with initial
acquisition, reconnect and backend/subscription repair because
it distinguishes three independently authorized acts:

1. An initial/bootstrap acquisition with its explicitly declared
   first-load and interest/fence coverage contract.
2. Bob's qualifying user grant G1 for precisely its specified
   physical A1 read(s) and adoption stage; O1/O2 debt and local
   presentation can survive A1 failure or incomplete coverage.
3. A separately authorized recovery acquisition, *only* when the
   selected product/profile has that permission; otherwise the
   system retains visible gap/advisory debt and promises only
   the appropriately weaker liveness/currentness guarantee.

These are not three reads attributed to one user action. A current
user-granted read may satisfy multiple obligations via actual
provider progression and completed physical adoption, but a
queued A2 cannot draw permission from an already consumed G1.
The initial baseline-to-interest gap, including a router that
never learned of interest before a relevant write, remains
explicitly handled through fence/replay/qualified reacquisition
or a declared weaker continuity promise.

### Recovery and fault consistency judgment

Revocation of obsolete private execution and physical actuation
rights is independent of best-effort host cancellation. Surviving
C42/C77 unknown historical outcomes must have a genuine owner,
original operation binding and declared retention/failure-domain
guarantee where durability is promised. Recovery does not use
a fresh command ID to convert delivery uncertainty into
a second domain write. It is permitted to stop safely in a
truthful unknown/blocked/manual disposition if its named
premises are unavailable; it cannot claim an automatic
committed/rejected result or resume an incompatible old plan.

A current authorized entity observation and command-specific
historical readback have different temporal and causal questions;
a later Reassign does not retroactively reject an earlier Claim.
The actual installed browser target and qualified model result
are separately matched to their physical/backend evidence.

## Cross-part obligations and acceptance boundaries

- **Parts I–II:** Full Clojure construction, admitted effects,
  owner-relative source/knowledge/progression, trusted model
  and business-rule authority, and the separation between
  semantics, evidence and actual execution remain controlling.
  Current physical and source facts never come from a
  diagnostic or copied permit map.
- **Part III:** The canonical choreography and role-projection
  subject remains the executable semantic source.
  Actual browser role-local receive does not prove
  global send/delivery/terminal history without
  independent correspondence witnesses.
- **Part IV:** Request/RequestAssignment transaction guards,
  stable command binding, trusted historical outcomes,
  durable work and authoritative provider progression
  remain the distinct backend evidence obligations.
- **Part V:** Provisional and continuity rights are
  owner/target-relative. Current authoritative
  adopted scopes can supersede them; delayed
  local restores cannot undo newer canonical
  installation or grant external domain authority.
- **Part VII:** Must close the installed application
  inventory, dynamic route/FX/worker/writer
  paths, rendered affordance bindings,
  browser source handlers and *actual served
  and loaded* assets. Unverified arbitrary
  Clojure or third-party effects retain explicit
  premises or unsupported strong profiles.
- **Part VIII:** Must independently prove useful
  positive HumanHelp paths and adversarial
  controls, including Create, Claim, genuine
  manager Reassign, lifecycle actions,
  creator-local display, one-Refresh
  remote adoption, search/options/scroll,
  and an explicitly selected response
  interaction. The still-unselected
  response-flow meaning is a product
  decision and not a passing capability.
- **Parts IX–X:** Formal subject, proof
  kernel, conditional certificate,
  exact finite check, actual backend/XTDB,
  browser-host source/physical refinement
  and witness extraction remain separately
  required with source and assumption
  provenance. A role-local trace or
  synthetic test is not universal proof.
- **Parts XI–XII:** Actual deployment,
  compatibility, service-worker/asset
  lifecycle, failure recovery, multi-node
  invalidation/interest fences, capacity
  limits and completion criteria
  retain the same application/Choreo
  semantics in single-node and
  multi-node realizations. A particular
  routing algorithm is not imposed as
  the semantic contract.

These future application, implementation, proof and
deployment obligations **do not invalidate** the
finished-system Part VI candidate. Their discharge
is required before stronger implementation,
release or end-to-end product claims can be made.
Any later concrete cross-part contradiction may
reopen this editorial acceptance through
a focused explicit revision.
