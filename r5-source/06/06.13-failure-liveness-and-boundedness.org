** Failure, liveness, and convergence

Safety, progress, and recovery are separate claims. The runtime must remain
truthful when it cannot make progress. A blocked acquisition, unknown command,
or unsupported continuation can be a correct safe state, provided its required
handling path is present and its presentation does not imply success.

*** Failures preserve authoritative commit truth

The application distinguishes at least:

| Failure class | Required semantic treatment |
|---------------+-----------------------------|
| Local input/admission rejection before send | Report the local condition without claiming an authoritative model rejection. |
| Authoritative operation rejection | Carry the permitted typed outcome under Part IV's binding and disclosure rules. |
| Transport loss after possible send | Preserve command uncertainty unless authoritative evidence resolves it. |
| Post-commit advisory/delivery failure | Preserve incorporation and the required delivery/recovery debt. |
| Acquisition failure | Preserve unmet observation requirements; do not invent presence or absence. |
| Installation failure | Preserve the distinction between acquired evidence and unestablished adoption. |
| Stale/incompatible continuation | Retire its rights and enter the admitted compatibility/recovery path. |
| Physical cleanup failure | Record resource debt without restoring retired semantic rights. |
| Runtime invariant failure | Contain affected owners and preserve evidence/uncertainty for diagnosis and recovery. |

A UI timeout means that a particular local waiting policy expired. Its meaning is
not automatically the operation's authoritative result. The timer contract names
its owner and whether it governs provisional display, transport waiting, command
reconciliation, or some other work.

*** Safety targets

Under the named semantic, implementation, and environmental premises, the browser
realization must establish:

- no protected physical effect proceeds without a current applicable issuance
  record and permit where required;
- a retired owner cannot regain private rights through a late or duplicate event;
- identity and provenance are preserved across normalization, effect issue, and
  completion rather than silently erased or substituted;
- adopted state reflects actual admitted installation within the claimed scope;
- progression and absence judgments use their qualified provider contracts;
- provisional presentation and local continuity cannot manufacture authoritative
  knowledge or revive superseded authoritative content;
- unknown commit is not converted into rejection or success by local timing;
- server authority never depends on obedience by the browser runtime.

These are conditional claims. An unverified renderer, host profile, progression
provider, or evidence extractor remains a named premise in claims that depend on
it. Passing tests must not silently remove that premise.

*** Progress needs explicit fairness and availability assumptions

A graph containing a path to a terminal state does not prove that an execution
will take that path. A liveness claim names its scheduling, delivery, availability,
permission, resource-budget, and user-action assumptions.

For example, eventual adoption of relevant board state can be claimed under
conditions such as:

1. the relevant authoritative state and query/context eventually stabilize;
2. the progression provider can supply a sufficiently strong authorized observation;
3. the required acquisition and installation permissions are eventually granted;
4. the scheduler, transport, and selected browser profile eventually complete
   enabled work within the promised failure model;
5. failed attempts retain debt and the declared retry/recovery policy continues;
6. no unresolved higher-priority overlay or incompatible ownership condition
   permanently blocks the required installation.

The conclusion is scoped convergence of the promised participant state, not
processing of every intermediate change or agreement with undisclosed data.
An explicit-refresh product may provide eventual convergence conditional on a
later Refresh action rather than unconditional background convergence.

Continuous changes, permanent denial of access, exhausted recovery budgets, or
an unavailable backend can prevent convergence without violating the safety
contract. The runtime exposes the resulting debt or blocked state. It must not
label the view current merely to make a progress metric succeed.

*** Boundedness is specified independently of single flight

The assembly declares budgets and overflow behavior for active cycles, successor
slots, unsatisfied obligations, retained command recovery, deduplication history,
physical remnants, and diagnostic buffers. These are different resources.

Checked coalescing or stronger reacquisition can compress advisory obligations
when the progression/coverage contract permits it. Otherwise the system needs
backpressure, a declared resynchronization state, or a visible capacity failure.
Dropping arbitrary obligations because the queue is full is not a valid bound.

A browser with one logical request per coordinator can still leak DOM captures,
failed-abort transports, or stale callback records indefinitely. Physical registry
and retention checks therefore accompany the logical scheduling bound.
