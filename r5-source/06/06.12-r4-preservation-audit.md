# R5.1 Part VI — 06.12 R4 preservation and Gesso correspondence

**R4 baseline:** Gessoplan main Part VI, Authoritative absence;
Presence and absence are both authoritative projection outcomes;
Authoritative absence may legitimately remove local representation;
Absence supersedes obsolete provisional presence; and Absence is
scoped to a projection. R4's Bob/R17 open-board versus completed-board
and provisional R30/C99 worked examples are especially important.
**Preferred starting architecture:** Gessoplan r5 Part VI.
**Reviewed source:** r5-source/06/06.12-authoritative-absence-and-progression.org.
**Disposition:** one substantive sectional review only, not independent
Part VI editorial acceptance or a certified browser implementation.

## R4 requirements preserved

- Authoritative presence, qualified absence, not yet acquired and
  failed/unavailable acquisition are distinct observable states.
  Neither empty DOM nor missing HTML provides model absence.
- R17's exclusion from Bob's authorized open-Requests projection
  is not global deletion: a separate completed-Requests
  projection may still contain R17. Scope, completeness,
  consistency and actor/tenant context must accompany
  the negative membership claim.
- A complete covered observation or a narrower trusted
  exclusion can establish legitimate negative membership.
  A partial page, filtered subset, redaction, denied read
  or failed request cannot establish the same absence.
- Admitted negative knowledge can permit safe physical
  removal, whose completion and per-target adoption
  remain independent of observation acquisition.
  Missing target and generic swap callback are not
  universal evidence of the intended removal postcondition.
- A later authorized absence can supersede stale
  provisional presence and expire rollback rights;
  older callbacks cannot resurrect removed content.
  The unresolved C99 Create's historical outcome
  remains separately tracked, rather than being
  inferred rejected from omission alone.
- Local search/filter toggles over an already justified
  cohort are presentation operations, not new
  authoritative membership facts.
- Absence is scoped to provider progression and
  can be replaced by a genuinely newer admitted
  presence; it is not an irreversible browser tombstone.

The revision retains R5's leaner architecture and exact
permit/observation/effect boundaries rather than copying
R4's long example-only helper functions into a second
semantic interpreter. It does not revive the obsolete
single-field helper model as the actual HumanHelp
RequestAssignment authority.

## Focused improvements in 06.12

1. Distinguished six source and physical states:
   present, authoritative absence, not acquired,
   failed/unknown, undisclosed, and physically hidden.
   Query/existence/field absence stay separate.
2. Qualified the negative coverage proof by applied
   query, authenticated principal/Organization,
   completeness, pagination, field/projection,
   temporal consistency, authoritative basis and
   provider progression/retention.
3. Permitted narrower explicit exclusion without
   forcing full-board transport; required the
   actual trusted source and nondisclosing result
   where existence is protected.
4. Rebuilt the R17 Qopen/B105 example as a staged
   semantic-to-physical removal with exact owner,
   permit, target instance, optional checked
   no-op and actual physical postcondition.
5. Separated local =show-terminal?= and live
   text filtering from applied server queries;
   stale local re-showing cannot restore
   invalidated content or protected old actors.
6. Joined provisional R30/E99 and unknown C99
   to temporal/possibly pending incorporation.
   Observed omission need not mean that a
   transaction which may later commit was
   authoritatively rejected.
7. Required provider-justified scope-relative
   progression, preserved incomparable evidence
   and rejected imaginary global snapshot claims
   from mixed whole-board R/newer card S.
8. Preserved acquired versus adopted negative
   knowledge and one-Refresh budget accounting
   when physical removal or count/OOB update fails.
9. Distinguished access/display revocation from
   evidence of domain deletion; browser-side
   privacy behavior never replaces current
   trusted server authorization.
10. Added ten independently positive/adversarial
    conformance histories for coverage, exclusion,
    model existence, physical removal, old
    rollback, C99 uncertainty, local filters,
    partial installation, one Refresh and privacy.

## Inspected Gesso v1622 implementation — source only

The provided Gesso application-assurance snapshot at
1b5e10e71c00e7784b019dee7dc1198a96fd03f6
was inspected in these actual source/test areas:

- src/gesso/live/browser/adapter.cljc maintains
  :authoritative frontier by logical scope and
  requires exact prior/new basis endpoints in an
  optional structurally valid :advances witness
  before permitting a different-basis swap.
  The first basis and a repeated identical basis
  may install under this local structural rule.
- On current fragment :htmx/after-swap, the adapter
  associates the candidate scope/basis into its
  :authoritative map and emits an
  :authoritative/installed observer effect.
  That transition does not encode a separate
  first-class entity/member :absent outcome,
  explicit negative query coverage, checked
  DOM removal postcondition, or physical OOB
  target completion for R17.
- src/gesso/live/progression.cljc supplies opaque
  bases and an orderless set of minimum refresh
  requirements, with set union and exact-duplicate
  collapse. Its :advances witness checks *shape
  and endpoints* and explicitly leaves truth of
  the progression relationship to the
  authority-specific provider boundary.
- src/gesso/live/browser/observation.cljc derives
  a source-relative local adapter observation
  from actual adapter transition/effects and
  classifies :authoritative/installed as a
  browser view installation. It does not assert
  a trusted XTDB transaction or universal
  physical DOM postcondition.
- Source-inspected tests include
  first-authoritative-basis-installs-without-ordering-assumption-test,
  distinct-authoritative-basis-requires-exact-progression-witness-test,
  malformed-authoritative-candidate-fails-closed-test
  in test/gesso/live/browser/adapter_test.cljc;
  real HTMX/SSE progression/coalescing cases in
  test/gesso/live/browser/chromium_integration.clj.
  These tests do not by themselves establish
  qualified negative membership or complete
  current-target DOM removal.

The current implementation provides real scoped basis,
generation and swap-correlation groundwork. It does
not yet establish, in the inspected source, a
general authoritative-absence membership protocol
or fully verified physical removal and adoption
for arbitrary model projections. This is an
implementation correspondence obligation, not
a reason to remove absence from the finished design.
All statements here are source-inspection findings,
not results from newly executed tests.
