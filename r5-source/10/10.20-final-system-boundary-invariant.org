** Formal correspondence and system-boundary invariant

#+begin_quote
A claim applies to production only through its stated representation, checking,
or refinement relation and the continued applicability of its premises. The
executable semantic core, proof kernel, dedicated checkers, external formal
checker, physical gateways, and deployed entrypoints have distinct obligations.
Source-derived coverage, observed execution, trusted backend behavior, and
universal theorems are not interchangeable evidence.

Full host-language implementations remain available through explicit contracts.
Finite checking remains finite unless a justified abstraction transfers it.
Physical guards and writes enforce current authority; certificates do not.
Known commits remain commits despite delivery loss, while participants preserve
unknown outcomes until permitted authoritative evidence resolves them.

Every relevant backend or implementation change triggers the appropriate
qualification and correspondence checks. Multi-node placement changes hosts,
not authority, command meaning, resource ownership, or HumanHelp's adoption
policy. The root report preserves the evidence class, scope, assumptions,
applicable identities, enforcing boundary, and unresolved obligations of each
claim.
#+end_quote
