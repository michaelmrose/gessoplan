## Editorial decision

Preserve R4's reproducible statement/input/result package and R5's selective semantic dependency lifecycle. Expand into a precise three-way distinction: (1) theorem validity under unchanged formal meaning and premises; (2) artifact/checker and source-to-implementation correspondence; (3) applicability of that theorem to the *currently installed* model, server, backend and browser. Meaningful reuse is allowed across independently irrelevant changes, but a matching fingerprint, copyable report, host-supplied identity or old in-process handle is not proof that the current application enforces the same obligations.

The finished design should let ordinary Gesso authors retain a single source of application meaning while framework maintainers and formal tools produce inspectable proof and diagnostic artifacts. Claims must reflect *actual observed* verification, including unsupported, incomplete, stale, timed-out and not-run statuses. There is no silent conversion of a finite check into a theorem, an abstract certificate into production authority, or a successful proof import into a right to read/Claim/Refresh/retry.

## R4/R5 preservation and strengthened decisions

1. **Complete evidence package.** Exact theorem or model property, semantics, source dependencies, proof/checker/toolchain versions, relevant axioms/options, finite domains and abstraction, actual result, production boundary, open obligations and reproduction instructions remain required as in R4.

2. **Source remains canonical.** The original admitted Gesso model, rule, operation and Choreo construction determine the proposition. Generated formal files, compiler output, cached proof reports and test fixtures are derivatives, not independently authoritative application models.

3. **Evidence category is claim-specific.** Kernel-checked, exact-finite, bounded, assembly-verified, runtime-enforced, tested, application-asserted, trusted and unknown descriptions do not form a numerical trust-ranking that can be upgraded by aggregating them.

4. **Observed results differ from intentions.** A recorded passing checker result names the actual execution; an unrun external prover, timeout, unsupported language, incomplete exploration or missing evidence is never written as completed proof.

5. **Exact finite domains remain visible.** Complete state and transition closure, operation inventory, bound, equality abstraction, fairness/deadlock semantics, exploration budget and counterexample scope must accompany a finite result. A bounded prefix is not exhaustive evidence.

6. **Semantics versus physical applicability.** A theorem can remain logically valid for its original source while a changed server route, browser asset, backend version or direct writer makes its production applicability stale. A failed real implementation does not by itself refute the abstract theorem.

7. **Source digest is structural identity.** Versioned canonical encoding may ignore proven irrelevant formatting/metadata while retaining ordered semantic values and nominal subject distinctions. A digest is not a universal semantic equivalence proof or proof of opaque Clojure implementation fidelity.

8. **Meaning-changing changes invalidate precisely.** Altered guards, typed reader contracts, operation/initializer/invariant catalogs, semantic primitives, proof inference rules, normalizer/translator semantics or used premises require dependent proof checks or qualified compatibility bridges.

9. **Reproducibility has three tiers.** Abstract semantic rechecking, exact generated artifact/loaded-byte identity, and current physical implementation/observation require distinct methods and ownership. Same build hash does not authenticate XTDB transactions; a valid proof does not imply current installed asset adoption.

10. **Toolchain and axioms matter.** The actual external kernel, axioms, proof term/importer, solver/model options, target versions, compiler and codec behavior enter the trusted evidence statement. A successful CLI process exit is not, by itself, a trusted proof.

11. **Nondeterministic tools are qualified.** A proof-producing solver may emit different but independently valid proof objects, while bitwise asset identity requires exact verified emitted/served bytes when that is the claim. Seeds, schedules and relevant flags are recorded rather than implied.

12. **Portable evidence is not a copied local handle.** A JVM-local registry-backed certificate cannot be serialized into remotely authoritative EDN. A supported durable proof import needs rechecking, authenticated attestation or a defined persistence/revocation lifecycle.

13. **Authenticity and truth are separate.** A digital signature can authenticate a signer and covered bytes under its threat model; it neither makes a false theorem true nor proves the signer observed a real backend effect.

14. **Open premises survive proof.** Original-source-required obligations, used assumptions and producer/consumer premise substitutions remain in the dependency ledger. A trusted external assumption may be accepted by profile but never mechanically discharged merely by composition.

15. **Revocation targets applicability.** Current backend qualification, external service or model-policy assumptions may expire/be contradicted. Dependent claim use is withdrawn or requalified; a previously correct abstract theorem and a historically incorporated C42 are not rewritten retroactively.

16. **Original-source dependency closure is essential.** A complete caller-supplied proof graph for a smaller catalog is insufficient. Source-recomputed initializers, operations, invariants and original open obligations are recorded independently of the submitted DAG.

17. **Nonsemantic edits may permit reuse.** A checked semantic identity can survive harmless formatting where the canonicalization contract intentionally discards it. A purely renamed physical callable can still reopen deployment linkage even with unchanged abstract interface.

18. **Current runtime enforcement is separate.** Principal/Membership, protected read permission, durable command replay, transaction-time guards and effect-time resource ownership must be checked at their actual boundaries. Proof reuse cannot grant a one-Refresh user action, one-shot physical attempt or retired browser effect lease.

19. **Failure is typed and fail-closed.** Mandatory strong-profile evidence absent at new admission causes rejection/unestablished status, not an automatic downgrade. Already admitted compatible immutable evidence can remain usable under its explicit current lifecycle when a prover is temporarily unavailable.

20. **Rolling release identity matters.** Different hosts/browser caches may use different callable, assembly, compiled asset, backend and credential generations. An application-wide claim needs an actual installed binding or checked compatibility across those generations, not merely matching source labels.

21. **Physical writer universe is independent.** A clean source/registered-writer report does not prove hidden XTDB admin, migration, job or external credentials cannot change protected state. Such a change reopens relevant physical correspondence even while a source theorem remains valid.

22. **A redacted explanation is not the checked input.** Trusted checkers may process original protected artifacts; copyable diagnostics disclose only authorized redacted source spans, statuses and correlation handles, without changing theorem meaning or exposing Request/command/credential data.

23. **Counterexamples are classified honestly.** Model counterexample, contradictory external premise, observed physical mismatch, tool failure and incomplete evidence have different owners and invalidation effects. A later diagnostic cannot retroactively prevent an unauthorized write.

24. **No vacuous application completion.** Legitimate Claim, Unclaim, Reassign and one user-permitted remote Refresh remain separate product positives. An all-rejecting assurance gate does not make HumanHelp complete by suppressing the operations whose correctness matters.

## Actual Gesso v1622 source correspondence and nonclaims

- `src/gesso/assurance/subject.clj` defines a versioned, bounded, deterministic structural encoder for pure EDN-like semantic interfaces, preserving meaningful ordering and type distinctions. It explicitly says a fingerprint is **structural** identity, not denotational equivalence, a theorem, executable implementation provenance or proof that a callable runs in the deployed application. It rejects unsupported values and normalization collisions within its scope.

- `src/gesso/assurance/claim.clj` defines a closed vocabulary of evidence-class *descriptions* and their stated bounds. Creating a =:kernel-checked= description or evidence reference cannot prove its proposition; the source expressly labels it raw unadmitted data.

- `src/gesso/assurance/assumption.clj` names unadmitted subject-/context-bound assumptions, origins, dependencies and implementation boundaries. This declaration layer issues only open descriptions, not discharged judgments; a copied matching assertion is not evidence of model policy or external provider truth.

- `src/gesso/assurance/admission.clj` issues restricted process-local structural handles only after actual local identity/assumption checks, using an identity-based private `WeakHashMap` registry. It does not prove business policy or provide durable/transferable checker evidence. It explicitly trusts the JVM against arbitrary reflection/Var replacement within its threat model.

- `src/gesso/assurance/dependency.clj` checks exact declared graph nodes, edges and versioned fingerprints against live subject-admission handles and demands a fresh current graph when asserting continued applicability. It checks *structural* closure relative to the supplied trusted current declaration, not source-complete business transitions or installed physical writer effects.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/certificate.clj` supports three distinct locally checked proof kinds—propositional, guarded transition and conditional induction—with genuine original-source checks and exact declared graph/current-premise requirements. `require-current-certificate!` reruns the source check and dependency graph check rather than trusting a copied report; its JVM-local opaque handle is neither an externally signed theorem nor runtime authorization. It explicitly leaves independent meta-soundness, all physical writer coverage, real initializer and XTDB/CLJS correspondence open.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/application_dependency.clj` re-derives the full *declared original source* induction and application evidence/obligation graph rather than trusting a submitted subset. Its process-local source-closure handle does not claim all installed handlers, operations outside the catalog, direct XTDB writes, production admission or universal physical correspondence.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/operation_interface_provenance.clj` regenerates an inspectable deterministic source-relative manifest of checked original operations, invariants, graph nodes/edges and still-open assumptions. Its own namespace description calls this a freely copyable, nonauthoritative diagnostic; matching its digest is not certification or physical write permission.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/application_assurance_report.clj` builds a current conditional, copyable application evidence summary and deliberately preserves independent kernel metatheory, actual installed application adoption, complete physical XTDB writer universe, universal source-to-physical refinement and browser/HTMX/SSE correspondence as open obligations. A positive local source report does not discharge those dependencies.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/conditional_single_node_refinement_evidence.clj` joins bounded *caller-supplied* single-node history and command/progression reports. It explicitly refuses to authenticate transaction origin, node, all writers, backend commit or deployed runtime. Its redacted result is diagnostic and cannot settle/retry/authorize a command.

- Relevant existing tests include `test/gesso/assurance/subject_test.clj`, `dependency_test.clj`, `assumption_test.clj`, `test/gesso/assurance/proof/certificate_test.clj`, `application_dependency_mutation_test.clj` and `operation_interface_provenance_mutation_test.clj`. Their code exists in v1622; this editorial revision did not execute these tests or claim they prove full external persistence/revocation.

**Implementation nonclaims:** No inspected v1622 component implements a universal portable cross-process proof import/attestation and revocation system, guarantees deployment-wide actual runtime version adoption, proves independent propositional kernel metatheory, dynamically enforces every backend writer/credential, or certifies HumanHelp's Part VIII product gates. The normative design remains forward-looking rather than reducing its requirements to current experimental modules.

## Eighteen paired positive/adversarial acceptance controls

1. **Canonical source:** Identical supported semantic interface rechecks to the same claim identity; a changed actual Claim guard cannot retain the old proof applicability.
2. **Meaningful ordering:** An explicitly irrelevant formatter/source location edit may reuse the original semantic theorem; altered proof-step operands/ordered operations may not reuse its semantic identity.
3. **External proof:** A genuine verified theorem with exact admitted axioms and checker version passes; a copied =:proved= flag or checker exit code alone does not.
4. **Finite evidence:** Fully explored declared finite graph receives the narrow exact-finite class; budget-exhausted search is incomplete, never universal no-counterexample proof.
5. **Process-local issuance:** Current original source and dependency graph revalidate the checked handle; copied EDN, forged handle or stale DAG cannot mint an issuer-controlled certificate.
6. **Premise inheritance:** Provider guarantee genuinely entails the exact consumer premise and retains all open conditions; a matching assumption name or success-shaped report does not discharge it.
7. **Revocation:** An expired/contradicted backend contract invalidates dependent physical applicability; a mathematically unchanged original proof is not falsely refuted merely by that deployment change.
8. **Checker upgrade:** A changed normalization/proof inference contract forces affected proof rechecking; old artifact fingerprint equality cannot hide a changed checker semantics.
9. **Installed host:** Actual current callable/route/worker/asset is bound by qualified host evidence; copied source-commit ID or old handler registry snapshot does not prove the live deployment.
10. **Bounded gateway:** One captured actual XTDB submit/result/readback supports its precise observation; matching ledger from another writer does not establish this attempt's causality.
11. **Current principal:** A real active helper's Membership and Request policy permits Claim; stale credentials or historical proof cannot authorize a revoked actor.
12. **Historical C42:** Compatible H2 recovery returns actual prior C42 result without duplicate incorporation; rerunning a proof does not grant a fresh logical command.
13. **Browser effect time:** Current owner installs its permitted target; retired A callback cannot mutate B-owned DOM using a copied proof or unchanged DOM ID.
14. **Rolling release:** Two deployed versions may qualify a common semantic theorem under a justified compatibility bridge; physically different unverified asset/callable versions do not get universal applicability from same source fingerprint.
15. **Privacy:** Authorized redacted diagnostics identify first blocker and relevant source span; raw Request/command/SQL/client credential data is not released in a copyable evidence report.
16. **Actual refutation:** A genuine contradictory source/model result blocks its dependent theorem; a tool outage is recorded unavailable rather than invented as a counterexample.
17. **External writer:** A new migration/job/direct XTDB path gets a valid permitted transition or real restriction; a missing inventory entry cannot prove writer absence.
18. **Positive application:** Eligible Claim/Unclaim/Reassign and user-scoped Refresh remain available and independently witnessed; a checker that rejects all operations cannot claim product completion.

Each case predeclares the actual source, meaning-changing mutation or other adversary, selected evidence class, producer/consumer, original and installed generation, first checker/enforcing boundary, valid positive and qualified physical oracle where needed. A source rejection does not prove a bypassing writer was stopped. A copied diagnostic, an incomplete explorer and a stale production identity cannot be promoted into an operation certificate.

## Editorial outcome and next owner

10.12 restores R4's explicit reproducibility contract and retains R5's selective semantic dependency and assumption lifecycle. It adds independently checkable semantic/artifact/physical replay, proof-import trust boundaries, measured result provenance, targeted stale/revoked applicability, rolling-deployment qualification and honest fail-closed mandatory proof profiles. The completed section is a timeless specification of *what Gesso evidence must mean*, not a claim that its full proof distribution and revocation system exists today.

**Nonclaims:** No new JVM, XTDB, Chromium, Node, formal prover run, cross-process attestation system, independent deployment audit, whole-system certification or HumanHelp product acceptance was executed or established by this editorial work.
