# R5.1 Part X — 10.17 Durable Rendezvous and Outbox R4 Preservation Audit

**Baseline:** R4 `main`, Part X multi-node durable rendezvous, distributed invalidation, worked Claim/Refresh and post-commit recovery. **Preferred:** R5.1 10.17 original statement of durable outbox, scoped delivery, registration fence and authorized acquisition. **Gesso inspected:** `application-assurance` `1b5e10e71c00e7784b019dee7dc1198a96fd03f6` (v1622). **Review status:** sectionally reviewed as a normative finished-system target; no deployment certification or runtime tests conducted.

## Editorial decision

Preserve R5's clearer staged authority, publication, subscription and adoption contracts, restore R4's durable Request path and invalidation-versus-message distinction, and make publication intent, dispatcher uncertainty, subscriber gaps, router failover and safe overdelivery falsifiable. R4's typing-message contrast belongs to 10.19 and its two-host HumanHelp scenario to 10.18; they are not deleted. No mandatory broker, Rust router, hierarchical graph or per-recipient receipt log is introduced.

## Implementation correspondence and nonclaims

- `src/gesso/live/invalidation.clj` expands declared primary changes, without inspecting XTDB commits or guaranteeing all physical writer paths publish.
- `src/gesso/live/source.clj` explicitly owns an **in-process** indexed hot source with scope coalescing; it is not durable cross-host routing or replay.
- `src/gesso/live/flow.clj` builds local per-client flows; it does not own distributed subscription fencing.
- `src/gesso/live/transport/sse.clj` owns SSE stream formatting and send lifecycle, not browser receipt.
- `src/gesso/model/command_resolution.clj` models a durable command record alongside a supplied plan under assumed backend atomicity and trusted disclosure; it does not supply outbox dispatch.
- `src/gesso/live/consistency/xtdb.clj` owns explicit basis/progression helpers, not XTDB transaction-log publication or distributed invalidation.
- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/xtdb_gateway_atomic_submitted_readback.clj` and `xtdb_gateway_command_outcome_bridge.clj` are bounded process-local observations; neither authenticates a cross-node dispatcher, all writers, browser notice nor causal production proof.

No complete installed outbox, router interest replay, multi-process physical failure suite or end-to-end event causality was established by source inspection. All physical challenges below are *prospective*.
