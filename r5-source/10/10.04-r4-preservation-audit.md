## Editorial synthesis

Preserve R5's crisp mechanism- and evidence-specific organization and the strong R4 demonstration that a checked theorem must *return to real executable boundaries*. Neither the preferred text's six-field correspondence record nor R4's illustrative Clojure functions may silently serve as an authenticated production receipt. The normative section restores R4's bidirectional provenance and falsifiable two-helper race/counterexample cases, while clarifying the current public HumanHelp model boundaries and the difference between proof status, physical authority and product completion.

The source deliberately uses a *schematic data fixture*, not an invented runnable public Gesso API. R4's model used a Request map with fields such as `:request/helper-id` to illustrate the formal shape; the accepted Part VIII/IX pilot instead targets public Request plus RequestAssignment and real Membership, Organization, and Location policy. The rewrite keeps the R4 proof-transfer obligation without falsely claiming that illustrative fields exist in the production model.

## R4 preservation and strengthened cross-part decisions

1. **Single production semantics.** Generated formal systems and production-issued plans derive from checked original Gesso source; formally verified F alongside unrelated executed G is prohibited as an end-to-end proof. Different representations are permitted only through a directional checked or clearly trusted correspondence relation.

2. **Forward and reverse traceability.** The report maps original semantics to formal/plan artifacts and maps formal obligations, assumptions and counterexamples back to source, deployment binding and current physical evidence. Reverse traceability improves repairability but does not prove either simulation direction by itself.

3. **Original obligation completeness.** Proof applications carry exact initializers, invariants, operations, guards, source dependencies and selected profile assumptions. A complete submitted proof DAG for a smaller catalog cannot certify the larger original app.

4. **Admission soundness is a production-acceptance implication.** If a supported current-source artifact is admitted, it must meet the appropriate formal judgment under its accepted premises. Acceptance of a few valid inputs, or agreement on only rejected cases, does not prove this universal direction.

5. **Completeness is different from soundness.** Required positive Create/Claim/Unclaim/Reassign and other HumanHelp user capabilities cannot disappear behind an all-rejecting assembler while a narrow safety statement stays vacuously true.

6. **Execution transfer is separately quantified.** Concrete relevant traces from actually installed I/E must relate in the stated direction to admitted S traces through a specified abstraction, observation alphabet, initial-state and failure/recovery semantics. A proof of source admission does not verify that an HTTP server executes the issued handler or a browser loads the emitted bytes.

7. **Progress requires more than safety.** Fairness, scheduling, delivery, durable retention, authorized acquisition, user action and termination/divergence must be named when the theorem quantifies over eventual completion. Unbounded silent stuttering can preserve safety while violating liveness.

8. **R4 HumanHelp Claim witness retained, corrected to actual public model shape.** Claim's Request/RequestAssignment one-primary invariants, current Membership/Organization/Location authority, model-owned policy, command identity, stable outcome, and production writer belong to the same semantic transition; a browser actor field is never the authenticated principal. Schematic R4 functions remain examples, not preinstalled APIs.

9. **Atomic formal actions require atomic physical enforcement.** A current-open check, current helper eligibility, Request/Organization association, command binding, ownership transition and durable command-result write cannot be independently pre-read then unconditionally written and still qualify as one protected atomic action. Qualified XTDB or another proven atomic mechanism may implement the relation.

10. **Two independent false-correspondence shapes.** Production may add unmodeled behavior such as an opaque priority lock, or may promote a browser-supplied helper ID to authority. The former threatens execution trace/refinement and positive policy completeness; the latter violates the protected actor/permission boundary. A matching name or model theorem repairs neither.

11. **Submission, observation and causality remain distinct.** Canonical lowering match, one controlled gateway call, process-local result, pinned basis/domain/ledger readback, real backend outcome, transaction-internal guard and complete physical writer coverage are separately qualified facts. Co-observation is not proof of production node identity, durable causation or universal refinement.

12. **Real command replay is historical.** C42 is a logical command, H1/H2 are attempts. A thrown post-submit H2 or later current Request card is not evidence that the original command was rejected or newly incorporated. Same command binding plus protected durable record governs authorized reconciliation.

13. **Selected currentness controls reuse.** Source meaning, proof/checker rule versions, issued plan, actual implementation closure/Var, installed route, served/loaded asset, provider and resource lifecycle have distinct identities and dependencies. Immutable proofs may be reused where valid; physical effects require fresh current authorization and qualified installed applicability.

14. **Exact external checker dependency.** Proof-producing elaboration remains untrusted when the selected mechanism requires a checker; a translator/compiler may be trusted only as an explicit named premise or backed by proof. Unsupported or timed-out stronger checking cannot silently downgrade to a green certificate.

15. **No fabricated attestation requirement.** An in-process issuance witness can establish local origin under a trusted JVM, not remote cryptographic identity. An actual captured XTDB call may be bounded evidence without cryptographic signing, but copied reports and caller-selected nodes are not authenticated physical occurrences.

16. **Counterexamples have two statuses.** The unguarded candidate Claim model may genuinely allow two incorporates and refute an abstract invariant; that trace does not refute a correctly guarded model or demonstrate the deployed writer executed the race. A correct formal model with an unguarded real writer represents a correspondence gap, not a formal counterexample.

17. **Source-owned repair loop.** Counterexamples and unsupported results map back through verified lowering identity to original model rules/operations, physical bindings and responsible evidence. A human or assistant may propose a fix, but editing only generated formal files to force a green checker or weakening policy without owner approval does not repair source/production.

18. **Framework and HumanHelp responsibilities stay separated.** Framework owns source interpretations, checkers, generated formal/plan artifacts and semantics of proof transfer. The public HumanHelp model owns business authority. Proof can neither decide whether the product owner's policy is desirable nor silently grant a private read/Refresh.

19. **Multi-node does not change semantics.** Single- and multi-node use the same Claim and acquisition meanings; router placement, interest/frontier registration, durable invalidation, replay, node health/failover and actual physical writer boundaries add evidence requirements rather than changing authorization.

20. **Independent product acceptance survives.** A correct Claim theorem and one production test do not prove the fourteen Part VIII completion gates, selective creator-local Create, remote advisory with one authorized Refresh, other lifecycle actions, two-client continuity or unselected response interaction.

## Gesso v1622 factual source evidence and limits

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/certificate.clj` issues source-relative, restricted, process-local proof handles for propositional, guarded-transition and conditional-induction kinds; declared dependency and premise checks are meaningful, but no universal runtime or source-to-physical theorem is issued.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/application_dependency.clj` re-derives original declared abstract catalog and premise closure. Its documentation explicitly states that the live handle is not proof of physical writer completeness, installed handler adoption, XTDB refinement or real runtime authority.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/application_assurance_report.clj` regenerates a copyable, nonauthoritative conditional report and keeps kernel metatheory, installed adoption, actual physical writer completeness, universal XTDB refinement and browser/HTMX/SSE correspondence as open obligations. A readable report is not issuer-controlled proof authority.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/installed_route_physical_correlation.clj` invokes one source-checked handler currently recorded in a Biff system slot, with a controlled request and captured XTDB adapter call site, and checks one correlated local result. Its explicit limitations include actual HTTP-server adoption, later replacement, caller/map honesty, backend receipt and prestate authenticity, hidden writers and universal refinement. It is a controlled privileged diagnostic, not transparent production middleware.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/xtdb_gateway_atomic_submitted_readback.clj` binds one restricted local plan and immutable submission/return to single-basis pinned readback. The captured node and declaration are caller-supplied; it does not establish command authorization, physical transaction causality, every writer, a real serving route, retry permission or browser settlement. Post-submission uncertainty is not automatically rejection.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/xtdb_gateway_command_outcome_bridge.clj` relates one submitted physical result and pinned canonical command record to a disclosure-gated reconciliation view. It expressly disclaims authenticated Ring request identity, production node/plan validity, universally causal XTDB execution and settled browser response.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/transaction_outcome_progression_correspondence.clj` classifies bounded physical outcome, command-resolution and progression evidence. It does not authenticate submitted inputs, transaction prestate, deployment, complete writer set or source-to-physical universal transition semantics.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/physical_writer_inventory.clj` obtains host-observed write-path declarations from a fresh enumerator, but its own comment states that enumeration is observational, not an exhaustive capability sandbox or proof there are no raw/external bypass writers.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/physical_xtdb_transition_provenance.clj` can produce an identity-registered process-local witness for an intentionally controlled test collector invocation; it does not authenticate the supplied node as production or establish a backend transaction receipt, protected business authority or real response/DOM adoption. That collector **writes** when called and is not an authorization or observational read-only source.

These are meaningful precursors for the future strategy. The accepted normative design is broader. Neither their individual test files nor source-level comments establish that all future theorem/physical correspondence gates have already passed. No runtime tests were run for this editorial revision.

## Predeclared positive/adversarial acceptance pairs

1. **Source derivation:** one original Claim source produces coherent formal and production representations; a separate hand-edited model F cannot certify unrelated production G.
2. **Source obligation closure:** all original operations, invariants and initializers are present; omission of difficult Unclaim under a valid smaller DAG is rejected.
3. **Admission soundness:** each admitted supported subject has the claimed checked judgment; a malformed admitted input refutes it, while reject-all fails separate positive completeness.
4. **Production trace:** selected installed handler maps actual relevant behaviors into the stated admitted abstract trace; matching final maps with hidden unauthorized steps cannot establish the same universal relation.
5. **Current actor:** trusted helper Membership and Request/Organization policy grants an eligible Claim; browser actor ID and stale Membership do not.
6. **Atomicity:** concurrent two-helper Claims preserve one active primary in the true guarded model writer; stale pre-read/unconditional writes fail the physical obligation.
7. **Command record:** real C42 original incorporation and legitimate historical replay are reconciled; later Request state or thrown H2 is not fabricated rejection/second write.
8. **Gateway observation:** owned immutable local transaction and process-local return/readback support that bounded observation; a copied receipt or caller-supplied node cannot be promoted to production causality.
9. **Source/host generations:** unaffected abstract proof can be reused; changed installed Var, route or browser assets invalidate dependent physical correspondence.
10. **Opaque provider:** permitted conditional implementation contract retains its exact assumptions; unqualified arbitrary Clojure predicate cannot become checked proof through a matching label.
11. **Actual counterexample:** defective unguarded source action yields abstract race trace; a correctly guarded formal action is not blamed for an unguarded real writer.
12. **Concretization:** abstraction permits one trace with unknown physical attainability; report describes that scope rather than claiming an observed XTDB event.
13. **Repair:** owner-approved source or verified translator repair regenerates formal/plan and is rechecked; only weakening generated invariant is not admitted as source repair.
14. **Privacy:** authorized reviewer sees source span and redacted failure; copied report cannot disclose tenant data or serve as a write capability.
15. **Conditional liveness:** proven fair recovery under declared availability/user-action and retention premises is labeled conditional; timeout and safety-only stuttering cannot claim eventual completion.

Each case fixes its exact original source, profile, precondition, first checking/enforcing boundary, independent positive, adversarial mutation and relevant effect oracle before exercise. Physical cases must distinguish prevented effect, detected post hoc and unknown/unobserved outcome. A positive abstract check never claims to have tested an unexercised deployed bypass.
