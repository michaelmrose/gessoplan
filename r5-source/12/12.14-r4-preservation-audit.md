## Editorial finding

R4's extra eight gates were not optional quality
recommendations: they close specific ways that
a superficially green application can be unsafe
or incomplete. R5 retains those distinctions
and improves their semantic ownership. The
revision gives each gate one visible positive,
nearby falsifying case, first responsible
admission or physical boundary and honest
evidence limit, without declaring all
candidate maps, proof reports, generated
registries or router notices authoritative.

The most important restored R4 constraints are:
representation is not admission; the complete
product is not the exposed registry;
authoritative incorporation is not receipt
or client knowledge; notice is not read/adoption
permission; checked evidence must remain
within its source, proof and installed scope;
hidden correctness-relevant policy is
not mechanically proved; formal assumptions
must be mapped to *real* production obligations;
and advisory human/AI policy review
cannot issue mechanical proof or change
original business intent.

The current design additionally separates
the C42 historical command identity from
same-ID physical attempts and fresh C43,
initial/manual Refresh from asynchronous
notices, all-writer/transaction prestate
from a convenient XTDB pre-read, and
S0/S1 abstract kernel soundness from
S2–S4 actual checked/typed/installed
production transfer. The full HumanHelp
Response interaction remains an
independently unselected product
decision; it is not invented for
this audit or eliminated from the
original denominator.

R4's Gate N explicitly disallows
making mechanical coverage look
complete merely because correctness-
relevant Clojure lives outside the
checker. The actual v1622 semantic-audit
module correctly reports portions
of model policy as *not inspected*.
A checked closed source subset can
be useful without pretending that
all host callbacks or external
database writers were found.
The revised Gate O requires a
literal source/formal assumption
to enforcing transaction/reader/
browser/host obligation record;
a green finite check is not
a substitute for that transfer.
Gate P preserves both safety and
design freedom: developers express
complex domain policy in Clojure
and choose business requirements;
Gesso generates and checks the
formal representations.

## Actual Gesso v1622 implementation correspondence

The following are bounded, *inspected* source
mechanisms. Their names and source comments
are not reports of executed runtime tests.

- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/certificate.clj`
  checks three restricted proof kinds using
  fresh current source and dependency
  material, issuing an opaque JVM-local
  handle. The namespace explicitly rejects
  same-shaped reports or hashes as proof,
  but leaves JVM trust, independent
  checker meta-soundness, actual protected
  effects and missing physical writers
  outside its guarantee. Relevant to
  Gates I and M.
- `src/gesso/model/command_resolution.clj`
  constructs a same-plan canonical command
  record and compatible binding replay,
  requiring trusted original actor,
  domain guards and disclosure supplied by
  the application. Missing record/uncertain
  transport does not prove rejection;
  real XTDB persistence and complex
  production authorization remain open.
  Relevant to I, K and L.
- `src/gesso/live/progression.cljc`
  keeps provider bases opaque and
  refresh requirements as orderless sets,
  accepting only the *shape* of a
  progression witness. It explicitly
  does not establish that the witness
  is truthful; current provider
  authority and acquisition remain
  external. Relevant to I, L and O.
- `src/gesso/live/browser/adapter.cljc`
  implements pure event/abstract-effect
  sequencing, guarded browser generations
  and abstract Live debt, forbidding
  browser realization of authoritative
  model operations. It does not
  establish Bob's product-selected
  one-use Refresh authorization or
  real OOB/DOM installation solely
  by a state-machine transition.
  Relevant to K, L and O.
- `src/gesso/assurance/report.clj`
  rechecks a bounded original-source
  proof-linked executor and can
  include caller-reported test
  observations. The report explicitly
  has no issuing, authenticated
  test-runner, production deployment
  or runtime-authority power.
  Relevant to I/M.
- `src/gesso/live/application_preflight/semantic_audit.clj`
  derives a limited operation summary
  from a freshly checked preflight
  report, explicitly marking model
  predicates, source rules and
  atomic guards as *not inspected*.
  It does not know all host Clojure
  policy, all physical writers
  or the existence of hidden policy.
  Relevant to J/N/P.
- `src/gesso/assurance/proof/xtdb_conditional_guard_admission.clj`
  recognizes restricted ASSERT/PUT
  and field-count guard plan *shapes*.
  Even a complete-looking guard
  depends on selected XTDB semantics,
  actual transaction-internal prestate,
  nested/other document fields,
  real writer coverage and
  unchanged submitted vectors.
  Relevant to M/O.

No new Gesso JVM, XTDB, browser,
multi-node or external proof test
ran. A bounded checked source
module is a useful prerequisite,
not a claim of running
framework-wide enforcement.
