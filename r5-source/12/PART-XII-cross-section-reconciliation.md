## Review method and what this decision establishes

This cross-section review checks the *assembled* Part XII rather than counting sixteen independently accepted subsections as an automatic integrated proof. It combines the original R4 structure and final principle, all sixteen separately documented R4 preservation/Gesso-source audits, the accepted Parts VI–XI source contracts, the revised eighteen stages, five kinds of dependency, Gates A–P, the single C42 worked path, the stopped release and final invariants.

R5 is preferred where its construction is more coherent; R4 continues to supply obligations, adversarial histories and permissible engineering choices. A timeless normative design may specify future functionality that v1622 does not yet implement. This review therefore distinguishes documentary preservation, internal consistency, actual inspected source mechanisms, runtime/test evidence, independent mathematical proof and production acceptance. No number of passed editorial checks authenticates an installed Biff handler, XTDB transaction-internal prestate, complete HumanHelp, all physical writers or cross-host fault tests.

The integrated candidate must keep *one original source and requirement owner* while avoiding one universal “proved/ready” state. Named operation, proof, host, browser, observation, authorization and completion subjects are separately qualified. A valid abstract result can coexist with a missing physical realization; a useful scoped operational slice can coexist with an unfinished product; a valid C42 history can survive failure of the server that previously handled it.

## Integrated adversarial histories assessed across sections

1. **Real Claim versus paper Claim.** Public Request and separate RequestAssignment must admit an eligible current helper. A checked toy scalar Request owner or disabled button cannot satisfy the original multi-record product positive. Relevant Stages 1–8/12–14, Gates B/C/D/G/J/O.
2. **Lost C42 response after incorporation.** A correct atomic command/assignment/outbox history may coexist with a browser status of commit-unknown. The response and old host may disappear, but C42-specific authorized historical resolution—not new C43 or a newer claimed card—owns attribution. Relevant Stages 5–6/8–9/11–12/18 and Gates D/K/L.
3. **O1/O2 during one Refresh.** The selected ordinary HumanHelp grant has one logical scoped acquisition and a default budget of one physical read, no automatic successor. A provider-qualified existing result may cover both obligations after complete installation; otherwise O2 remains owed. A new explicit user grant or independently admitted recovery entitlement is required for an additional physical acquisition. Relevant Stage 9/12/18, Gates L/K/O and Part VI/VIII.
4. **Committed outbox before host/router failure.** A durable notice intent survives A's death under the selected strong profile; the replacement may conservatively redeliver duplicates. Stale interest filters, late registration or missing coverage must be fenced/replayed/detected or allow protected reacquisition. Neither “sent” nor an empty filter proves user receipt or adopted freshness. Relevant Stages 9/18, Gates K/L/O.
5. **Physical browser installation after owner retirement.** Pure adapter approval and response parsing do not grant an old callback rights to mutate a successor DOM instance. Actual HTMX/OOB completion, current target ownership and independent shared-resource rights matter. Relevant Stage 8/9, Gates E/G/H/L.
6. **Valid abstract proof with changed physical writer.** An unaffected seven-rule/guarded proof may remain true, while the actual XTDB adapter, frame, additional writer or emitted JS needs fresh S2–S4/installed qualification. A copied certificate or previous Chromium result cannot authorize the successor. Relevant Stages 3/6/10–11/13/16–17, Gates I/M/N/O.
7. **P23 artifact offered for P24 activation.** The stopped-candidate example deliberately has real local successes but stale browser bytes, a missing trusted provider, incomplete product inventory and unselected Response. Stop at the earliest legitimate owner; a source-negative gate cannot pretend to have tested the later dynamic path. Repair original owners and requalify installation/activation separately. Relevant Stages 4/10–12/14–15 and Gates A–P.
8. **Current business-policy change with failed user rights.** A Membership revocation or Organization policy change invalidates an applicable future protected operation/read. Local UI continuity and historical command records remain separate; old effects cannot resurrect. Relevant Stages 1–2/5–8/11–12, Gates C/D/F/K/P.
9. **Small independent theorem versus product acceptance.** A genuine S0/S1 external result needs actual theorem/axiom/tool identity; S2/S3/S4 and complete HumanHelp product positives remain separately unestablished. An independent narrow theorem is still useful rather than failed because the product Response contract is open. Relevant Stages 3/12–13/16–17, Gates J/M/O.
10. **Two-host prototype versus production qualification.** A bounded host-switch prototype can reveal lost command/interest windows. It does not prove complete load/fault, replay retention, registered interests, current disclosure or user-independent convergence. Relevant Stage 18, Stage 11 and Gates G/L/O.

These are *editorially reconciled specifications and future acceptance oracles*, not executed physical fault tests, model-checker traces, a verified command history, complete HumanHelp or a proof of Gesso.
