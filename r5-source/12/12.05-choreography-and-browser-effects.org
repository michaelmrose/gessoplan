*** Stage 7 -- Portable choreography, distributed admissibility, and projection

Construct the global and local semantics around public operation contracts.
Admitted projections preserve the declared global/local relation for the supported
subset and generate the canonical executable plan.

Deliver:

- explicit roles, actors, principals, hosts, authorities, and placement;
- closed message, state, and operation boundaries with runtime value validation;
- knowledge from declared inputs, communication, authoritative observations,
  deterministic derivation, and explicit premises;
- provenance, authority, path-refinement, and scoped-agreement judgments at use;
- behavior-sensitive branch discrimination, terminal states, and required failure
  or recovery successors;
- projection and lowering subject bindings, format versions, and correspondence;
- JVM/Node/Chromium conformance for the actual supported portable value subset.

Acceptance rejects branches without justified discriminators, invented payload
knowledge, premature output use, authority escalation, and unsupported constructs
presented as checked. A named opaque operation remains usable through its admitted
interface; projection does not pretend to translate arbitrary JVM code into a
browser.

For the same admitted plan, initial semantic state, and event stream, the portable
semantics agree within their declared representation. This does not promise
identical external schedules or prove physical browser effects. Certificates can
strengthen these judgments through Stage 13 without introducing a parallel runtime.

*** Stage 8 -- Browser semantics and physical effect enforcement

Use a pure transition core over adapter state and normalized events, with explicit
abstract effects and a narrow physical shell. Qualify the actual HTMX lifecycle
and browser behavior used by that shell. No invented or merely convenient hook
can stand in for the real request, swap, settle, or deferred mutation boundary.

Deliver:

- owner-relative admission for execution-private and shared resource events;
- fresh resource instances, effect identities/phases, retirement, and cleanup;
- bounded active/queued acquisition slots plus separate metadata and physical
  remnant limits;
- typed provisional projections, rejection, settlement, supersession, and unknown
  outcome handling;
- actual effect permission checks immediately before physical mutation;
- completion checks before recording authoritative adoption and debt discharge;
- authoritative presence/absence and partial-installation repair;
- continuity independent of domain truth, including local options and scroll;
- stale-plan compatibility and recovery without retaining revoked effect rights.

Acceptance exercises deferred mutation after resource replacement, duplicate
completion, failed abort, late rollback, stale plan, partial installation, and
continuity failure in real Chromium as well as the portable model. A callback
admitted earlier cannot carry a cached Boolean permission into a later resource.

A Create projection need not invent an authoritative basis for a nonexistent
entity. Its intent and enclosing known facts follow the operation contract.
Current-state supersession may repair presentation while command attribution
remains unknown. Retiring the browser execution cannot silently erase that
separate command obligation.
