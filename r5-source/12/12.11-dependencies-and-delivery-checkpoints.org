** Dependency structure and delivery checkpoints

The eighteen stages provide *contract navigation and normal delivery priority*,
not a strict serial project plan, implementation-progress percentage or evidence
that every earlier stage has passed all of its strongest exit gates. One admitted
application can be built and evaluated in an explicit supported scope while
larger proof, product or multi-node work proceeds separately. Every result must
retain the original subject, the selected claim, actual positive behavior,
missing mandatory duties and exact evidentiary strength. Part XI independently
owns release and completion decisions; this section only maps their inputs.

*** Classify dependency edges before using the stage table

The roadmap distinguishes five different relationships:

- *Semantic prerequisite:* a consumer cannot legitimately interpret or use a
  subject without the earlier exact semantic contract and required facts.
- *Implementation priority:* completing this construction first usually
  reduces rework, although independent exploration may begin sooner.
- *Exploratory feedback:* bounded prototype or finite/model/browser test
  may reveal a missing source contract; its result does not gain the stronger
  qualification of a finished downstream system.
- *Optional early hardening:* cheap identity, proof, instrumentation, mutation,
  lifecycle or release safety mechanisms may close an immediate real defect.
  Their early presence does not certify all later stage responsibilities.
- *Completion dependency:* the requested *named* milestone/release profile
  cannot pass until every independently required gate has actual applicable
  evidence or an explicitly allowed and declared premise.

A dependency in a checker source, proof DAG, generated route registry or
document stage list is not automatically evidence that the *original*
required capability, writer, initializer, policy predicate or physical path
inventory is complete. Independently establish the relevant denominator
before declaring source-closed coverage. The checked subset and complete
requested application/product must remain distinguishable.

The delivery priority is: establish original public semantics, typed rules
and actual Biff/XTDB host; make one real, permitted Claim work through
admitted operation, Choreo, browser, guarded transaction and scoped
recovery; close the same application's source/physical path; expand the
real HumanHelp product and generic adverse coverage; compose proof
claims and independent mathematical obligations; then qualify
production-formal transfer and actual multi-host fault envelope.
A small external proof, two-host experiment, or source-derived
diagnostic can start as soon as its real prerequisites exist.
No advanced proof fixture repairs a missing enabled browser Claim
or bypassed writer, and no working Claim excuses an unproved
mandatory source/correspondence obligation.

This graph is not a demand to implement every capability of Stage 9 before
*publishing a first durable notice*, or every feature of Stage 8 before
*exploring multi-node command failover*. The publication half consumes
authoritative operation/transaction contracts; physical browser
adoption adds browser rights, user grant and actual installation.
Similarly a conditional seven-rule kernel theorem need not await
production HumanHelp Response, while S4 transfer to its installed
writer cannot be claimed without corresponding physical coverage.

A runnable Claim slice consumes the actual trusted route/FX and
protected transaction path before its first observed user-facing
effect, even if Stage 10's *general* closure and Stage 11's *complete*
release management are unfinished. An early narrow staging instance
needs its own valid installation and enforced safety scope; a
green in-process assembly is never evidence the tested/production
listener used the checked handler. The same distinction applies
to the actual emitted/served/loaded browser artifact, not just
a correctly shaped manifest or generated file name.

*** Avoid apparent construction and evidence cycles

The selected source, checked subject, physical plan, actually
installed executor and observed command are different objects.
Their relationships can be built in increments without allowing
a later report to retroactively manufacture an earlier right:

- A *startup* preflight seam exists before broad whole-app closure;
  its first served profile rejects the known missing inputs and
  unsafe early initializer/handler effects. Full source/writer/
  alternative-route coverage is a separately checked later claim.
- A *source-derived* operation and provisional browser plan may
  exist before all formal proofs; first actual user acceptance still
  requires current model authority, correct physical effects,
  honest error/unknown handling and independent positive evidence.
- An *initial* Live read and registration mechanism precedes
  mature durable routing. Multi-node late-interest fencing,
  publication replay and user-authorized acquisition may be
  explored early, but a prototype cannot claim full availability
  or remote automatic adoption from an advisory.
- A *first* diagnostic/finite counterexample can be checked
  before generated external-formal semantics; an illustrative
  fixture cannot be relabeled an executed generated-backend
  counterexample or a theorem about production.
- Source-proof/issuer admission and physically installed
  realization are linked by selected correspondence relations,
  not by matching digests. An incomplete actual writer inventory
  is an open premise even when the submitted proof DAG is closed.

When downstream exploration exposes a missing rule, actor distinction,
guard/frame, source obligation, dynamic recovery branch, interest
fence or incompatibility, repair the *original owning contract*.
Rebuild only the actually affected semantic, proof, plan, handler,
browser, routing and installation evidence dependency cone;
do not copy new policy into a separate prover, router or browser
machine to evade the source. A genuine unmodified abstract theorem
may be reused while its previous physical applicability becomes stale.

*** Useful integrated checkpoints and honest positive witnesses

A checkpoint names the *first independently useful integrated result*,
its allowed evidence and its still-open stronger obligations. It is
neither a schedule promise nor a permanent success status.

| Checkpoint | Required useful result and first admissible positive | What remains separate or cannot substitute |
|------------+-----------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------|
| Public semantic operation | Real model-owned Claim with actor, Request/RequestAssignment, current guard/effect/command and original capability ledger | No claim that a typed map or one-document example committed an atomic multi-record effect |
| Physically guarded command | Eligible Claim incorporates through selected actual XTDB writer, with competing/unauthorized negative and C42-specific historical unknown/replay | Pinned external read, local receipt or later matching card is not a universal transaction-internal-prestate/causality theorem |
| Actual browser Claim slice | Installed emitted artifact presents enabled control, reaches trusted Biff route, settles/recovers permitted result and survives reload | A browser fixture, HTTP 200 or optimistic card is not persisted authority; Claim is not the complete product |
| Live selected acquisition | Initial authorized board can be acquired; advisory and explicit one-use Refresh are separately exercised with actual scope/installation coverage | Lost notice, late interest, incomplete OOB, remote auto-adoption or missing router gaps remain tracked |
| Closed installed application profile | Original selected source/capability edges, preflight, handler, route, asset, effect and required recovery are qualified for the *actual served* scope | Cannot infer all source policies, external writers or a larger requested product denominator from green assembly |
| Real HumanHelp product | All Part VIII independent capabilities and fourteen positive/adverse product gates, including six lifecycle actions, real Reassign and selected Response | Claim-only pilot, disabled button, synthetic session or general formal theorem cannot replace user acceptance |
| Compositional Claim certificate | Same original model/relationship/operation/Choreo/plan and real pilot bound by accepted source/issuer obligations with exact assumptions | A copyable report, closed caller DAG or narrowed operation catalog cannot assert full source or physical closure |
| Independent kernel theorem | Actual checked S0/S1 theorem for the stable selected calculus with reproducible proof, premises and axioms | Finite truth table, CLI return, S2 production checker and S3/S4 typed/physical correspondence are different subjects |
| Qualified formal transfer | Selected S2–S4 obligations and concrete-to-abstract execution relation are proved or accurately qualified as the named profile demands | Admission theorem alone cannot establish XTDB atomicity, browser execution or unconditional progress |
| Production multi-node | Actual independently failing hosts, original command/history and durable notice recovery, fence/replay, protocol overlap and measured fault/load envelope | Two host labels, shared database alone, guaranteed receipt of every SSE event or user-independent visible convergence |

Each checkpoint has paired eligible/adversarial tests at its *first
legitimately enforcing* boundary. A defect prevented only after an
unauthorized write or browser disclosure cannot count as
before-effect prevention. A reject-everything implementation
does not earn credit for its negative results without a usable
valid operation. A resource, backend or test that has not been
physically exercised remains an explicit limitation, not a
counterfactual pass.

A complete real Claim path includes compatible replay and
=:commit-unknown= after possible transmission, changed current
Membership, genuine competing assignment, and ordinary creator-
local/remote-board distinctions. A new browser execution,
reconnect or host switch does not authorize the old one-shot
effect or erase command-specific resolution. Strong physical
readback, proof, browser installation and model authority
each report their own observations and assumptions.

*** Completion mapping and honest progress reporting

Part XI owns the original *four independent completion milestones*,
their subject-specific qualification and the *four release
decisions* (candidate, installation, activation, completion).
The release decisions do not themselves determine which
milestone has been achieved; stage labels do not establish
either result. The following is a dependency map, not a
second completion policy or an ordered percentage scale.

| Independent milestone | Evidence path required for its chosen profile | Non-substitutable gap |
|-----------------------+-----------------------------------------------+-----------------------|
| [[#milestone-operational-application][Operational application]] | Real installed 1, 2, selected 4–12, plus applicable adverse/runtime evidence from 14–15 and all independent Part VIII gates | Missing Response, real Reassign, protected writer, installed browser, explicit Refresh or eligible user positive |
| [[#milestone-compositional-certification][Compositional certification]] | Selected 1–3 and 13 with original source/operation/invariant coverage, genuine Claim path, current issuer and applicable 14–15 checks | Closed reduced proof DAG, copied certificate, toy Request/assignment or unqualified installed bridge |
| [[#milestone-external-formal-verification][External formal verification]] | 16–17 for *the requested S0–S4 frontier*, actual theorem/tool identity, conditional premises and corresponding source/production use | Finite samples, formal theorem for disconnected model or a claimed S4 without physical transfer |
| [[#milestone-multi-node-production][Production multi-node]] | 18 plus selected 4–11 authority/release and actual topology, failure, recovery, workload, security and browser evidence | Two successful node requests, non-replayable lost advisory, unqualified interest filters or unconditional liveness |

Do not infer that the *operational* milestone has to wait for
every S0–S4 theorem or the final multi-node topology, or that a
small real two-host fault experiment can be counted as the
*production multi-node* milestone. A release explicitly requesting
those stronger claims must meet its independently predeclared
requirements. A claim-specific conditional proof may be genuinely
valuable without satisfying an independent product-completeness
milestone.

For every progress measure, freeze the original required numerator/
denominator subjects *before* checking the currently exposed
application. Report eligible product capabilities, source/assembly
closure, semantic judgments, physical installed-path enforcement,
formal transfer, mutation first-gate quality, fault recovery,
asset/host compatibility and executed tests as distinct dimensions.
Distinguish =:passed=, =:failed=, =:incomplete=, =:unavailable=,
=:unsupported=, =:skipped=, =:stale= and =:missing= at the actual
Part XI gate boundary. Do not infer that uninspected == empty or
that unavailable == inapplicable. A stricter requested profile
may not be silently replaced after a failing check by a new
smaller one.

A checkpoint report binds its source, selected claim, known
environment, admitted model/plan, actual installed host/writer,
served browser artifact, issued/observed evidence and remaining
premises. An original product owner can approve a *new*
requirement subject, with old and new counts preserved; a
generated registry cannot lower the old denominator itself.
A previously valid abstract theorem can survive an unrelated
CSS change while the deployed asset/generation still needs
independent physical compatibility checking. Conversely, a
changed protected writer can invalidate physical applicability
without retroactively disproving the unchanged theorem.

The next useful work item is the missing *highest-consequence
currently required boundary* for the chosen claim, not
automatically the next stage number or the easiest proof/report
to generate. Exploratory discoveries may increase the list
of named open work without reversing genuine completed
applicable evidence. Progress remains net improvement
against the stable obligations, not a forecast based on
revisions, tests or checked-file counts.
