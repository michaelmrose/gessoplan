** Whole-application security and construction invariant

Preflight establishes the stated structural relationships and selected assurance
conditions. It does not confer a principal, disclose a protected result, authorize
a transaction, or establish that a mutable world still satisfies a prior guard.
Those premises are checked at the trusted boundary that owns them.

 
*** Trusted authority is an explicit boundary, not metadata

The canonical construction may establish that an operation has one admitted
name, typed arguments, generated plan, physical transport and trusted server
executor. It must not replace the authenticated principal or current model
authorization with a browser argument, role label, stored card, copied proof
report, or prior successful preflight. The server resolves every protected
operation through its own installed and current trusted registry.

| Source of information | Authority it may establish |
|-----------------------+----------------------------|
| Rendered control, plan digest, basis, command metadata or browser certificate | A request for a particular semantic action or a participant-relative observation; **no** principal authentication, permission, transaction or disclosure authority. |
| Trusted request ingress | Valid method/route/envelope, decoded subject, authenticated principal/tenant and correct issued executor binding; **not** indefinite business permission. |
| Public authoritative model operation and guarded transaction | Current domain guard, permissible effect and authoritative result under the selected XTDB/backend premises. |
| Durable command record and resolver | Command-specific historical incorporation or an explicitly unresolved/nondisclosable state under the *original* binding and a current trusted disclosure predicate; **not** permission to issue a new mutation. |
| Application assembly, artifact receipt or host installation evidence | Structural/build/serving correspondence at its qualified subject and generation; **not** an operation or data-access capability. |
| Formal proof, finite-model report, checker diagnosis or runtime observation | The exact proved, bounded, observed, open or assumed proposition with its scope; **no** automatically conferred authority beyond the independent trusted execution boundary. |

Untrusted values may guide selection only after they are decoded, normalized,
validated and matched to the trusted route/operation/subject contract.
Inconsistent Request IDs in path and command arguments, a forged principal,
a wrong tenant, an invalidly replayed command ID, or a purported assembly
certificate supplied by the client cannot win a conflict with trusted
context. Protected result disclosure, including history of a command that
has already incorporated, is evaluated under its own current authorization
policy, not the policy used when the command was originally issued.

The browser can forge local metadata, operation names, command bytes, certificates,
and assembly IDs. Trusted ingress and model operations remain secure without
assuming that the browser follows the admitted implementation. Conversely, server
construction controls accidental misuse by trusted host code; a hostile JVM with
reflection or arbitrary bytecode replacement is outside that claim unless an
additional isolation mechanism is explicitly supplied and qualified.

 
*** Threat model and effect containment

Gesso's structural contracts are designed to prevent accidental and
misconfigured bypass in cooperating application/server code and to
remain safe against a malicious or stale browser request. They do not
by themselves sandbox arbitrary trusted application code, a compromised
JVM, reflection, hostile classloading, administrator-level access to the
XTDB process, or an independent writer with unrestricted database
credentials. Claims covering such adversaries require separately
qualified process isolation, physical write capability restriction,
deployment authentication or independent complete writer evidence.

The protected write domain and its allowed writer policy are named.
A route registry that lists every declared Choreo operation cannot
establish that no scheduler, initializer, migration, worker, raw XTDB
client or external writer can mutate the same protected state. A
host-supplied writer inventory is useful conditional evidence, but its
completeness and ability to constrain actual physical effects remain
separate propositions. Stronger all-writer invariants must be enforced
at the actual capability boundary or depend on explicitly justified
complete deployment/physical-writer premises. Hidden writers are
not excluded by matching operation IDs or a successful local proof.

The same principle governs model authorization: an application-supplied
callback that claims to enforce active Membership is not automatically
equivalent to a transaction-internal guard. Correctness of the declared
business rule, the admitted abstract transition, the real executable
guard, the physical XTDB prestate and commit, and every interfering
writer require separately applicable evidence. A finite example of
Claim safety or conditional induction can establish an important
abstract fact without proving all deployed transactions respect it.

A structural report, artifact receipt, process-local handle, proof certificate,
and runtime observation each has a defined role. None silently acquires the
others' authority. Production evidence is applied only to its actual subject,
scope, assumptions, and current applicability.

 
*** No credential promotion across lifecycle, evidence or time

Each issued effect right has a checked owner, operation, principal scope,
source generation, physical target, permitted use and retirement contract.
Source/assembly closure cannot mint that right merely by returning a
copyable report. An issued request-local callable cannot be copied as a
portable credential or reused by another invocation without its own
admission. A browser-issued one-Refresh permission is deliberately
distinct from the existence of an advisory or the ability to connect
to an SSE stream; a consumed grant cannot be revived by replay.

Construction, installation, actual network serving, current invocation
authorization, successful authoritative incorporation, permitted
readback, physical DOM installation and subsequent participant
continuity remain independent statements. When a Claim commits but
its response is lost, its incorporation does not disappear: the
participant remains =:unknown= until command-specific evidence
authorizes a different settlement. When the card is updated and a
later Reassign changes current state, that observation does not
rewrite historical incorporation. A failed response renderer cannot
turn a prior committed mutation into rejection; a later observer
cannot serve as a retroactive pre-effect guard.

Generation changes preserve the original binding of already issued
commands, qualified old-client asset/plan compatibility and declared
recovery work. They do not preserve an obsolete principal's right to
initiate new protected effects. An old callback cannot silently
borrow a new handler/provider or a retired browser target. An
initializer that opened a listener or writer before preflight
cannot be excused by placing the checker first in a later Biff
=:start= ordering. New protected traffic is admitted only
through the selected, actually adopted generation and its
applicable physical/effect/security gates.

Single-node and distributed realizations are constrained by the
same security and semantic invariants. Multi-node routing, interest
advertisement, failover, replay and invalidation duplication require
additional consistency, isolation and liveness premises; a
router's locally missing interest is not authority to omit
a remote participant's permissible recovery. Advisory Live
delivery alone never authorizes a protected data read, and
missing messages never prove the participant is up to date.

The whole-application invariant is:

#+begin_quote
One source-owned definition yields an admitted semantic and physical realization
under an explicit assurance policy. Every required operation, effect, output,
observation, and recovery edge has a compatible current binding, and the actual
installed paths enforce the required checks before their protected actions.
Construction, artifact, installation, invocation, and physical-completion facts
remain distinct. Missing structural edges reject rather than silently weaken
meaning; opaque application logic retains explicit contracts and premises.
Admitted assembly instances remain coherent through build, serving, asynchronous
work, reload, and retirement. Declared closure, production enforcement, and
independent product completeness are separately evidenced, and authority remains
with the trusted runtime/model boundaries that own the relevant current facts.
#+end_quote

 
*** Conditions for interpreting the invariant

The quotation is a **normative end-state property**, not a certificate
that a particular Gesso commit or arbitrary Clojure application
currently satisfies it. Its "every required" quantification applies
to the *truthfully covered* operation, effect, writer, product and
deployment scope under the selected assurance policy. It cannot
prove unknown writers absent or make a smaller operation catalog
satisfy broader HumanHelp capabilities. To assert it of an admitted application
generation =A= and its specified protected effects, the evidence
must account for all the following conditions:

1. The source-owned catalog and its independently selected
   positive application requirements are identified; required
   operations, rules and recovery cases are nonvacuously covered.
   Unknown or opaque business rules remain declared premises,
   not implicitly absent.
2. Semantic operations, browser controls/plan, exact generated
   artifacts, actual routes, trusted model calls, physical
   providers, protected writers, final output, observation and
   recovery all have appropriately checked bindings. A
   structurally known missing edge prevents admission, rather
   than downgrading a managed operation to anonymous HTMX.
3. The installed startup, actual handler, guarded transaction
   path and relevant response/asset serving mechanisms enforce
   those checks in the qualified deployment. Local system-map
   equality and source-relative diagnostics do not establish
   physical serving or writer completeness.
4. Each attempted effect is checked against *current* trusted
   authentication, authorization, model guards, command
   binding and transaction assumptions at its legitimate
   authoritative boundary. The invariant must continue to
   hold under all covered relevant concurrent writers, not
   only the operation that the user clicked.
5. Protected output, prior-command disclosure, Live interest,
   one-Refresh acquisition, physical adoption and old
   command resolution respect their own permissions,
   progression and lifetime, including ambiguous delivery,
   postcommit failures and generation turnover.
6. Relevant changed sources, providers, checkers, plans,
   backend assumptions or deployed wiring invalidate
   dependent evidence. Bounded finite checking,
   conditional induction, host observations, compiler
   premises, actual backend behavior and production
   deployment are never collapsed into one green claim.
7. Independent real user acceptance demonstrates required
   positive Create/Claim/Unclaim/Cancel/complete/Reassign
   and advisory/Refresh behaviors as applicable; a
   structurally closed, successfully rejecting but
   functionally empty application does not qualify.

A qualified invariant is about the actions and physical
deployment scopes it actually covers. Fairness, continuous
connectivity, backend correctness, correct application
business rules, distributed recovery and hostile trusted
processes require separately named assumptions or proofs.
A finite model checker can prove an invariant for its
admitted finite model; the complete real application
guarantee additionally needs correct mappings and
coverage for all claimed physical effects.

*** Security and construction falsification histories

Tests select the original source-owned application,
intentionally change one structural or physical edge,
and observe its legitimate real boundary. Valid positive
cases remain independently required.

| History | Required result and security interpretation |
|---------+---------------------------------------------|
| An authorized helper uses the real Claim control, and the current model incorporates the guarded transaction with permitted readback. | Structural, trusted-effect, participant and physical adoption evidence remain separate; valid Claim must stay usable. |
| The browser forges =:principal= or Membership role in a Claim payload. | Trusted ingress/model ignore that authority claim and enforce current authenticated principal and tenant. |
| A Request R1 Claim envelope is sent to the installed R2 Claim route. | Reject contradictory normalized selectors before protected effect; do not choose the more convenient untrusted field. |
| The browser sends a copied ApplicationAssembly, valid artifact receipt or alleged proof certificate to skip server checks. | Reject authority promotion; actual installed server and current model requirements remain mandatory. |
| A command ID that previously incorporated is replayed with a different operation, arguments or principal. | Reject incompatible binding or withhold disclosure; do not authorize a new mutation or reveal a protected historical result. |
| A correctly bound old command result is requested by a principal lacking current disclosure permission. | Return the authorized nondisclosing disposition, not the old outcome or a fabricated rejection. |
| Claim's declared active-Membership rule passes a finite check but the issued XTDB callback omits the real current guard. | Keep the abstract result separate; fail/open physical model-enforcement correspondence and relevant product acceptance. |
| A worker using raw XTDB privileges alters RequestAssignment outside the registered route catalog. | Reject complete-writer/security claims absent an actual shared capability restriction or independently complete interference proof. |
| A current generated browser asset is checked locally but a different bundle is served to an old page. | Reject or qualify exact serving/generation correspondence; source receipt alone is insufficient. |
| Biff's preflight starts first in ordered =:start=, while a previous =:init= has already exposed protected work. | Fail the no-unchecked-exposure condition or require a real pre-activation fence. |
| A valid Claim commits, but its HTML response is malformed and never delivered. | Retain authoritative incorporation and participant =:unknown= until admissible command resolution; output rejection is not transaction rollback. |
| An SSE advisory arrives twice after a one-use Refresh grant was consumed. | Preserve advisory debt without granting unauthorized repeated acquisition or adoption. |
| A Live router fails while a new subscriber's interest was not yet known to its replacement. | Require qualified fence/replay/reacquisition coverage or report an open no-gap premise; a silent stream is not fresh knowledge. |
| A server-side diagnostic report is copied from generation A and submitted as generation B's admission proof. | Refuse report-as-capability; recheck original sources, installed bindings and applicable changed premises. |
| The managed application has no usable manager Reassign, although its reduced semantic graph and guards pass. | Structural success may stand; independent positive product completeness fails. |
| A runtime checker is unsupported or times out while all route/asset declarations look correct. | Retain unsupported/inconclusive mandatory obligations under the selected policy; no automatic downgrade or universal certificate. |

These histories are requirements for future qualified
implementations and tests, not assertions of a freshly
completed Gesso test suite. Their scope and independent
acceptance must remain visible in the application's
semantic audit and explanation surface.

*** Part VII completion is not automatic acceptance

Finishing the fifteen sectional R5.1 source reviews
establishes only editorial progress. Whole-Part-VII
reconciliation must separately inspect internal
duplication and contradictions, cross-part interfaces,
the complete R4 preservation inventory, and consistency
of the revised final invariant with its construction,
browser, model and deployment premises.

Independent reviewed-candidate acceptance then evaluates
the resulting published part and its original sources.
Neither step grants whole-application certification to
Gesso or HumanHelp. Parts VIII–XII and full-plan review
retain their separate outstanding requirements.
