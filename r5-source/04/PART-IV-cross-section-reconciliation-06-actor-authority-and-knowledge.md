**Status:** sixth bounded reconciliation, not whole-Part-IV candidate

## Cross-part finding

Accepted Part II distinguishes principal, role, actor, host, authority,
semantic command, resource instance and local knowledge. Part III
requires a local branch to possess justified role-specific evidence;
another actor knowing a command ID or holding a newer observation
does not satisfy that condition. Accepted HumanHelp Part VIII selects
per-actor board adoption and explicit one-Refresh permission.

The same authenticated principal can use two tabs with different
board frontiers, pending advisories, grants and physical owners.
A durable command record survives an originating actor and may be
recovered by an authorized successor, but the successful command
does not mean that successor received an earlier role message or
owns the earlier actor's DOM. A protected subscription also cannot
become an unlimited disclosure right after Membership or Organization
permissions change.

## Normative resolution

- 04.06 distinguishes durable command identity from actor-local
  branch knowledge and newly issued capabilities after replacement.
- 04.08 requires a currently authorized, actor-correlated read or
  communication before the successor knows a historical result;
  retired callbacks and installation rights are not transferred.
- 04.14 binds Live interests to actor, principal, authorized scope
  and lifetime, with current trusted reauthorization or revocation.
- 04.16 retains separate same-principal tab cohorts, Refresh grants
  and pending debt; logout/context switch retires incompatible rights.
- 04.17 includes paired tests for two-tab isolation, recovered role
  knowledge, and permission changes after Live subscription admission.
- The Interleaved Refresh and newer local Request evidence heading
  is restored as a distinct Org heading rather than part of a
  preceding prose sentence.

## Paired cases

| History | Justified conclusion | Unsupported promotion |

| Alice tab A Refreshes and installs P20; Alice tab B remains at P17. | Tab A has qualified P20 adoption, B retains separate debt and older display. | Tab B inherits installed P20 or A's user grant merely because principal/session match. |
| E1 submitted C42, then retired; E2 is authorized to ask for C42's result. | E2 may learn via a new admitted recovery path and continue its declared branch. | E2 claims historical receipt of E1's message or E1's DOM rights. |
| One SSE transport carries multiple actor-bound interests. | Each interested actor separately receives qualified advisory knowledge. | Shared transport supplies shared authoritative observation or installation. |
| Membership permission disappears after interest registration. | Later protected disclosures use recheck, expiry or revocation contract. | Earlier interest registration permanently authorizes private updates. |
| Alice signs out and Bob becomes active before a delayed response arrives. | Incompatible owner effects are retired; new server disclosures are authorized independently. | Alice's old private content installs in Bob's current board. |
