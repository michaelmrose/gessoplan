*** Progression flows through propagation

When an authoritative change creates a required invalidation, the propagation
path preserves enough information for the resulting reread to include that
change. The sequence is:

1. Qualified outcome evidence establishes the authoritative change.
2. The durable change/publication record carries an admitted progression
   requirement and affected scope information.
3. Routing delivers an advisory to a relevant participant under its transport
   and recovery contract.
4. The participant's acquisition requests a scope and progression sufficient
   to cover the relevant obligation.
5. The acquisition boundary admits only evidence that actually establishes the
   promised coverage and visibility.

The advisory remains advisory: it can establish that an acquisition is needed
without supplying the new authoritative contents. Preserving progression does
not require every stage to repeat the complete token; a protected reference to
the requirement is sufficient when it can be resolved under the contract.

A tokenless advisory needs an alternative qualified fence or acquisition rule
that prevents a satisfying reread from preceding the triggering change. Merely
reading something newer than the participant's old display may still miss that
change. If the transport cannot supply such a rule, the weaker guarantee must
be explicit.

*** Initial acquisition and subscription races

Initial content for a managed projection participates in the same scope,
authority, progression, and installation contract as refresh content. Being
server-rendered does not exempt it. The owner/resource instance and any initial
basis claim must be established through the admitted bootstrap path.

The relationship between initial read and subscription must avoid an uncovered
interval when the application promises eventual awareness of changes. Suitable
strategies include an admitted read/subscription fence, replay from a durable
cursor, buffered changes across registration, or another qualified recovery
mechanism. Each strategy states how it handles reconnect and owner replacement.

A periodic authoritative refresh may provide a weaker eventual-awareness
strategy under explicit scheduling and availability assumptions. It does not
prove delivery of every invalidation. A router's successful local subscription
does not by itself prove that no relevant upstream change was lost before its
interest became visible.

*** Observed basis is not authority to commit

A browser-supplied observed basis can be useful as an untrusted input to
freshness handling. It does not prove that the browser acquired or adopted the
corresponding data, and it does not establish current authorization. Stronger
claims require admitted evidence bound to the relevant observation and actor.

The server must also bound and validate requested consistency behavior. A
malformed, foreign-incarnation, or unattainable token must not force unbounded
waiting or silently downgrade to an unconstrained read.

Advancement alone does not invalidate a command. The model checks its actual
business preconditions. When the operation explicitly requires an expected
domain version, the server enforces that condition as part of command meaning;
when it does not, unrelated database advancement is not a reason to reject.

Dependency upgrades must not silently change token interpretation, read
visibility, temporal defaults, or command/result compatibility. The assembly
and qualification artifacts bind the relevant provider contract, and persisted
requirements need an explicit compatibility or migration policy.
