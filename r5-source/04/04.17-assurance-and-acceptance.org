** Assurance obligations and acceptance cases

Part IV's assurance is a chain of bounded claims. The semantic transition must
express the intended rule; construction must bind it to the actual inputs and
implementation; the complete submission must realize the protected effect;
qualified backend behavior must justify the outcome; propagation and acquisition
must preserve scope and progression; installation must justify adoption.

A report uses Part I's separate claim dimensions: proposition, subject,
mechanism, premises, coverage, applicability, and status. It distinguishes
derivation or construction checking, runtime enforcement, testing, and explicit
assertion. Named environmental premises qualify a claim; conditionality is not
an additional proof mechanism. These descriptions are not interchangeable
assurance grades. A strong proof of an abstract fragment cannot conceal an
unexamined physical execution boundary. Status belongs to a precisely stated
proposition and subject: an established abstract invariant, a source-derived
conditional construction report, a trusted runtime enforcement requirement, a
bounded finite search and an observed physical outcome are different judgments.
Their agreement can support a stronger composed claim only through the declared
composition and correspondence rules. No copied report, familiar status tag or
passing test grants a certificate or application admission by itself.

*** Required invariant families

The supported construction and runtime must establish, or explicitly qualify,
at least the following property families:

1. *Binding fidelity*: operation, command, canonical arguments, principal,
   authority, target, and realization cannot be silently substituted between
   admission, submission, outcome, and reconciliation.
2. *Authoritative safety*: each incorporated transition satisfies its protected
   business conditions and declared frame under the backend/writer premises.
3. *Command consistency*: compatible repetition does not incorporate again;
   incompatible reuse cannot overwrite the old binding; terminal disposition is
   reported only with evidence of its declared scope.
4. *Truthful uncertainty*: absence of outcome evidence, timeout, and local
   retirement do not manufacture non-incorporation.
5. *Recoverable required work*: committed operations retain the declared
   command/result and propagation or external-work evidence across promised
   failure modes.
6. *Scoped observation*: acquired data supports only its admitted scope,
   coverage, temporal interpretation, visibility context, and progression.
7. *Justified adoption*: only current permitted installation/adoption paths
   advance adopted knowledge and discharge adoption debt.
8. *Evidence fidelity*: a classification, certificate, or passing test is not
   promoted into a stronger proposition than it establishes.

Each family needs a named source-owned operation or application subject, its
claimed boundary and the actual premises on which its status depends. An
uninspected writer inventory is unknown, not empty; a zero count of detected
violations is only about the inspected population. A missing required operation
is not a proof that the remaining operations preserve every invariant. Likewise,
an invariant true only because no valid transition is enabled is insufficient
when the application promises a usable operation. Independently declared
capability and positive reachability obligations prevent vacuous success.

Liveness claims are separate. They identify scheduling, delivery, backend,
provider, retention, authority, and human-participation assumptions. Safety must
not depend on pretending that those assumptions always produce timely success.

A structural mismatch is rejected at the owning admission boundary; a valid
construction with a modeled counterexample does not receive the claimed
invariant; an unestablished premise remains explicitly unestablished unless the
chosen policy permits a named conditional assertion. A heuristic suspicion about
otherwise admitted business policy is a review finding, not automatically a
proof failure. Assertions cannot substitute for a missing required declaration,
contradictory operation contract or fabricated physical evidence. The report
must preserve both unsupported and contradicted outcomes without silently
relabeling either as business rejection or environmental unavailability.

*** Executable conformance cases

The implementation must provide conformance cases for the supported mechanisms,
including negative cases, reachable positive controls, and relevant
interleavings. Each case names its original source and operation, the controlled
mutation or fault, expected checking boundary, affected proposition, oracle,
forbidden physical action, and permitted continuation. A positive case must pass
through the *same* boundary as its adverse variant; rejecting every input is
not conformance. The independent effect observer must establish when a
forbidden mutation, disclosure, installation or retry did or did not occur;
absence of a logged success message is not proof of absence. These cases
complement semantic derivations; passing them does not prove arbitrary backend
behavior.

| Area | Case | Required observation |
|------+-------+----------------------|
| Target binding | Command names R1; acquisition or submitted mutation targets R2. | Admission or execution rejects the mismatch; no claimed R1 transition is established from R2's state. |

| Relationship | Membership belongs to another principal or Organization. | New Claim is not authorized by that Membership. |

| Different-command concurrency | Two helpers claim the same unclaimed Request. | Only transitions allowed by protected current state incorporate. |
| Replay | Original Claim succeeded, response was lost, Request is now claimed. | Compatible permitted replay returns the recorded outcome without a second effect. |
| Incompatible reuse | Same ID, changed canonical arguments or authority binding. | No overwrite or new incorporation under the old binding; disclosure follows policy. |
| Terminal rejection | A pending attempt races with rejection closure. | The protocol cannot settle rejection while permitting that command to incorporate later. |
| Unknown outcome | Submission may have committed; local response is lost. | Unknown remains until admitted evidence resolves it. |
| Disclosure | A former caller no longer has permission to read a recorded result. | No unauthorized result disclosure and no invented domain rejection. |

| Acquisition failure | An empty-looking result actually represents failure, denial, or partial coverage. | No authoritative absence or completeness claim is created. |
| Adoption | Installation is allowed, then fails or targets a retired owner. | Acquire-and-adopt debt is not discharged by permission alone. |

| Subscription race | A relevant change occurs between initial read and effective subscription. | The declared fence, replay, or recovery mechanism covers the gap, or the weaker guarantee is explicit. |
| Retention | A stale retry arrives after result eviction. | The namespace/horizon/tombstone policy prevents an unjustified duplicate effect. |
| Dependency change | Backend or adapter behavior changes beneath a retained proof claim. | Relevant qualification is invalidated or re-established; old evidence is not silently reused. |

The semantic cases should be derived from the same canonical definitions used
for construction wherever practical. Independent adversarial and mutation cases
then challenge the correspondence boundary rather than merely restating the
implementation. Backend integration cases exercise the actual qualified adapter
and its relevant transaction/read paths.

*** Paired positive/adverse control families

The following pairs complement the preceding negative corpus. The valid side
must be accepted under its declared premises; the adverse side must be
rejected, remain unknown, or be qualified at the appropriate boundary.
These cases cannot all be replaced by a stub that returns =:unsupported=.

| Admitted positive control | Paired defect or fault | Independent evidence or classification |
|---------------------------+------------------------+----------------------------------------|
| A permitted HumanHelp Claim establishes one active primary RequestAssignment under current guards. | Competing Claim, stale Membership or omitted uniqueness/absence guard. | Execute the actual protected writer and independently inspect Request and assignment facts; reject unsafe incorporation. |
| A compatible C42 repeat resolves its historical result under current disclosure policy without another write. | Incompatible command binding, changed principal, or unauthorized result lookup. | Observe exact command-record binding and no extra incorporation or information leak. |
| A complete submitted domain transition and command-result footprint is present under the qualified backend. | A prepared checked fragment is altered, an extra writer is appended, or a result is copied from another invocation. | Compare actual submitted effect, bound return and scoped physical readback; do not infer causality from mere co-observation. |
| A command times out after possibly committing and is reconciled with authentic command-specific evidence. | Timeout is treated as durable rejection or produces a fresh unqualified command. | Preserve unknown until admitted settlement; observe absence of an unauthorized duplicate effect. |
| A valid payment intent is submitted after durable work registration and recovered with matching provider evidence. | Provider succeeds before local incorporation, response vanishes, or deduplication retention lapses. | Check qualified external receipts and recovery debt; no blind new-key retry or false atomicity. |
| A provider result is recorded under current local Order guards with separate, recoverable change-publication work. | Receipt incorporation succeeds but process dies before Live publication; a retry resends provider authorization or discards Order change debt. | Verify the original provider work is not duplicated and the qualifying Order reread/notification remains recoverable. |
| A required change is committed with retrievable source impact and its interested browser later acquires a fenced view. | No current local router knows the eventual interest when the commit happens; a merely healthy SSE stream is treated as proof no impact was missed. | Inject the registration/failover race; verify an authorized covering read or declared weaker contract without fabricating exact delivery. |
| A permitted full-board read has complete scope and admitted progression through its required frontier. | A newer card, partial page, wrong tenant/time or shape-valid witness is substituted. | Verify read coverage and provider relation independently; retain the unmet board obligation. |
| A managed initial projection has an admitted acquisition/subscription continuity fence. | The board changes after read but before interest registration or router failover. | Replay, reacquire or report the weaker awareness contract; a healthy SSE connection alone does not pass. |
| O1/P18 acquisition installs while later O2/P20 remains, under one authorized HumanHelp Refresh grant. | A stale callback erases O2 or automatic queued reads exceed the user's grant. | Observe current debt and actual authorized acquisitions; one in-flight limit does not prove consent. |
| A correlated permitted after-swap effect installs into the current owner and semantic adoption records it. | Allow-swap succeeds but DOM mutation fails, completes partially, or targets retired E1/T1. | Physical-installation oracle and owner/effect correlation determine adoption or unresolved repair. |
| Remote R stays advisory while Bob's local S is visibly confirmed under an excluding filter. | Newer S card evidence imports R, clears remote debt, or hides S until Bob resets search. | Two independent authenticated browsers verify visible result area, retained options and selective membership. |
| A valid opaque Clojure realization is admitted with its explicitly accepted correspondence premise. | The same opaque body is silently promoted to verified semantic completeness or used to repair missing structural input. | Keep the premise and inspected scope visible; reject structural defects independently. |
| A complete supported operation family includes eligible transitions and explicit disabled/rejected paths. | Reassign is deleted from both implementation and finite model, or all guards are false. | Independent required-capability inventory and reachability/witness coverage detect vacuity. |

Test fixtures must distinguish admitted evidence from illustrative maps. A
fixture that creates an advancement witness by changing a token field does not
test provider truth. A fixture that returns a successful predicate without
performing installation does not test adoption. A diagnostic resolver that
finds a retained record does not demonstrate a durable recovery driver.

A finite-state check reports the explored state/input universes, enabled
operations, examined interleavings, exhaustion or incompleteness and
proposition scope. Enumerating sample integers or principal IDs is not a
general theorem. Inductive preservation requires qualified initialization
and a step rule for every admitted operation under the declared assumptions;
neither finite success nor preservation alone proves physical execution
correspondence. Fault injection must distinguish an injected failure from
a malformed baseline and test the actual recovery or prohibition boundary.

*** Completion criteria for this part

An implementation satisfies this part for an admitted operation family when its
public interface, canonical semantics, construction, physical submission,
backend profile, outcome interpretation, command policy, propagation, and
acquisition/adoption paths form one inspectable contract. Supported mechanisms
must reject mismatches early and explain the exact unsatisfied obligation.

Unsupported proof cases do not disappear. They are either implemented through
another supported mechanism, admitted with explicit accepted assertions under
policy, or rejected with a usable explanation. Dynamic unavailability has an
explicit continuation. Success means the claimed semantic effect and evidence
were established, not merely that the application started without errors.

Completion is evaluated *per admitted operation family and assurance profile*.
A root application report identifies exact source/certificate identities and
versions, the enumerated operations and independent required capabilities, the
inspected physical route/writer/worker/browser boundaries, conditional premises,
uncovered or untrusted implementation bodies, admissible positive/negative cases,
and unresolved correspondence. Changes to backend behavior, trusted adapters,
installed handlers, asset bytes or relevant business policy invalidate only the
dependent claims under the declared dependency rules, but cannot retain a stale
root acceptance. One certified source fragment does not imply every deployed
entry point exercised it; genuine installation/adoption evidence must justify
that stronger conclusion.

An assertion cannot repair a missing operation binding, malformed schema,
unresolved capability, or contradictory effect declaration. It supplies a named
premise about otherwise well-formed behavior; structural admission obligations
still have to be discharged.
