*** Requirement satisfaction and safe combination
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: req-progression-combination
:END:

The executable semantic core defines the following relations for each supported
scope and progression contract. The notation does not prescribe API names.

- =satisfies(O, R)=: admitted observation O establishes R's scope, coverage, and
  consistency requirements.
- =entails(R2, R1)=: every admitted observation satisfying R2 also satisfies R1.
- =combine(R1, R2)=: a representation retaining both requirements, or an explicit
  unsupported result where the requested representation cannot do so.
- =fulfills(E, B)=: the admitted evidence collection E fulfills every obligation
  in bundle B at each obligation's declared completion milestone.

The default conservative combination is conjunction: retain both obligations.
A provider may offer a sound compact representative or join. A successful
single-observation combination must satisfy the law:

#+begin_example
satisfies(O, combine(R1, R2))
  implies satisfies(O, R1) and satisfies(O, R2)
#+end_example

When no one observation can satisfy both, a bundle may allow separate
acquisitions. Bundle fulfillment does not imply a common snapshot. It also does
not erase the distinction between acquiring evidence and adopting it.

Dropping R1 because R2 supersedes it requires established entailment, including
compatible scope and coverage. A later-looking token for another query is not
such evidence. Unknown comparison does not authorize dropping either obligation.

Normalization preserves semantic meaning and is deterministic where stable
identity depends on it, under the declared semantic/provider versions. If the
implementation coalesces duplicates, reorders, or regroups requirements, it must
establish the corresponding idempotence, commutativity, or associativity of
obligation meaning. These need not be claims of byte-identical representation.

A stronger combined requirement may preserve safety while making acquisition
impossible. A liveness claim must therefore establish that the strengthened
requirement is satisfiable by the admitted read path under its declared progress
premises. Soundness is not permission to promise eventual acquisition of an
impossible join.

*** Bounded representation and backpressure

One in-flight request does not imply bounded retained debt. A coordinator may
accumulate incomparable scopes or requirements while that request is pending.
Its bounds must distinguish request concurrency, obligation count, representation
size, and any finite abstraction used in model checking.

For a finite scope domain with sound subsumption, one representative per scope
may suffice. Otherwise the implementation may retain bundles, partition work,
apply backpressure, request an authorized broader acquisition, or report an
unsupported configuration. It must not silently discard obligations to make a
counter stay below a limit.

Broader acquisition requires permission and a coverage contract. It does not
automatically authorize broader visible adoption. A user who has not chosen to
refresh board membership must not acquire a new visible board merely because
the coordinator compacted its metadata.

Resource retirement may legitimately end obligations scoped solely to that
resource, under an explicit retirement policy. It must not erase durable
command uncertainty or work owned elsewhere. The reason for retiring an
obligation is part of its lifecycle semantics, not a claim that it was satisfied.

*** Mixed observations do not imply an aggregate snapshot

A board may retain membership justified at P17 while one Request card is
updated through a later correlated operation result at P19. That can be a valid
application state under its adoption policy. It is not a complete board snapshot
at P19.

The representation must preserve enough provenance to support only the claims
actually established. It need not use a universal persisted fact graph, one
mutable object per fact, or a per-entity integer revision. It does need to
distinguish the scope of the later card observation from the older board
membership observation.

Replacing an adopted projection requires its declared supersession or
reconciliation relation. An incomparable observation may remain useful evidence
without being eligible to replace the current display. Equivalent bases also
do not make different projection schemas, principals, or query meanings
interchangeable.
